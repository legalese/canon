# SOURCE-LICENSE — Penal Law 5737-1977 (Israel)

**Status: OPEN — not determined.** This file records the questions, not answers. Do not read
the absence of a restriction here as a grant.

Like `il/ofek-hadash-2008`, this row was not read from an official publisher, so it carries two
questions where a statute row normally carries one.

## Question 1 — the statute itself

The instrument is an Act of the Knesset, published in *Reshumot* (Sefer HaChukim 864 p. 226 and
the 155 amendment issues since). Israel's Copyright Law 5768-2007, s. 6, is generally read as
placing statutes, regulations, Knesset records and court decisions outside copyright. **That
reading has not been checked against the section text for this row** — do that before relying on
it, and record the result here.

## Question 2 — the consolidation we actually read

The deposit is the Open Book of Laws page on Hebrew Wikisource, revision 3023424 of 2026-07-07
(`revision.json`). Wikisource contributions are published under **CC BY-SA 3.0** (and GFDL). Two
things follow, neither yet resolved:

- The *consolidation* — the merging of 155 amendments into one running text, the bracketed
  amendment annotations on every section, the comparison tables — is the volunteers' and the
  bot's work, not the Knesset's. Whatever s. 6 says about the statute, it says nothing about this
  layer. If that layer is copyrightable, an encoding derived from it inherits CC BY-SA's
  attribution and share-alike terms.
- The Open Book is **not citable as law**. It is a secondary text of unknown accuracy. The
  official trail is fully present in the same deposit — 264 `fs.knesset.gov.il` PDF links, one
  per enactment and amendment, fetchable without geo-restriction — and any section that matters
  to an encoding should be read back against its Knesset PDF before a golden is blessed on it.
