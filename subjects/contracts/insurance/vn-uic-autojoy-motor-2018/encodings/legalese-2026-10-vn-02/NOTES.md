**The three layers.**
Is it covered: `Part 1 — the reasons there is no cover for` lists every reason (definition 5, the period, the end of the contract, the premium, a transfer, the one-year claim period, Article 10.1, each exclusion not lifted); `Part 2 — the answer to` does the same for Part 2.
How much: `Art 13.4 — the settlement of` returns every step of Art 13.4 as a field of `A settlement` (cost, depreciation, insured proportion, sanction rate, deductible, wreck, Article 8 share, the Art 12.1 cap, Art 10.2 costs, Art 16.2 set-off), so an answer reads back line by line.
Duties and deadlines: regulative rules for Art 3.2, 4.2.3, 4.2.4, 4.2.7, 5.2.4, 5.2.4.1 and 5.2.6.3, exercised with `#TRACE`; time-bars (Art 9) as functions returning dates.

**What the encoding does not answer, by name** (a `REFUSE` whose message is its name):

- a kind of vehicle definition 5 names on neither side (its list ends in "....");
- any claim where an exclusion whose facts hold is lifted by a supplementary clause the policy takes: the document contains no such clause, and Article 1 ranks them above these Rules;
- the rate UIC chooses under Art 15.1.1.4 or 15.1.3, and the premium that "should have been paid" under Art 15.1.5, when the case does not supply them;
- the day the claim file counts as complete when the authority never concludes (Art 4.2.3 gives UIC no time after its 90 days);
- Part 2 Art 4.2(b) for a person with no workplace;
- a Part 2 accident abroad under an agreement whose terms are not in the document;
- everything in Part 3.

**Inputs, never defaults.** Everything the document leaves to the contract or schedule (sum insured, market value, period, premium and instalments, deductible amount, supplementary clauses taken, Part 2 sum per person and number of persons) is a field of `A policy` with no default; every valuation (market value at a loss, repair and replacement costs, the technical assessment's percentage, the wreck's value, Part 2's payout-table percentage) is a field of the assessment or the accident.
The Ministry of Finance's payout table (Decision 05/TC-BH of 1993) that Part 2 relies on is not in the sources; the percentage it gives an injury is an input.

**Assumption A1 (scope of a "contract").** The encoding answers what these Rules say.
A contract's own amending or supplementary clauses rank above the Rules (Art 1) and are not modelled, except that a supplementary clause lifting an exclusion is recognised and answered by the refusal above (fork F42).
