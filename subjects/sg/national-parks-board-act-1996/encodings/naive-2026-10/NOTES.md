## Why this Act, and why scoped

**11 of the 527 Singapore Acts** deposited here cite it. It is an institutional Act
that sets up a statutory board, so most of it is lists of functions and powers and the
transfer of staff and property. This row takes only the provisions that decide
something: execution under seal (s 4(2)), delegation and subdelegation (s 10),
protection from personal liability (s 11), the financial year (s 19), the symbol
offence (s 29), the exclusion of liability for paid information (s 30), where
moneys go (s 31), the steps that need the Minister's approval (s 32, Second Schedule
paras 15 and 16), membership, disqualification, quorum and voting (First Schedule
paras 1, 9, 11 and 13), and the pension apportionment for staff transferred in 1996
(Third Schedule para 3). Functions (s 6), most powers, Ministerial directions, staff,
grants, investment, estimates, and the 1996 and 2019 (AVA) transfers are not
encoded.

## What the Act turns out to say

### 1. The Board itself has less protection for free information than for paid information

s 30 protects the Board **and** its employees against loss caused by errors in
information, but only where the information is "supplied to the public on payment
of a prescribed fee", and only good faith in the ordinary course of duties is
needed. Reasonable care is not required. s 11, the general protection, names
members, the Chief Executive, employees, delegates and others, but not the Board
itself, and it requires good faith **and** reasonable care. So free information gets
no s 30 protection. That last point is an inference from what the two sections name
and leave out. Asserted.

### 2. Delegation reaches contractors, but never the power to borrow

s 10(1)(e) lets the Board delegate any of its functions, duties or powers "under
this Act or any other Act" to "a person engaged as a contractor by the Board", or to
a wholly-owned subsidiary. s 10(3) keeps back only the power to borrow, the power to
lend or subscribe to shares, and anything declared non-delegable. Asserted.

### 3. Subdelegation runs one level, and follows the delegate's side of the line

s 10(2): a member, the Chief Executive, an employee or a committee may be authorised
to subdelegate to another member or a qualified Board employee. A subsidiary or
contractor may be authorised to subdelegate only to "an appropriately qualified
employee of that delegate". s 10(4): a subdelegate cannot delegate again. A
contractor cannot hand a power to a Board employee; the Chief Executive cannot hand
one to an unqualified employee. Asserted.

### 4. Composition sums go to the Consolidated Fund, everything else to the Board

s 31: all moneys recovered or sums collected under this Act or any other Act the
Board administers go into the Board's general funds, "other than composition sums",
which go into the Consolidated Fund. Asserted.

### 5. A 6-month sentence disqualifies a member for life unless pardoned

First Schedule para 11(b): a person sentenced to imprisonment "for a term of at least
6 months" who "has not received a free pardon" must not be appointed or continue as a
member. No time limit is stated. Para 9: missing 3 consecutive meetings without leave
vacates the office. A mental disorder disqualifies only together with incapacity to
manage one's affairs. Asserted.

### 6. Small numbers that govern the Board

The Board is a Chairperson plus 6 to 10 other members (para 1); 5 is a quorum
(para 13(2)); decisions are by simple majority of those present and voting, with a
casting vote for whoever presides (para 13(3)); 4 members can force a meeting
within 7 days (para 13(6)). The Minister may revoke any appointment "without giving
any reason" (para 5, not encoded). Asserted, except para 5.

### 7. Ministerial approval is needed for loans, joint ventures, companies and regulations, not for investing or fees

Second Schedule paras 15 and 16 and s 32(1) require the Minister's approval. Investment
under s 17 (the standard investment power of statutory bodies) and charging fees
(para 19) carry no approval requirement in this Act. Asserted.

### 8. Other rules

The symbol offence (s 29) needs use without permission of an identical or confusingly
similar symbol; maximum $10,000, 6 months or both. A sealed document needs two
authorised members' signatures, or one and the Chief Executive's (s 4(2)). The
financial year runs 1 April to 31 March (s 19). Asserted.

## What would need doing before this is worth anything

- Third Schedule para 3's wording ("such portion ... as the same bears to the
  proportion which ...") is tangled. It is read here, as an **inference**, as the
  benefit times Government emoluments over total emoluments.
- s 10(2)(a) says "an appropriately qualified employee of the Board or person
  performing duties in the Board"; whether "appropriately qualified" also governs the
  second limb was not resolved, and the second limb is not encoded.
- s 11 coverage is a single input, not derived from the list of persons.
- The Parks and Trees Act 2005 (which supplies the definitions of park, plant and
  nature reserve) and any regulations made under s 32 were not read.
- No case law was searched.
