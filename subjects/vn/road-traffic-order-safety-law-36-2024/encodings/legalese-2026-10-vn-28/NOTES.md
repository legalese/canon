## 1. What is encoded and what is not

### 1.1 The sources: two gazette issues

The Government gazette of 24 August 2024 prints the Law across two issues, and the sources hold both:

- `law36-2024-qh15-977-978.txt`, issue 977+978: the title, Chapter I (Articles 1-9, among them Article 2, the definitions, and Article 9, the prohibited acts) and Chapter II as far as Article 23.
- `law36-2024-qh15.txt`, issue 979+980: from Article 24 to Article 89 and the signature. Its line 16 reads "(Tiếp theo Công báo số 977 + 978)" (continued from issue 977+978).

The first pass of this row had only issue 979+980; Articles 1-23 were missing, and Decision 2 then declined every positive alcohol reading except blood alcohol found endogenous, for want of Article 9.
The lead added issue 977+978 on 2026-10-07, and this second pass read Articles 1-9 in full and Articles 10-23 for anything on age, licences, alcohol, registration or service life (none).
What Articles 1-23 changed:
Article 9(2) forbids driving with any alcohol in the blood or breath, so the Law's level is zero and Decision 2 now answers (findings R1, R13);
Article 9(10) forbids converting other automobiles into passenger automobiles, which narrows finding R9;
Article 2(2) and 2(9) confirm the split of motor vehicles, special-use machines and their drivers that Article 34 and 56 draw (finding R8);
Article 2(12) defines a road traffic accident as a collision (finding R15, reading only).

### 1.2 What is encoded

- **Article 2(2), (7), (9)**: the definitions the rest rests on (road vehicles, conversion, the driver), quoted where they are used.
- **Article 9(1)-(3), (10)**: the prohibitions on driving without the licence required, with alcohol, and with drugs or stimulants; and on converting automobiles into passenger automobiles.
- **Article 34** (the kinds of road vehicle), (1)(a)-(g), (2), (3): a classification of a vehicle described by its measurements and features.
- **Articles 56(1), (2), (4), (5)**, **57(1)-(5), (6)(a), (7)**, **58(3)**, **59(1)**, **62(5)**: who may drive what, with which licence, when a licence is in force and may be driven under, and the ages.
- **Articles 88(1), (3)** and **89(1)-(3)**: from when the Law governs, what a licence of a former class still entitles its holder to drive, and what it may be exchanged for.
- **Articles 81(3), 83(2)(a), 87(5)**: testing after an accident, and the Ministry of Health's determination of endogenous alcohol in blood.
- **Article 39(6)**, **40(1)-(4)**, **43(2)(c)**: revocation of the registration certificate and plate, the service life, and who stays responsible until revocation.

Three decisions, in `law36-vn28-decisions.l4`, each `@export`ed and each taking the day as an input:

1. `Decision 1 — the driver … may drive … on …`: of the age, and holding a licence (or the certificates, or the learner's permission) that entitles the vehicle on the day.
   Its parts are callable on their own: `Article 59 — the driver … is of the age to drive … on …`, `Article 56(1) — the driver … holds a licence that entitles the driving of … on …`, `Article 56 — a driving licence is required to drive …`, and per licence `Article 57(7)(a) — … has expired by …`, `Article 57(7)(b) — … has been revoked under Article 62(5) by …`, `Article 58(3) — points are left on the licence …`.
2. `Decision 2 — the test … exceeds the permitted level, on …` and `Decision 2 — the test … found a prohibited narcotic or stimulant, on …`; `the permitted level of alcohol under the Law`, which is 0 (Article 9(2)); and the Law's own test, `Article 9(2) — the test … shows driving with alcohol in the blood or breath, on …`.
3. `Decision 3 — the registration or plate of … has been revoked, or it is past its service life, on …`, with its parts `Article 39 — the registration certificate in … has been revoked by …`, `Article 39 — the plate in … has been revoked by …`, `Article 40 — the vehicle … is past its service life on …`, `Article 40 — less than a year of service life is left to … on …` (for Decree 67 Article 9(1)(b)) and `Article 43(2)(c) — …`.

### 1.4 What is not encoded

In Chapter III: Articles 35-38, 39(1)-(5), (7)-(8), and 41-55 save 43(2)(c).
In Chapter IV: Articles 56(1)(a)-(d), (2)(a)-(đ), (3); 57(6)(b)-(d), (8), (9); 58(1)-(2), (4)-(6); 59(2); 60-61; 62(1)-(4), (6)-(7); 63-64.
Each with its reason in section 2.

**Declined by a named `REFUSE`:** a day before 1 January 2025 (the Road Traffic Law 23/2008/QH12, not in the sources); a foreign or international licence; an army or police driver on duty; a vehicle that answers no limb of Article 34, or two; a trailer described alone; a licence whose class and date of issue disagree, or whose printed expiry contradicts Article 57(5); a vehicle no class entitles, when its age is asked; a service life the Government's rules fix and the caller did not supply; a conversion Article 40(2) does not provide for.

**No figure in this encoding comes from anywhere but the text.**
The Law's alcohol level is zero (Article 9(2)); the figure 0 in the encoding is that.
The Law states no service life in years (Article 40(4) leaves it to the Government), and none is invented here.

## 4. Findings

A finding is a defect or a surprise in the Law as written (or in how it meets Decree 67), not an ambiguity resolved.
Each gives the source lines, a minimal scenario, and the evidence: an assertion in `law36-vn28-findings.l4` (satisfied, because it shows what the Law does), or the words "reading only".
Decree 67 is quoted in English: its Vietnamese is not in this row's source, and the checker admits only this Law's words.

**R1. Decree 67 speaks of exceeding a "normal value"; the Law permits no alcohol at all.**
Decree 67 Article 7(2)(đ) excludes damage to property where the driver's blood or breath alcohol exceeds "the normal value in the guidance of the Ministry of Health" (Decree 67 src 225-227, in VN-10's rendering).
This Law forbids driving "mà trong máu hoặc hơi thở có nồng độ cồn" (with an alcohol concentration in the blood or breath; Article 9(2), 977+978 src 238-239): any alcohol, no level.
Its only other words on the matter are the tests (81(3), src 1760-1765; 83(2)(a), src 1818-1824) and the Ministry of Health's power over "xác định nồng độ cồn và nồng độ cồn nội sinh trong máu" (87(5), src 1934-1936).
So the decree presupposes a tolerance the Law does not grant, and refers to a guidance that this row (like VN-10) has not found.
The two fit together on one reading only: the decree's "normal value" is the body's own alcohol, which the Ministry determines under 87(5) (fork F8); then any other alcohol exceeds it.
On the other reading the decree's exclusion waits on a figure nobody has located, while the driver has broken the Law at any reading above zero.
Scenario: a breath reading of 0.05 after an accident damaging property.
Evidence: `law36-vn28-findings.l4`, R1 (the Law's level is 0; the reading exceeds it).

**R2. The same class letters mean different vehicles before and after 1 January 2025.**
A card of class "D" issued in 2025 drives coaches of more than 29 seats (57(1)(i), src 1042-1046); one issued in 2024 drives 9 to 29 seats (89(2)(i), src 1992-1995).
A "B1" card is a car licence if issued before 2025 (89(2)(e), src 1983-1985) and a three-wheeled motorcycle licence after (57(1)(c), src 1017-1018); A1 and C also change meaning.
Decree 67's limb "a licence not appropriate to the vehicle" cannot be applied to the letters alone: the date of issue decides.
Scenario: a man of 40 with a "D" card drives a 35-seat coach in 2026.
Evidence: `law36-vn28-findings.l4`, R2: licensed with a 2025 card, unlicensed with a 2024 card.

**R3. The E classes, and the former F classes, do not name the vehicle without its trailer.**
Class B covers a B automobile towing up to 750 kg (src 1019-1022); class BE covers it towing more than 750 kg (src 1047-1048) and says nothing of the automobile alone; the same for C1E, CE, D1E, D2E, DE (src 1049-1060) and for FB2, FC, FD, FE (src 1999-2005).
On the text, a BE holder is unlicensed driving the car alone or with a 500 kg trailer, and so excluded under Decree 67 Article 7(2)(c), unless the driver also holds the base class.
Scenario: a driver whose only card is BE drives the car with no trailer.
Evidence: `law36-vn28-findings.l4`, R3; fork F7.

**R4. No class entitles a special-use automobile above 3,500 kg towing a trailer of up to 750 kg.**
C1 and C let only "xe ô tô tải" (trucks) tow up to 750 kg (src 1024-1025, 1028-1029); C1E and CE begin above 750 kg (src 1049-1053).
Scenario: a 6,000 kg special-use automobile towing a 500 kg trailer: no licence of any class lets anyone drive it, and Article 59 then gives it no age.
Evidence: `law36-vn28-findings.l4`, R4 (the list of entitling classes is empty; the age is declined; even a class C holder is unlicensed).

**R5. A licence with no points left is in force, yet may not be driven under; Decree 67's licence limbs do not name it.**
Article 57(7) lists only expiry and revocation as making a licence not in force (src 1089-1091); Article 58(3) says the holder of a licence with no points left may not drive under it (src 1127-1129); Article 56(1) asks for a licence "đang còn điểm, còn hiệu lực" (src 970).
Decree 67 Article 7(2)(c) names no licence, an invalid, erased, expired or inappropriate licence, and a suspended or revoked one (Decree 67 src 215-222); none is a licence with no points.
Scenario: a class B driver with 0 points injures a pedestrian: the Law says the driver may not drive; whether the insurer's exclusion bites is not settled by either text.
Evidence: `law36-vn28-findings.l4`, R5 (in force TRUE; may drive FALSE; the class still fits).

**R6. An unexchanged former A1 card does not reach the smallest motorcycles.**
Former A1 covers two-wheeled motorcycles from 50 to under 175 cm3, or 4 to under 14 kW (src 1966-1968).
A two-wheeler under 50 cm3 (or under 4 kW) designed for more than 50 km/h is a motorcycle, not a moped (34(1)(e), (g), src 275-284), and needs a licence; a new A1 covers it (src 1012-1013), the restricted A that a former A1 is exchanged for covers it (src 2010-2012), and the former A1 itself does not.
Scenario: a holder of a 2010 A1 card rides a 49 cm3 motorcycle designed for 60 km/h.
Evidence: `law36-vn28-findings.l4`, R6.

**R7. Article 34(1)(a) and (g) overlap.**
A three-wheeler of more than 400 kg kerb mass is an automobile (src 256-257); a three-wheeler designed for not more than 50 km/h with not more than 50 cm3 is a moped (src 278-283), with no limit on mass.
The motorcycle limb excludes mopeds; the automobile limb does not.
Scenario: a 450 kg three-wheeler with a 45 cm3 engine designed for 40 km/h: as a moped it needs no licence; as an automobile it needs class B.
Evidence: `law36-vn28-findings.l4`, R7 (both kinds; Decision 1 declines).

**R8. A tractor is no longer a motor vehicle, and its driver needs no driving licence; Decree 67 still treats it as both.**
Article 2(2) sorts road vehicles into motor vehicles, rudimentary vehicles and special-use machines (977+978 src 34-36); Article 34(3)(c) puts "Máy kéo" among the special-use machines (src 298), not the motor vehicles of 34(1); Article 9(1) forbids operating a special-use machine without "giấy phép lái xe hoặc chứng chỉ bồi dưỡng kiến thức pháp luật về giao thông đường bộ" and the machine certificate (977+978 src 235-237); Article 56(2) says the same (src 982-986).
Decree 67 Annex I prices a tractor as a motor vehicle (VN-10, Annex I section A.VII.5), and Article 7(2)(c) of the decree asks for a driving licence.
Scenario: a 20-year-old with both certificates and no licence drives a tractor and injures someone: lawful under this Law; "no driving licence" under the decree's literal words, unless the decree's qualifier (vehicles that must have a licence) is read to save it.
Evidence: `law36-vn28-findings.l4`, R8.

**R9. Article 40(2) leaves some conversions without a rule.**
40(2)(a) and (b) govern a conversion that gains or loses a service life; (c) names conversions from passenger to goods automobiles and from four-wheeled motorised passenger to goods vehicles (src 503-512).
A conversion from one kind with a service life to another, other than (c)'s, is not provided for.
The plainest case, a truck converted into a coach, Article 9(10) forbids, save for defence or security (977+978 src 258-259), and a defence vehicle has no service life (40(3)(b)); so the gap is narrower than it looks.
It remains for a conversion 9(10) does not reach because the vehicles are not automobiles: a four-wheeled motorised goods vehicle made into a four-wheeled motorised passenger vehicle, the reverse of 40(2)(c)'s second limb.
Scenario: such a conversion; the goods vehicle's rule or the passenger vehicle's?
Evidence: `law36-vn28-findings.l4`, R9 (9(10) does not forbid it; 40(2) declined by name; and the forbidden truck-to-coach conversion, for which 40(2) has no rule either).

**R10. The Law fixes no service life in years.**
Article 40 says which kinds have none (40(3)), how the life is counted (40(1)), and leaves the rest to the Government (40(4), src 517).
Decree 67 lets an insurer refuse a vehicle past its service life (Article 4(5)(a)) and allows a short term for one with less than a year left (Article 9(1)(b)); both depend on rules not in the sources.
The one figure in the Law is Article 46(1)(a)'s: an automobile in school transport must have a service life of not more than 20 years (src 692-693), a condition of that business, not of the vehicle's service life.
Evidence: `law36-vn28-findings.l4`, R10 (declined by name).

**R11. Former classes measure trucks by payload, the new ones by gross mass; an exchange can lose a vehicle.**
Former B2 drives trucks under 3,500 kg payload (src 1986-1988); class B drives trucks up to 3,500 kg gross design mass (src 1019-1021); 89(3)(e) exchanges B1 and B2 for "B or C1" (src 2021-2023) and says nothing of who chooses or how.
Scenario: a truck of 2,000 kg payload and 5,000 kg gross: the unexchanged B2 holder is licensed; exchanged for B, unlicensed; for C1, licensed.
Evidence: `law36-vn28-findings.l4`, R11.

**R12. A sale is a case for revocation, and the seller stays responsible until it is done; with an auctioned plate only the certificate goes.**
Article 39(6)(a) makes a transfer of ownership a case for revoking the registration certificate and plate, and for an auctioned plate the certificate only (src 480-481); Article 43(2)(c) keeps the person named in the certificate responsible as owner until the revocation is done (src 630-632); Article 36(3)(b) lets the owner keep the plate number for 5 years (src 363-368).
For Decree 67 this answers VN-10's finding R9 in part: the decree's Article 9(3) points to its Article 11, which reaches a sale, because a sale leads to revocation.
It also opens two questions: between the sale and the revocation the seller's insurance runs and the seller stays responsible; and Decree 67 Article 11 ends the insurance on revocation of "the registration certificate, the plate", which, where only the certificate is revoked, it does not say is enough.
Evidence: `law36-vn28-findings.l4`, R12.

**R13. Article 87(5) speaks of blood; Article 9(2) and Decree 67 of blood or breath.**
The Ministry of Health's determination of endogenous alcohol is "trong máu" (in blood, src 1936); Article 9(2) forbids alcohol in the blood or the breath (977+978 src 238-239), and Decree 67's limb reaches breath too.
A breath reading cannot be found endogenous under 87(5), so the exception of fork F28 is open only to a driver whose blood was tested.
Scenario: two drivers with the same endogenous alcohol, one breath-tested at the roadside, one blood-tested in hospital: the first is caught, the second cleared.
Evidence: `law36-vn28-findings.l4`, R13 (blood, endogenous: not exceeded; breath, endogenous: exceeded).

**R14. A learner drives an automobile lawfully with no licence.**
Article 56(5) has learners and examinees drive training and test automobiles on their routes with an instructor or examiner (src 1006-1009).
Article 9(1) forbids driving a motor vehicle without a licence "theo quy định của pháp luật" (as the law requires; 977+978 src 234), which the learner does not breach.
Decree 67 Article 7(2)(c) excludes a driver with no licence of a vehicle that must have one; an automobile must (Article 56(1)).
Scenario: a learner in a driving-school car (a class Decree 67 Annex I prices, section A.VII.1) injures a pedestrian.
Evidence: `law36-vn28-findings.l4`, R14 (may drive; a licence is required for the vehicle; the learner holds none).

**R15. The Law's road traffic accident is a collision; Decree 67's accident may not be.**
Article 2(12) defines "Tai nạn giao thông đường bộ" as "va chạm" (a collision) involving people or vehicles in road traffic, unintended, causing harm to life, health or property (977+978 src 63-66).
Decree 67 insures liability for harm in an accident; VN-10's deposit (its modules, NOTES.md and GLOSSARY.md) quotes no definition of the accident, and neither "tai nạn giao thông" nor "va chạm" occurs in it (checked by grep, 2026-10-07).
If the decree's accident is read through this definition, harm without a collision (a passenger thrown in a bus that brakes hard, a fire from a parked car) is not a road traffic accident.
Evidence: reading only.

**Reading only.**
