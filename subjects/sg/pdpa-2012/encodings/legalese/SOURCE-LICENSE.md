# SOURCE-LICENSE — Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (Singapore)

**Status: OPEN — not determined.** This file records the question, not an answer. Do not
read the absence of a restriction here as a grant.

## The question

The source text is Singapore primary and subsidiary legislation, published by the Attorney-
General's Chambers on the Singapore Statutes Online service (<https://sso.agc.gov.sg>),
together with PDPC Advisory Guidelines published by the Personal Data Protection Commission.
Singapore government material is subject to Crown/Government copyright and to the publishing
body's own terms of use. **Which terms permit what, for a corpus of this kind, has not been
checked.** It must be settled before this row moves off `mengwong/drafts`.

Unlike the two cases the repository README already resolves — United States federal material
(public domain, 17 U.S.C. § 105 and the edicts-of-government doctrine) and United Kingdom
legislation (Open Government Licence v3) — Singapore has no equivalent finding recorded here
yet.

## What this encoding actually reproduces

Relevant to the answer, and smaller than it might appear: `pdpa-dbno.l4` **cites and
paraphrases rather than reproducing**. Section numbers appear in comments (§26A, §26B, §26C,
§26D, First Schedule Part 1); the operative content is restated as L4 declarations. The one
verbatim fragment is the five-word phrase `"a data breach occurred"` quoted from §26A. No
substantial passage of the Act, the Regulations, or the Guidelines is reproduced, and no
`source/` bundle of the text is vendored under this subject.
