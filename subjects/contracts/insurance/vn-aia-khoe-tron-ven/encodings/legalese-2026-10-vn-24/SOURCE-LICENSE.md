# Source licence — row VN-24

The source is AIA Vietnam's product rules and terms for Khỏe Trọn Vẹn (V012022). Its licence position is recorded once, at subject level: `../../SOURCE-LICENSE.md` (terms **undetermined**; the PDF and its text rendering are not held in this repository; quotation ruled acceptable for short clause-level lines, Meng, 2026-10-06).

What this encoding quotes:

- **739 `-- src:N |` comment lines in the `.l4` modules, quoting 735 distinct lines** of the 3 473-line `pdftotext` rendering, which has about 1 394 lines of text once blank lines, the diagonal watermark and the running headers and footers are set aside (so about 53% of the text lines).
- Every quotation is generated by `tools/vnsrc.py quote`, never typed. Long provisions are quoted in part: in Annexes 5-7 only the first line or two of each definition, in Annex 1 and Parts I-V at most two or three lines per quoted range, with a comment `(src N-M: K further line(s) not quoted)` pointing at the rest.
- Short verbatim Vietnamese runs (terms, headings) also appear in comments, `GLOSSARY.md`, `COMPARABLES.md` and `NOTES.md`; each is checked to occur in the source.

If the issuer objects to the quotation, delete the `-- src:N |` lines and the `-- (src …)` pointers; the rules and the tests do not depend on them.

The encoding itself is Apache-2.0. That does not extend to the quoted source text.
