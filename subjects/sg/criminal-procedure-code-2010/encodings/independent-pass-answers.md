## C. Definitions (s 2(1))

- Arrestable offence: police may ordinarily arrest without warrant according to col 3 or under any other written law. 379 arrestable; 323 non-arrestable; 109 of 379 arrestable; 109 of 323 non-arrestable.
- Bailable offence: shown as bailable in col 5 or made bailable by any other written law; everything else non-bailable. 323 bailable; 379 non-bailable; 109 of 323 bailable; 120B conspiracy to commit 379 non-bailable.
- Fine-only offence: punishable only with a fine: 137, 154, 155, 283, 290(a) are fine-only; 177 line 1 (imprisonment one month or fine*) is NOT; any "Imprisonment ..., or fine, or both" is not.
- Derived rows follow the underlying offence's row:
  - Abetment (109) of 323: non-arrestable, summons, bailable, 3 years or fine*, MC or DC.
  - Abetment (109) of 379: arrestable, warrant, non-bailable, 3 years, MC or DC.
  - 120B conspiracy to commit a 121D offence: arrestable, warrant, non-bailable, District Court.
  - 512(2) attempt of 323: non-arrestable, summons, bailable; attempt of 379: arrestable, warrant, non-bailable; attempt at 392 (robbery): court DC.
  - 73/74A/74B on 323: arrest Y, warrant, bailable (as 323), up to twice 3 years, MC or DC.
  - 471 using a forged document: arrestable, warrant, bailable, punishment of forgery, court of the forgery.

## D. Jurisdiction (ss 7(1)(a), 8(1), 9)

- MC may try if max imprisonment <= 5 years or fine-only (s 7(1)(a)): 5 years yes; 6 years no (unless s 9(2)(a): col 7 says MC; or s 9(1) PP written authorisation).
- DC may try if max imprisonment <= 10 years or fine-only (s 8(1)): 10 yes; 11 no (unless s 9(3)(a) col 7 says DC; (b) other law says DC; (c) PP applies and accused consent (all accused if several)). Never an offence punishable with death under s 9(3).
- s 9(2): 284(1)(d) (6 years, col 7 MD): MC may try. 175 (one month, col 7 "District Court" only): MC may still try under s 7(1)(a). 193 line 1 (7 years, col 7 D): MC may not try without s 9(1) authorisation.
- s 9(3): DC cannot try 302 (Death) or 121 (Death or life). 395 (20 years and caning, blank col 7): DC only under (c) with PP application and consent. 392 line 2 (14 years, col 7 D): DC yes by 9(3)(a). 375(2) rape (blank): DC only by (c).
- Other-law rows: offence with 4 years: MC yes, DC yes; 7 years: MC no, DC yes; 12 years: MC no, DC no unless the law says or s 9(3)(c); fine only: both.

## E. Acts creating their own offences

- PCA s 32(1): every PCA offence is deemed arrestable for CPC purposes: a PCA offence punishable with under 3 years (band 3 would say N) is nevertheless arrestable; warrant/summons and bail stay per the band (summons, bailable).
- PCA s 34: DC has jurisdiction to try any PCA offence and award full punishment: PCA offence with 15 years max: DC may try (s 8(1) alone would deny). MC: only per s 7(1)(a) (<= 5 years).
- MDA s 25(1): police may arrest without warrant anyone who has committed or is reasonably suspected of an offence under the Act: MDA offences are arrestable (via "any other written law" in s 2(1)).
- MDA s 53: DC or MC has jurisdiction to hear and determine all proceedings under the Act; DC may impose the full penalty except death. MDA offence with 30 years: DC may try. MDA offence punishable with death: DC may not impose death.
- The last four rows give the default; the Act's own section overrides arrest (PCA 32(1)) and jurisdiction (PCA 34, MDA 53).

## G. ss 159 and 211A

- s 159(1): CCDP apply to an offence that is (a) specified in the Second Schedule AND (b) to be tried in a District Court. MDA offence in DC: applies. MDA offence in MC: does not (159(3), unless all parties consent). Road Traffic Act offence in DC: does not unless all parties consent; with consent applies. Penal Code offence in DC: applies. Immigration Act s 6 offence in DC: does not; s 5 in DC: applies.
- s 211A(1): CCDP in Division 3 apply to any offence (a) that must be tried in the General Division of the High Court, or (b) set out in a written law specified in the Second Schedule and PP designates the General Division to try. 211A(2): any other offence to be tried in the General Division only if all parties consent.
  - Murder (must be tried in HC): applies. Moneylenders Act offence PP designates to HC: applies. Road Traffic Act offence PP designates to HC: no, absent consent; with consent: yes. Scheduled-law offence tried in HC without PP designation and not required to be there: not under (b); only by consent under (2).
