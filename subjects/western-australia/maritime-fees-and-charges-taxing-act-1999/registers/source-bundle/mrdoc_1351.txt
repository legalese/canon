Western Australia
Maritime Fees and Charges (Taxing) Act 1999
An Act to impose certain fees and charges levied by maritime authorities to the extent that any such fee or charge may be a tax.
The Parliament of Western Australia enacts as follows:
1.	Short title
		This Act may be cited as the Maritime Fees and Charges (Taxing) Act 1999.
2.	Commencement
		This Act comes into operation on the day on which it receives the Royal Assent.
3.	Imposition of tax under Port Authorities Act 1999
		To the extent that any fee or charge referred to in section 37 of the Port Authorities Act 1999 may be a tax, this Act imposes the fee or charge.
