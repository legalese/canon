## What is encoded, and what is not

The law gives ordinary Israeli residents two credit points under s 34, a quarter point under s 36, half a point to a woman under s 36A, one point under s 37 to a favoured individual who proves he maintained his spouse, and the rest of Chapter Three's credits.
Two powers take that away from, or hand it to, two groups of people.

**s 48 and the Order of 5755-1995.**
A resident of the Area who is not an Israeli citizen (both words as s 3A(a) defines them) is treated "as if he were an Israeli resident" for ss 34, 36 and 37.
The Order applied those three sections to every such resident.
It does not name s 36A, nor any other section.
So the resident of the Area gets: s 34, two points; s 36, a quarter point; s 37, one point if its own facts are met.
Whether he gets s 36A's half point is not the Order's doing: s 36A has no residence limb, and that is fork F1 carried from row IL-01.

**s 48A and the Regulations of 5775-2014.**
A foreign worker (a worker who is not an Israeli citizen or resident, who holds or needs an Entry into Israel Law visa) who is a "legal foreign worker" (stay and work lawful, not an expert or guest lecturer, a B/1 temporary-worker visa) gets, in computing tax on his income under s 2(1) or (2):

- in the caring sector (a caring permit and the stay extended under s 3A(b) of the Entry into Israel Law): points under ss 34, 36 and 36A, which is 2, 1/4, and 1/2 if a woman (reg 3(a));
- otherwise: one point under s 34 (reg 3(a1)), and half a point under s 36A if a woman (reg 3(b));
- for a stay of part of the tax year: one twelfth of those points for each month of the stay (reg 3(c));
- and nothing else: "the provisions of Chapter Three, except as stated in these Regulations, do not apply in computing the tax on the income of a foreign worker" (reg 3(d)).
Reg 3(d) says "a foreign worker", not "a legal foreign worker", so a foreign worker who is not legal gets nothing at all.
The Regulations apply to income produced in the tax year 2015 or after (reg 4).

**Not encoded.**
The ordinary sections 34, 36, 36A, 37 themselves (rows IL-01, IL-08), s 66 (IL-02), s 3A(f) (an Israeli citizen resident in or operating in the Area, IL-01), the s 1 residence test (IL-08), the credit point's value and the set-off (s 33A, s 120B; IL-01, IL-03), the rest of the Foreign Workers Law.
This row takes their results as inputs.
