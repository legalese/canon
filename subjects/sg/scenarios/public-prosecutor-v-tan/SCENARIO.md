# Scenario: Public Prosecutor v Tan

One case run through four encodings. `l4` cannot import across subjects, so each Act has its own file in its own `encodings/` directory, all generated by `build.py` from one set of facts. Only the Acts' `Written Law Procedure Statement`s cross between rows: each Act's file asserts the statement it computes, and the CPC file hard-codes the same values.

**Facts.** Tan holds the key to a flat and is found holding packets of 4 g of diamorphine. At the station he offers Officer Lim, a Government officer, $5,000 to lose the exhibit. Charges: Misuse of Drugs Act 1973 s 5(1); Prevention of Corruption Act 1960 s 5(b).

| file | assertions | what it decides |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `misuse-of-drugs-act-1973/encodings/mda-tests-scenario-tan.l4` | 21 | controlled drug; s 17 presumption (4 g over, 2 g not); s 18(1)/(2); s 5(1) with and without rebuttal; punishment 5 to 20 years, 5 strokes minimum; the statement handed to the Code |
| `prevention-of-corruption-act-1960/encodings/pca-tests-scenario-tan.l4` | 15 | gratification; agent; s 5(b) with corruption shown, and via the s 8 presumption; the s 8 presumption lost when the contrary is proved; punishment $100,000 / 5 years; the statement handed to the Code |
| `evidence-act-1893/encodings/ea-tests-scenario-tan.l4` | 5 | Tan's offer is a relevant admission; s 23's condition does not exclude it in a criminal trial; s 107 burden on the accused for a proviso in the law defining the offence |
| `criminal-procedure-code-2010/encodings/cpc-tests-scenario-tan.l4` | 12 | both offences arrestable; courts that may try each (MDA 20 years needs the Act's statement for a District Court); both within the Second Schedule; s 159 disclosure procedures |

**Not linked by a real import:** the CPC takes the two statements as hard-coded copies, and takes the maximum term (20 and 5 years) from the caller. The EA file feeds nothing into the CPC: the Code's Part 14 is not encoded. The s 107 fact (that the s 17 rebuttal is "a proviso in the law defining the offence") is an input chosen by the test, not a conclusion of the encoding.
