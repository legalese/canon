# SOURCE-LICENSE — Israeli primary and subsidiary legislation

**Status: OPEN — not determined for this row, on the same question as `il/penal-law-1977`, `il/ofek-hadash-2008` and `il/hvac-work-licensing-2025`.** This file records the question, not an answer. Do not read the absence of a restriction here as a grant.

## The question

The sources are the Traffic Ordinance [New Version] 5721-1961 and the Traffic Regulations 5721-1961, both official publications of the State of Israel; one amending Act published in Sefer HaChukim and one set of amending regulations published in Kovetz HaTakanot; and a consolidated text of each instrument read from Hebrew Wikisource (ספר החוקים הפתוח), a volunteer project.
Israeli law provides that official publications of the State are not subject to copyright in the ordinary way, but **which terms permit what, for a corpus of this kind, has not been checked here**, and the Wikisource layer carries its own licence (CC BY-SA 3.0 for contributor text) whose reach over a mechanically consolidated statute is a separate question.
It must be settled before this row moves off the drafts shelf.

## What this encoding actually reproduces

- `source/traffic-ordinance.wiki` and `source/traffic-regulations.wiki` are the Wikisource wikitext of both instruments, deposited verbatim, with their revision ids in `source/revisions.json` and digests in `source/SHA256SUMS`.
- `source/sefer-hachukim-2265-p91-amendment-97.pdf` is the gazette issue carrying Amendment No. 97, as served by the Knesset's archive (fs.knesset.gov.il); `source/kovetz-hatakanot-6825-p114.pdf` is the Regulations' gazette issue as served by olaw.org.il.
- The `.l4` modules quote the definition of "שיכור" (paragraphs (3A)(a)-(d)), reg. 169A's definition and s.62(3) in comments, in Hebrew, and paraphrase the rest in English. No other passage is reproduced.
