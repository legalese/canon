## The subject

The National Health Insurance Law, 5754-1994, in the Hebrew Wikisource consolidation deposited at `../../registers/source-bundle/national-health-insurance-law-5754-1994.he.wiki.txt` (sha256 `3cc1bf4c098b23c4efb0e6a3e10e282874a7b42bee0986395003c47aecba52b2`, page revision 3081417).
It is an unofficial consolidation; the authoritative text is the Law as published in Reshumot with every amending Act.
Section 14 imposes the health insurance contribution: an employee's, paid by the employer and deducted from his wage; a self-employed person's; the contribution of one who is neither; a fixed amount for an old-age pensioner; the minimum amount; a contribution on certain National Insurance benefits; and the exemptions.
The employer-liable employee's contribution is what the capstone (row IL-07) takes today as an input, and what its GAPS.md (item 4, line 68) and IL-08i's NOTES.md list as missing.

## Scope: pinned

Encoded: s 2 "income"; s 14(a) as far as the contribution reads it; s 14(b)(1), (b)(2), (c)(1), (c)(2); (d)(1)-(3); (e)(1)-(4); (e1); (f); (f1); (g); (g1); (g2); s 15(a), (b) as the reason the National Insurance Law's maximum and minimum income are candidates (they are forks, not rules).
The question answered: what health insurance contribution does one insured person owe, for one calendar month from January 2026, and who pays it.
Out of scope, each with its reason in NOTES.md section 2: the basket of services, the sick funds, the regulation of services, financing and its allocation, the offences, the waiting period and its "special payment", the Minister's regulation-making powers (h), (i), the Institute's power to deduct from other payments (j), (d)(4), s 55 soldiers, s 57 special rates, s 15(c).
