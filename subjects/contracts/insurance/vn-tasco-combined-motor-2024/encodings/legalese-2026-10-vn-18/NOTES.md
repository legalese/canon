## 1. What is encoded and what is not

**The whole document is in scope** and every provision has a disposition in section 2; none is left deferred.

**Encoded:** the decision's effective date; the opening paragraph's list of vehicles; the definitions that decide cases (5, 7, 9, 10, 11, 13-16, 19); Chương I (the contract and its period, premium conditions and termination with refunds, Điều 1-3; the duties with deadlines, Điều 4-5, as regulative rules; transfer of ownership, Điều 6; the assessor's cost, Điều 7; the claim file, Điều 8; double insurance, Điều 9; the time limits and forum, Điều 10; the eleven general exclusions, Điều 11; the reductions, Điều 13); Chương II (physical damage: perils, exclusions, sum insured, partial and total loss, the depreciation table, salvage, deductible); Chương III (accident cover for the driver, assistant and people on board); Chương IV (liability for goods carried); Chương V (voluntary third-party liability); Chương VI (the fifteen additional clauses BS01-BS15) with Điều 35's table of optional deductibles.
Three layers are kept apart: whether the event is covered (perils, exclusions, buy-backs), how much is payable (depreciation, ratio, deductible, reduction, costs, cap), and what each party must do by when (DEONTIC rules in `tasco-ch1-duties.l4` and `tasco-ch1-contract.l4`).

**Inputs, not encoded:** everything the rules leave to the certificate (sums insured, the deductible stated, the period, the premium, the number of persons insured, the limits, the clauses held); everything they leave to another instrument (the Government decree on compulsory motor liability insurance, Decree 67/2023, whose payment table, compulsory limits and exclusions Điều 24, 31-34 adopt; the road traffic law's age limits and licence rules; the Ministry of Health's alcohol level; the Civil Code liability of Điều 26); and the determinations the rules give Tasco (the market value of Điều 16.3, the rate within the ranges of Điều 13.1.2-1.3, the share of fault of Điều 34).

## Findings

Each: the source lines, a minimal scenario, and the evidence. "Reading only" where the encoding cannot show it as an answer.

- **X1. Alcohol and drugs exclude only damage to property.** Điều 11.4 (src:640-644) begins "Thiệt hại đối với tài sản". A drunk driver killed in a crash is paid the full accident benefit of Chương III: `tasco-tests-ch3-5.l4`, `#ASSERT … a drunk driver killed … EQUALS payable 100_000_000`. (The same fact excludes goods and physical damage, which are property.) Against the insurer.
- **X2. "Chủ xe" defined twice.** The opening paragraph calls the buyer "chủ xe" (src:48); definition 5 defines "Chủ xe" as the owner or lawful possessor (src:67-69). Refunds go to "chủ xe" (src:197, 202), premiums are paid by the buyer. Reading only.
- **X3. "Tổn thất" is sudden damage, yet wear and tear is excluded as a "tổn thất".** Definition 18 (src:132-133) against Điều 15.1 (src:779). Reading only.
- **X4. Definition 19 is circular**: "Tai nạn giao thông gồm: Va chạm giao thông và tai nạn giao thông" (src:140-141). Reading only.
- **X5. Tasco's own termination refunds premium even after a claim.** Điều 3.2 third paragraph (src:201-203) has no "đã xảy ra sự kiện bảo hiểm" exception, unlike 3.1 and 3.2's second paragraph. `tasco-tests-ch1.l4`: `art 3.2 — the refund when Tasco terminates … EQUALS 1_830_000`, a rule that takes no insured-event fact.
- **X6. Điều 6.1 and 6.2 conflict.** A late notice where the old owner agreed to pass the contract on satisfies both (src:423-429). Taken: 6.2 ends it at the transfer (`… JUST (YMD 2025 3 17)) TRUE FALSE EQUALS it ends at the moment of the transfer`), so a loss in the gap is unpaid although the new owner was to be covered "từ thời điểm chuyển quyền sở hữu".
- **X7. Điều 6 is silent** where notice was in time and the old owner neither passed the contract on nor asked to end it: `#ASSERT REFUSED art 6 — what the transfer does … FALSE FALSE`.
- **X8. A broken cross-reference.** Điều 4.1.6 reduces compensation for breaches of "khoản 2, Điều 6" (src:257-258); Điều 6 is the transfer of ownership and its point 2 is no duty. The duties are Điều 5, point 2. Reading only.
- **X9. A second broken cross-reference.** Điều 4.2.6 points to "Điều 7" for the claim file (src:330-331); the claim file is Điều 8. Reading only.
- **X10. The claim file is whatever Tasco asks for.** Điều 8 says the file is "một hoặc nhiều loại tài liệu sau" (src:470), and the payment clock of Điều 4.2.3 runs only from a complete file (src:277-278). The claimant cannot tell when the file is complete. The encoding lists what a file may contain (`art 8 — …`, 11 documents for a plain collision); it cannot say which are required. Reading only.
- **X11. A complaint in time under 10.2 can be out of time under 10.1.** 10.1 voids "mọi khiếu nại" one year after the event (src:596-598); 10.2 gives 90 days from the decision (src:600-602). Event 1 June 2024, decision received 1 July 2025, complaint 1 August 2025: `tasco-tests-ch1.l4`, both assertions under "Findings shown on the encoding".
- **X13. Tasco's three working days have no consequence.** Điều 13.1.2(a) gives Tasco at most 03 working days to give its opinion on dismantling (src:699-700) and says nothing of what follows if it does not; an owner who dismantles after the deadline still faces the 20%-50% reduction: `… dismantled after Tasco's opinion was overdue, Tasco chose 20% … EQUALS 20%`.
- **X14. Two exclusions under one number.** Điều 11.1 prints the intentional act and the inspection certificate under "1." (src:614-623, page 10). Reading only.
- **X15. Reductions of up to 100% at Tasco's discretion.** Điều 13.1.2 and 13.1.3 reduce by 20%-50% and 50%-100% "tùy theo mức độ lỗi" (src:693, 705) with no criteria; 13.1.3(b) reaches any untruthful document. The encoding cannot compute the rate: `#ASSERT REFUSED … repaired without consent, no rate chosen …`.
- **X16. The total-loss threshold parts at exactly 75%.** Điều 18.2.1 says "trên 75%" for damage and "bằng hoặc trên 75%" for repair cost (src:931-934): `tasco-tests-ch2.l4`, the 337.5-of-450 million case.
- **X17. Permanent partial disability is not a benefit.** Điều 24 pays death or permanent total disability (24.1) and temporary injury (24.2) (src:1002-1008); a permanently but partially disabled passenger falls between: `#ASSERT REFUSED … a passenger partially and permanently disabled …`.

- **X19. Điều 32 and 34.1 disagree.** Điều 32 pays only the part above the compulsory limit (src:1095-1099); 34.1's formula ignores that layer (src:1133). An owner who paid 100,000,000 against a (hypothetical) compulsory limit of 150,000,000: 34.1 alone gives 60,000,000, the combined reading 0 (`tasco-tests-ch3-5.l4`).
- **X20. The clauses point to other documents.** Chương VI's clauses are "quy định trong Quy tắc bảo hiểm vật chất xe ô tô" (src:1155-1157); BS14 follows "Quy tắc bảo hiểm tự nguyện xe ôtô của Bảo hiểm Tasco" (src:1378); BS15 "quy tắc bảo hiểm xe cơ giới của Bảo hiểm Tasco" (src:1409). - **X21. BS03 does not cover an electric car's flooded motor.** Điều 15.2 excludes water hammer AND damage to the electric motor of an electric car (src:784-785); BS03 buys back only "hiện tượng thủy kích" (src:1190-1192): `… a contract on an electric car cover with BS03 a flooded electric motor … EQUALS excluded`.
- **X22. BS04 sets no limit for a contract under 12 months** (src:1208-1209): `#ASSERT REFUSED BS04 — the most thefts paid under a contract of 11`.
- **X23. BS09 qualifies vehicles at eight kinds of site and pays at five.** Airports, industrial zones and mining areas are in the condition (src:1300-1302), not the benefit (src:1305-1307): `BS09 — the loss is at a site the benefit names a forklift at an airport` is FALSE.
- **X24. BS15 charges a deductible 42 times Điều 19's for a battery Chương II already pays.** The depreciation table already has a traction-battery column (src:875, page 15), so the base cover pays the battery; BS15 adds a deductible of 10%, at least 3.000.000 (src:1410). On a 37-month electric car, battery 300,000,000: Chương II pays it within 226,500,000 for battery and bumper after a 500,000 deductible; BS15 computed alone pays 189,000,000 for the battery after a 21,000,000 deductible (`tasco-tests-ch6-claims.l4`). How the two combine is unsaid (fork F35).
(X12 is not used: a candidate about stolen-vehicle claims and the one-year bar was withdrawn, since it rested on assumptions about criminal procedure outside the sources.)

- **X25. Chương V's inspection-certificate exclusion is stricter than Chương II's.** Điều 33.3 (src:1110-1112) repeats Điều 11.1's words without the three spared causes: a certificate lapsed only by a roof rack excludes a third-party claim (`tasco-tests-ch3-5.l4`) but not a physical damage claim (`tasco-tests-ch1.l4`).
