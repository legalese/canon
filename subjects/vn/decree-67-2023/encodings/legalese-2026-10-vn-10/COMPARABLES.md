# Comparables — Decree 67/2023/NĐ-CP, compulsory motor insurance (row VN-10)

One row per parameter, for the later cross-insurer analysis.
The Vietnamese is verbatim from the gazette text; "src line N" is line N of `../../source/raw/nd67-congbao-1017-1018.txt`, or of `nd67-congbao-1019-1020.txt` where the line says so.
"not stated" means the decree does not state it; where the Law on Insurance Business fills it, NOTES.md §3 (LAW forks) says so, and nothing here is filled from that Law.
The two vintages agree on every row: Decree 220/2026 changes only the word for the third party.
