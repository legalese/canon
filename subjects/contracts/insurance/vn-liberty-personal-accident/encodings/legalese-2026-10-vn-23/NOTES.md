## 1. What is encoded and what is not

**The whole document is in scope, and every provision has a row in section 2.**
Encoded as rules: the insuring clause and the payee; the definitions that decide something (period and deferred start, effective date, insurance year, age, minor, dependant, residence, Accident, Injury, permanence, employee, group, pre-existing condition); benefits A.1 and A.2 with both tables and every note under them; benefits B, C and D; the geographical scope (as an input); the limit per conveyance; limitations 1(a)-(c), 2, 3 and 4; special provisions 1-9; every general exclusion limb and special exclusions 1-3, 5 and 9; general provisions 1-9, 11-14, 16, 17 and conversion; and the three layers assembled in `the decision on` a claim.
Regulative rules (`DEONTIC`, exercised by `#TRACE`): the notice and forms duties of 13(a), the premium duty of 16A, and the permission to go to court of 14.

**Reached and refused by name:** special exclusions 4 and 6, which are written for property insurance (`special exclusions 4 and 6 are written for property insurance, ...`); an injury no table row prices, until the Company's rate is supplied; an arm or hand injury where handedness is not in the record; benefit B on income where the income is not in the record.

**Inert** (quoted or cited, operative on nothing): the documents that make up the contract, the definitions the wording never uses again, special exclusions 7 and 8 (property and pollution), general provisions 10 and 15, the precautions and disclosure duties that carry no stated consequence of their own.

**Inputs, never defaults:** everything the wording leaves to the summary or certificate (the period, each benefit's limit, the temporary-injury basis and day limits, the daily hospital allowance, the geographical area, food-poisoning cover, other exclusions agreed), and every judgement the wording hands to a person (occupation class, reasonable and customary charges, medical necessity, whether an injury item falls within a larger one, the Company's rate for an injury not in the tables, "adventurous activity").

**Not encoded:** the Law on Insurance Business. Where it fills or overrides the document, a fork tagged LAW says so (section 3).

## 4. Findings

The hostile reading, from the policyholder's side and the insurer's.
Each: the source lines, a minimal scenario, and the evidence (an assertion in `lpa-tests-findings.l4`, section D-number, or `lpa-tests.l4`), or "reading only".
A finding is a defect in the instrument as written; a fork (section 3) is an ambiguity resolved.

**D1. "hoặc đang thực hiện công việc" excludes every injury at work** (src 785-787). General exclusion 6(d) excludes injury while the insured is "tham gia các cuộc diễn tập huấn luyện quân sự, tham gia chiến đấu ... và/hoặc đang công tác với tư cách là thành viên của lực lượng vũ trang hoặc công an hoặc đang thực hiện công việc". Read literally, the last words exclude any insured performing work. Scenario: an office employee falls at her desk. Evidence: `general exclusion 6(d) read literally excludes` `performing work` is TRUE, while the encoding's reading (fork F22) engages nothing. A business group accident policy whose exclusions, literally read, remove injuries at work.

**D2. Cover ends retroactively on the day the insured left Vietnam** (src 1255-1260; 1288-1290; 1323-1325). 7.1(e) ends cover on "Ngày Người lao động ra khỏi lãnh thổ Việt Nam" once the absence passes 180 days without notice or agreement. Scenario: leaves 1 March, injured abroad 1 April; whether 1 April was covered depends on whether the insured comes home by day 180. Evidence: D2, two assertions (181 days: not in force on 1 April; 180 days: in force). 3(f) (src 1189-1191) separately caps worldwide stays at 180 days.

**D3. An unpaid premium: nothing, 0,1%, or the full benefit** (src 1199-1214; 1552-1581). 4(a)(ii) and 4(b) say no liability arises if the premium condition fails; 16B(b) pays events before the termination at most 0,1% of the limit; the Law (art 27(1)(b), aid line 646-651) requires a health contract to pay events before a termination for non-payment. On a hospital allowance of 400.000 a day, 0,1% is 400 đồng. Evidence: D3; `claim D` in `lpa-tests.l4` (B 30.000, C 50.000, D 400). The employee bears the employer's failure to pay.

**D4. The top-up for death after a permanent injury benefit is almost unreachable** (src 156-163; 552-558). Partial permanence exists only from 52 weeks after the accident; 1(b)'s top-up needs death within 52 weeks of the Injury. Scenario: right eye lost 10 January 2026 (40% paid, 400.000.000 on a 1.000.000.000 limit); death 10 January 2027: nothing more. The same death with no permanent injury claim pays 1.000.000.000. Evidence: D4. A family that claimed the permanent injury benefit is 600.000.000 worse off.

**D5. General provision 13's deadlines cannot all be met** (src 1479-1486; 1515-1516). (i) If "sự kiện bảo hiểm" is the accident, the one-year filing limit expires before a total permanent injury can exist (104 weeks). (ii) The forms and evidence are due within 30 days of the insured event, and 13(d) voids a claim out of time or procedure, so the one-year period does no work. LAW: art 30 gives one year from the event, not counting force majeure. Evidence: D5; `claim B` (notice on day 31: every benefit void).

**D6. The teeth bands have a gap, an overlap and a dead row** (src 371-374). Exactly 8 teeth are in no band; more than 8 that dentures can replace, in none; 3 teeth in two (8% and 3%); and "Từ 1 – 3 răng 3%" is under the 5% floor (src 467), so on its own it never pays. Evidence: D6.

**D7. 10 of 31 finger sets and 20 of 31 toe sets have no row** (src 403-411, 448-450). Any two fingers without the thumb, three including the index but not the thumb, and all five; any toes without the big toe, two toes, all five. They go to the Company's sole discretion (src 459-465). The 50% ankylosis rule for toes other than the big toe (src 453-454) has no amputation amount to halve; the non-dominant middle finger's ankylosis (2,5%) is under the 5% floor. Evidence: D7.

**D8. Three different rules for medical expenses another contract covers** (src 573-578; 887-889; 1449-1453). Limitation 4: pay the excess. Special exclusion 3: pay nothing. General provision 11: the excess, or a rateable share, with no criterion for which. Scenario: 10.000.000 of bills, 4.000.000 met elsewhere, limits 50.000.000 of 100.000.000: 6.000.000, 0, or 5.000.000. Evidence: D8.

**D9. 52 weeks or 12 months** (src 229-231 against 512-513). The definition of medical expenses runs 12 months from the Injury; benefit C, 52 weeks. A bill on day 365 is inside one and outside the other. Evidence: D9.

**D10. Dental surgery: included and excluded** (src 232-233 against 829). Definition (b) includes "phẫu thuật nha khoa"; general exclusion 12 excludes "Tất cả các điều trị liên quan đến nha khoa". Reading only (the encoding takes F15).

**D11. Asphyxiation by fumes: extended and excluded** (src 616-619 against 770-771). Evidence: D11 (covered on the encoding's reading, F21); 5(g) unqualified would exclude it.

**D12. Any Injury must be notified "ngay", or cover ends** (src 1261-1267; 1291-1297; 1328-1335). 7.1(f) ends cover on the day the insured fails to notify at once a change increasing the risk, "bao gồm ... bất kỳ Ốm đau/Bệnh tật/Thương tật nào phát sinh trong Thời hạn bảo hiểm"; 13(a) allows 30 days for notice of an Injury. Read literally, the claimed injury, notified on day 20, ends cover on the day of the accident. Evidence: D12.

**D13. The cap on all benefits rests on an optional benefit** (src 311-314; 560-562). 1(c) caps all benefits at 100% of the death or permanent injury limit; benefit A is a choice. Without it, read literally, the cap is 0 (D13). With it, medical expenses count toward a cap set by the death limit: `claim H` in `lpa-tests.l4` cuts 150.000.000 of allowed medical expenses to 100.000.000.

**D14. Any breach of any law or regulation, intentional or not, excludes** (src 776-781). 6(a) names no link to the accident and includes "vi phạm luật giao thông". Scenario: a rider breaks a traffic rule unrelated to how the accident happened. Evidence: `claim C` in `lpa-tests.l4`; the encoding requires the breach to be a circumstance of the accident (fork F24), and the literal text does not even require that.

**D15. "Any race" swallows the carve-out for racing on foot** (src 727-728 against 739-740). 4(l) excludes racing "khác hơn so với đi bộ"; 4(e) excludes "bất kỳ cuộc đua nào". Evidence: D15.

**D16. The short-period bands share their edges** (src 1432-1434). "Đến 3 tháng" and "Từ 3 tháng đến 6 tháng" both include 3 months; likewise 6. Evidence: D16.

**D17. Overlapping and missing measurement bands** (src 364-365; 445-447). 5 cm is in two shortening bands (30% and 20%); "khoảng" (about) gives no measure; a skull loss under 3 cm2 and a shortening under 3 cm have no band. Evidence: D17; `lpa-tests.l4` (2,9 cm2, 2,9 cm).

**D18. The application outranks the endorsements that amend the contract** (src 1124-1130). An endorsement that changes a term cannot prevail over the application or quotation it changes. Evidence: D18.

**D19. Words and figures differ** (src 1220). "một triệu năm trăm (1.500.000)": the words, read literally, are one million five hundred (1.000.500); the second paragraph (src 1226-1227) and 8B (src 1415-1416) say "một triệu năm trăm ngàn". Whether everyday Vietnamese reads the first as 1.500.000 is outside knowledge, unverified. Reading only; the figure is encoded.

**D20. Defined terms that carry no weight, and weighty terms never defined.** Defined and never used again: "Năm bảo hiểm", "Đang làm việc", "Hiệu lực bảo hiểm", "Quê quán", "Mất thị lực", "Mất chi". The last two matter: the tables say "Mất một cánh tay", "một bàn tay", "Mất cả hai chân", and nothing ties them to "Mất chi" (loss from the wrist or ankle upward). Capitalised and never defined: "Tử vong", "Thai sản", "Nhân viên được bảo hiểm", "Hợp đồng bảo hiểm Nhóm". "Ôm đau" misspells the defined "Ốm đau" (src 153). Reading only.

**D21. Discretion with no criteria.** The rate for an injury not in the tables, at the Company's "toàn quyền đơn phương" (src 464-465); the norms for medically necessary hospital days (src 276-278); which remedy for non-disclosure (src 1150-1153); "terms the Company thinks fit" on a material change (src 1238-1241); how a refund is computed, "tùy theo quyết định của Công ty" (src 1414-1415); excess or rateable share (src 1450-1453); which bank and which moment for the exchange rate (src 1588-1591); conversion "theo quyết định đơn phương của Công ty" (src 1601-1604); dependants "trừ trường hợp Công ty có quyết định khác" (src 110); "Các tài liệu khác khi Công ty yêu cầu" (src 1513), an open list of documents whose absence voids a claim under 13(d). Evidence for the first: `lpa-tests.l4` (an injury not in the tables is refused until the Company's rate is supplied); the rest reading only.

**D22. Special provision 6 puts the employer's duty on the employee** (src 625-627): "với điều kiện Người được bảo hiểm phải thông báo vào cuối mỗi tháng". The list of employees is the policyholder's; the insured cannot know it. Reading only.

**D23. 7(h) ends cover "on" the last day of the period** (src 1269, 1299, 1327), while the period and the insurance year run to its end (src 88-92, 98-102). Read like 7(a)-(g), the last day would be uncovered. Reading only; fork F29.

**D24. Property and reinsurance clauses in an accident wording** (src 891-1103). Special exclusions 4 and 6 (nuclear energy risks, defined as classes of property and liability insurance), 7 (data and software damage) and 8 (pollution of property, clean-up, fines) concern nothing this contract pays for. Evidence: D24 (special exclusions 4 and 6 refused by name).

**D25. Cancellation for non-disclosure without intent** (src 1143-1153). 2(a) lets the Company cancel from the effective date for information the policyholder "phải biết hoặc được cho là biết"; LAW art 22(2) (aid line 543-553) requires intentional incomplete or false information. Reading only.

**D26. A subrogation clause where the Law forbids subrogation** (src 1457-1463). LAW art 16(4) and art 38 (aid line 402-406, 792-802). The clause's "phù hợp với quy định của pháp luật" may save it by giving it nothing to do. Reading only.

**D27. No time for the Company to pay** (silence). Only limitation 3 times the permanent injury instalments; LAW art 31 (aid line 718-724) fills 15 days. Reading only.

**D28. A 30-day period whose payment period ends after it** (src 1542-1544). "nếu Thời hạn bảo hiểm ít hơn 30 ngày" applies only under 30 days; a period of exactly 30 days takes 30 days from the effective date, which end the day after the period (fork F35). Evidence: D28.

**D31. An extension that cuts cover** (src 650-653). Special provision 8 extends cover to motorcycles "với điều kiện là xe gắn máy/xe mô tô không vượt quá 150cc"; no exclusion names motorcycles, so a 175cc rider is either covered by the insuring clause or uncovered by an extension. Evidence: D31 (fork F19).
