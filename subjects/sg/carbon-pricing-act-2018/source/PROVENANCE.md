## Extraction notes

- Repeated page footers ("Singapore Statutes Online Current version as at …") are in the text and are not part of the Act.
- Section 79 is followed by a stray "[80" (the pre-revision section number) on the line before the First Schedule; it is a marker, not text.
- **Instrument it is made under**: none; a primary Act. The Carbon Pricing Regulations (prescribed industry sectors, ICC criteria and limits, record periods, compoundable offences) are not in the source.
