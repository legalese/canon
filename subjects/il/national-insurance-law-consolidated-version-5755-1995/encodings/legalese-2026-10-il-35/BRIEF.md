# Encoding brief: the consumer price index readings (CBS) and the rise of the index that ITO s 1 / s 120B and NII s 334(a)(1) take from them, in L4

Row IL-35, run id `IL-35-20261008`, encoder `enc-il-35` (one session, no sub-agents).
This row is DATA plus the few rules that say which readings a Law takes; it is not a body of law.
This brief is the whole specification, so that a reviewer or an independent test author can work from it alone.

## The subject

Two Laws index amounts by the consumer price index (the "madad") the Central Bureau of Statistics (CBS) publishes:

- **Income Tax Ordinance [New Version], s 120B (הצמדה)** adjusts the income ceilings, credit-point and allowance-point amounts, social deductions and recognised donation amounts on 1 January of each tax year "by the rate of rise of the index in the previous tax year" ((a)), and the allowance point mid-year "by the rate of rise of the index from the start of the tax year until the end of the said month" ((b)); (e)(1) freezes the amounts for 2025-2027 and (e)(2) restarts on 1 January 2028 from the 1 January 2024 amounts with "the index of tax year 2027".
  The Ordinance's s 1 defines "מדד" (the CBS consumer price index) and "שיעור עליית המדד בתקופה פלונית" (the rate of rise of the index in a period): the index published last before the END of the period, less the index published last before the START of the period, over the latter.
  s 1 defines "שנת מס" (a tax year) as twelve consecutive months beginning 1 January, or a special assessment period if one is fixed.
- **National Insurance Law [Consolidated Version] 5755-1995, s 334(a)(1)** updates the reduced collection threshold on 1 January of 2026 to 2028 "by the rate of rise of the index published last before 1 January, against the index published last before 1 January of the previous year".
  Its s 1 defines "מדד" as the consumer price index the CBS publishes monthly.

Rows IL-03 (`ito-120b-indexation.l4`) and IL-04 (`nii-s334-interpretation.l4`) take the readings as INPUTS.
This row is what could supply them.

## Scope, pinned

- The published figures: every monthly reading, its base, and the Bureau's two percentages; the bases; the Bureau's linkage coefficients as step factors; the months the series covers.
- "The index published last before" a day (ITO s 1 "שיעור עליית המדד"), for the start and for the end of a month.
- "The rate of rise of the index in a period" (ITO s 1), for a period of whole months and for a twelve-month period.
- ITO s 120B(a): the rise in the previous tax year; s 120B(b): the rise from the start of the tax year to the end of a month; s 120B(e)(2): the period of the rise on 1 January 2028.
- NII s 334(a)(1): the rise for 1 January of 2026 to 2028.
- The pair of readings on one scale, in the shape IL-03 and IL-04 take as input.
- Out of scope: what a rise does to an amount (IL-03, IL-04); the rounding of an adjusted amount (ITO s 120B(d), an Order not deposited); the average wage (NII s 334(a)(2)); a special assessment period; any index other than the general CPI.

## Rules that matter

- Within one base the Law's arithmetic applies to the Bureau's figures as printed.
- Across a change of base the text does not say how readings are compared: one named switch, declined by default (Meng, SHRUG, 2026-10-08), the readings kept by name and tested.
- A fall in the index is returned as a negative rate; what it does is IL-03 F4 and IL-04 F6.
- A month outside the series is refused by name, never extrapolated.
- No figure is invented: the publication lag is an assumption recorded as such.
