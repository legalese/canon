17     31AA. Limit on increase in rent

18          (1) In this section --

19          CPI means the quarterly index number for the rents

20          subgroup of the housing group of the Consumer Price

21          Index for Perth published by the Australian Bureau of

22          Statistics established by the Australian Bureau of

23          Statistics Act 1975 (Cth) section 5(1);

24          increase limit, in relation to an increase in the rent

25          payable under a residential tenancy agreement, means

26          the amount calculated using the formula --

            L=R×  current CPI - initial CPI      × 1.1

8   31AB. Lessor's application to increase rent above increase

9           limit

10          (1) In this section --

11          increase limit has the meaning given in

12          section 31AA(1).

13          (2) A lessor under a residential tenancy agreement may

14          apply to a competent court for an order approving an

15          increase in the rent payable under the agreement by an

16          amount greater than the increase limit.

17          (3) In deciding the application, the court must have regard

18          to the following matters --

19          (a) the general level of rents for comparable

20                 premises in the locality or a similar locality;

21          (b) the estimated capital value of the premises;

22          (c) the amount of the outgoings in respect of the

23                 premises required to be met by the lessor under

24                 the agreement;

25          (d) the estimated cost of any services provided by

26                 the lessor or tenant under the agreement;

27          (e) the value and nature of the chattels provided

28                 with the premises for use by the tenant;

29          (f) the accommodation and amenities provided in

30                 the premises and their state of repair and

31                 general condition.

9     71BA. Termination of social housing tenancy agreement

10          by lessor

11          (1) A lessor may give notice of termination of a social

12          housing tenancy agreement to the tenant on the ground

13          that the lessor genuinely intends --

14          (a) to sell the premises; or

15          (b) to reconstruct, renovate or make major repairs

16          to the premises, which cannot reasonably be

17          carried out with the tenant living in the

18          premises; or

19          (c) to use the premises for another lawful use other

20          than as social housing premises.

21          (2) If a lessor gives notice of termination under

22          subsection (1), the period of notice must not be less

23          than 90 days before the day on which the tenant is

24          required under the notice to give the lessor vacant

25          possession of the premises.
