# Source license

The text of **Competition Act** is published on the [Justice Laws Website (Department of Justice Canada)](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/).

- **Terms**: Reproduction of Federal Law Order (SI/97-5): anyone may reproduce federal enactments and consolidations free of charge and without asking permission. Two conditions apply: take due diligence over accuracy, and do not present the copy as an official version. The Order covers reproduction; it does not expressly cover adaptation.
- **Terms checked**: 2026-09-22, at https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/FAQ/index.html
- **From the terms**: "anyone may, without charge or request for permission, reproduce enactments and consolidations of enactments of the Government of Canada"
- **Attribution required**: none required, but the copy must not be represented as an official version and must be accurate
- **Source**: https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-34/index.html

## Consequence for this subject

Reproduction is permitted on the conditions above, but the terms do not expressly grant a right to ADAPT. An L4 encoding that quotes the statute is arguably an adaptation. **Confirm before depositing text or quoting it in an encoding** -- a human decision recorded here.

## Language

English is not the only authoritative language. Federal Acts are enacted in both English and French, and the two versions have equal authority (Constitution Act, 1982, s. 18; Official Languages Act, s. 13). Justice Laws publishes both versions, at /eng/ and /fra/.
