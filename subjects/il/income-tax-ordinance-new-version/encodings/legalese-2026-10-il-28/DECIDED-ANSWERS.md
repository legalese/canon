# DECIDED-ANSWERS: IL-28 (ITO ss 48, 48A; Order 5755-1995; Credits for a Foreign Worker Regulations 5775-2014; Foreign Workers Law 5751-1991)

Author: fid-il-28, written from the Hebrew source alone before opening the encoding.
Sources read: ITO text lines 1561-1601 (ss 33A, 34, 36, 36A, 37), 1806-1810 (ss 48, 48A), 453-461 (s 3A definitions); Order 5755-1995 s 1; Regulations 5775-2014 regs 1-4; Foreign Workers Law s 1 ("עובד זר" – עובד שאינו אזרח ישראל, או תושב בה).
Confidence: H = text decides; M = text decides with one reading choice; L = reading choice is contestable.
"Points" means credit points (נקודות זיכוי) under Chapter Three of Part C; money is points times the point value of s 33A ("2,820 in 2023; 2,904 in 2024-2027", from the note in the deposited text).

## The rules as I read them

- R1 (s 34): "בחישוב המס של יחיד שהיה תושב ישראל בשנת המס יובאו בחשבון שתי נקודות זיכוי": 2 points for an individual resident in the tax year.
- R2 (s 36): "בחישוב המס של יחיד תושב ישראל תובא בחשבון 1/4 נקודת זיכוי כזיכוי נסיעה": 1/4 point for a resident individual.
- R3 (s 36A): "בחישוב המס של אשה תובא בחשבון 1/2 נקודת זיכוי": 1/2 point for a woman, no residence condition in the words.
- R4 (s 37): "יחיד מוטב תושב ישראל, שהוכיח להנחת דעתו של פקיד השומה כי בשנת המס שכלכלת בן זוגו היתה עליו": 1 point, if (a) a "beneficiary individual" (he or his spouse reached retirement age, or he or his spouse is blind or disabled per s 9(5)(a) or (a1)) and (b) he proved to the assessor that the spouse's livelihood was on him in the tax year.
- R5 (Order, s 1): ss 34, 36 and 37 "יחולו גם לגבי תושבי אזור שאינם אזרחים ישראליים, כאילו היו תושבי ישראל". s 36A is not named.
- R6 ("אזרח ישראלי" in s 3A): an Israeli citizen; an Israeli resident; one entitled to immigrate under the Law of Return who is an Area resident; a body of persons controlled by any of those. So the Order reaches an Area resident who is none of these.
- R7 (Regs reg 3(א)): caring-sector legal foreign worker: "יובאו בחשבון נקודות זיכוי לפי סעיפים 34, 36 ו־36א": 2 + 1/4, plus 1/2 if (and only because s 36A speaks of a woman) a woman. So man 2.25, woman 2.75.
- R8 (reg 3(א1)): other legal foreign worker: "תובא בחשבון נקודת זיכוי לפי סעיף 34": ONE point (not two).
- R9 (reg 3(ב)): "עובדת זרה חוקית שאינה ... בתחום הסיעוד": half a point under s 36A. So other legal man 1, woman 1.5.
- R10 (reg 3(ג)): part-year stay: one twelfth of the points of (א)-(ב) times the number of months stayed in the tax year.
- R11 (reg 3(ד)): Chapter Three "למעט האמור בתקנות אלה, לא יחולו" in computing the tax of any foreign worker (not only legal ones): so no s 35, 37, 38, 39, 40 etc. and nothing for a non-legal worker.
- R12 (regs 3(א), (א1), (ב)): credits are brought in "בחישוב המס על הכנסה לפי סעיף 2(1) או (2)": only for tax on income under s 2(1) (work) or 2(2) (business).
- R13 (reg 2): legal foreign worker: ALL of (1) stay and employment in Israel or the Area lawful; (2) not a "foreign expert" or "visiting lecturer"; (3) holds a visa and B/1 temporary-worker licence.
- R14 (reg 1): caring-sector legal FW: a legal FW who received a permit in the caring field and whose stay extension is under s 3A(b) of the Entry into Israel Law.
- R15 (reg 4): applies to income produced in tax year 2015 or after.
- R16 (s 48A): the regs apply "אף אם רואים אותו כתושב לענין פקודה זו": a foreign worker who is a tax resident still falls under the regs.

## Genuine ambiguities (flagged; expect refusal or a named reading)

- AMB-1 (s 36A for an Area-resident woman). The Order names 34, 36, 37 only. s 36A's words have no residence condition. Reading taken: the Order grants her nothing under 36A, so 2.25 (and 3.25 with s 37). Alternative: s 36A applies to her on its own words, 2.75 (3.75). Confidence L.
- AMB-2 (month counting under reg 3(ג)). "במספר החודשים שבהם שהה בארץ" does not say how a partial month counts. Reading taken: whole calendar months only, input as a count 1-12. A fraction of a month is a refusal. Confidence M.
- AMB-3 (Area resident who is also a foreign worker). An Area resident non-citizen is "עובד שאינו אזרח ישראל" under the Foreign Workers Law, but the regs' definition requires an entry visa under s 2 of the Entry into Israel Law (or one that is required), which Area workers ordinarily lack. Reading taken: if the person has an Area residence and no s 2 visa requirement, the Order governs; if both flags are set the answer is a refusal. Confidence L.
- AMB-4 (years 2015-2018). The deposited regulations are a consolidation that lists amendments of 5777 (KT 472) and 5778 (KT 2656) at reg 3. The amendments' commencement is not deposited. Reading taken: tax years 2015-2018 cannot be answered from the deposited text; expect refusal. Confidence L. Years 2019 and after: the consolidated text answers. Confidence M (the consolidation is unofficial).
- AMB-5 (Order's commencement). The Order (signed 1 March 1995) has no commencement clause. Reading taken: it applies to tax years 1995 and after (1995 flagged L); 1994 and before is a refusal.
- AMB-6 (money). The point value is in the text only as a note for 2023 and for 2024-2027. For any other year points are answerable, money is "needs the published figure" (s 120A index). Confidence H.
- AMB-7 (proration of the Order). The Order has no 1/12 rule; s 34 says "היה תושב ישראל בשנת המס". Reading taken: an Area resident gets the full points however many months, because the proration of reg 3(ג) is only for foreign workers. Confidence M.
