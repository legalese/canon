# Source license

The text of **Code of Virginia, Title 2.2 (Administration of Government), Chapter 39 - Virginia Human Rights Act** is published on the [Virginia Law Portal - Code of Virginia (Virginia General Assembly, Division of Legislative Automated Systems, for the Virginia Code Commission)](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/).

- **Terms**: The site footer reads '(c) Copyright Commonwealth of Virginia, <year>. All rights reserved. Site developed by the Division of Legislative Automated Systems (DLAS)', but the Code of Virginia pages themselves carry a narrower and more informative notice: the online database 'excludes material copyrighted by the publisher, Michie, a division of Matthew Bender', naming annotations and revisors' notes as the copyrighted material and directing users who want the annotated Code to LexisNexis or to West (Thomson Reuters). So the Commonwealth's own position is that the copyrighted layer is the commercial publisher's editorial apparatus, which the free online database does not contain. Under the US government edicts doctrine (Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org, 590 U.S. 255 (2020)) the text of the Code of Virginia as enacted is not copyrightable at all, so the footer's 'All rights reserved' cannot reach the enacted text: reuse AND adaptation of the statutory text are permitted. Reinforcing this, the register publishes the whole Code as downloadable PDF and CSV and exposes JSON/XML web services for developers, with no stated licence, fee, registration or permission requirement and no restriction on redistribution. The official annotated print Code is produced under contract by Michie/Matthew Bender (LexisNexis), so annotations, revisors' notes and the annotated arrangement must not be taken from a print or Lexis volume.

In the United States the text of a statute is not copyrightable: it is an edict of government (*Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org*, 590 U.S. 255 (2020)). A register's copyright notice can still cover a publisher's annotations, headnotes and numbering, which this corpus does not reproduce.
