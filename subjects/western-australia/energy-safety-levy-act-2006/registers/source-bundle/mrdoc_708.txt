Western Australia
Energy Safety Levy Act 2006
An Act to impose the energy safety levy determined under the Energy Safety Act 2006.


The Parliament of Western Australia enacts as follows:
1.	Short title
		This is the Energy Safety Levy Act 2006.
2.	Commencement
		This Act comes into operation on the day on which the Energy Safety Act 2006 comes into operation.
3.	Energy safety levy imposed
		If a levy is determined under the Energy Safety Act 2006 sections 14(1) and 16 in respect of an energy industry participant, that levy is imposed in respect of the energy industry participant.
