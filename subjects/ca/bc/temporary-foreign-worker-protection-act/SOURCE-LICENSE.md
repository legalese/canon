## Consequence for this subject

## Language

English only. BC statutes are enacted and published in English alone. BC has no constitutional bilingual-enactment requirement (unlike federal law, Quebec, Manitoba and New Brunswick), and BC Laws publishes only English text. One caveat: the consolidations on BC Laws are not the official version for legal purposes, as the licence's attribution wording itself states.
