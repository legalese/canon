## Why this Act, and why scoped

**5 of the 527 Singapore Acts** deposited here cite it. It is mostly an
institutional Act, so this row takes the rules that decide something: board size,
disqualification, quorum and voting (ss 10, 12, 19, 21, 31, 33); secrecy and
immunity (ss 44, 45); the Part 7 media competition and control rules (ss 59, 60B,
62-66); reconsideration and appeal (ss 67A, 68); and the offences, penalties and
composition (ss 9, 44, 65(8), 66(8), 74-77). Functions and powers, finance, codes
of practice and directions, separation orders, entry and search, legal privilege,
officers' liability and the transfer and transitional Parts are not encoded. The
interests that ss 59(4) and 60A(3) tell you to disregard (bare trustees, lenders,
underwriters, proxies, liquidators) are not modelled.

## What the Act turns out to say

### 1. A media regulated person cannot change hands at 30% without approval

s 65(1)-(3): without "the prior written approval of the Authority" no one may
become a "30% controller" (30% of equity interests or of voting power, counting
associates, s 59), buy a licensed business "as a going concern", or obtain
"effective control". Either threshold alone is enough; effective control needs no
percentage at all. A corporation is an associate where 12% of its voting power is
controlled (s 60B(g), (h)). Asserted.

### 2. The prescribed-transaction route swaps approval for a 7-day notice, and only trustees are punished for missing it

s 65(5): no approval is needed where the event happens "by virtue only of any
transaction prescribed". The regulated person (or its trustee-manager or trustee)
must then notify within 7 days of becoming aware (s 65(6)). But the offence in
s 65(8), $10,000, names only a trustee-manager or trustee, and s 65(9) excludes one
that is itself the regulated person. The deposit states no penalty for a regulated
person that is not a trustee and fails to notify. Asserted.

### 3. Financial penalties scale with turnover, with a $1 million floor on the cap

s 66(3): the maximum is "the higher of" 10% of the annual turnover of the part of
the business where the infringement occurred, or $1 million. Failing without
reasonable excuse to comply with a written order is an offence carrying $100,000
or 3 years (s 66(8)), far above the Act's other offences. Asserted.

### 4. Only agreements of a kind named in a code of practice are prohibited

s 62(2) bites only on an agreement, decision or concerted practice "of a nature
specified by the Authority in a code of practice" AND with an anti-competitive
object or effect. Dominance under s 63 is "in the opinion of the Authority". Both
can be exempted under s 64. Asserted.

### 5. A reconsideration request by anyone kills a Minister's appeal on the same decision

Both routes have 14-day deadlines (ss 67A(2), 68(3)(d)); the Minister may allow
longer for an appeal. If a reconsideration request is made, by the appellant
**or by any other person**, on the same decision, the appeal "is deemed to be
withdrawn" (s 67A(4), (5)). The decision must be complied with meanwhile, and the
Minister's decision is final (s 68(7), (8)). Asserted, except compliance pending
challenge and finality, which are noted but not encoded.

### 6. Silence at a board meeting is a vote in favour

s 33(4): a member present "is presumed to have agreed to, and to have cast a vote
in favour of" a decision unless he or she expressly dissents. The presiding member
has a casting vote on equality (s 33(2)). The quorum is the higher of one-third of
members or 3 (s 31), so a 20-member board needs 7. Asserted.

### 7. Composition is capped at $2,000

s 77(1): a prescribed compoundable offence may be compounded for at most the lower
of half the maximum fine or $2,000; for breach of secrecy (maximum $2,000, s 44(2))
that is $1,000. Which offences are compoundable is left to regulations, which were
not retrieved. Asserted.

## What would need doing before this is worth anything

- The regulations prescribing transactions under s 65(5), compoundable offences
  under s 77 and interest under s 79A were not retrieved.
- The codes of practice that give s 62 its content were not retrieved.
- The disregarded interests in ss 59(4) and 60A(3), and the full associate test in
  s 60B, are not modelled.
- Directions under s 65A, information powers under s 65B and separation orders
  under s 69A are not encoded.
- Day 14 is treated as within time for ss 67A(2) and 68(3)(d); that is a reading,
  not something the text states.
