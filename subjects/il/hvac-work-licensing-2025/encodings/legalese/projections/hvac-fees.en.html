Hvac Fees

Chapter A -- Definitions

reg. 1 Definitions

Definitions

  1. “Service” means one of:
    1. theoretical examination
    2. practical examination
    3. appeal against a theoretical examination score
    4. appeal against a practical examination score
    5. application for a licence
    6. receipt or renewal of a licence
    7. temporary licence for a foreign expert
  2. “Vintage” means one of:
    1. the SimpLEX draft
    2. the Regulations as made
    3. the Regulations as amended

Chapter B -- Fees

reg. 2 Fees -- the draft as shown in SimpLEX

  1. The fee prescribed by the draft for means the fee the SimpLEX draft prescribes for service, in new shekels, if any.

reg. 2 Fees -- as made, Kovetz HaTakanot 5785 p. 2116

  1. The fee prescribed by the Regulations as made for means the fee the Regulations as made in July 2025 prescribe for service, in new shekels, if any.

reg. 2 Fees -- as amended, Kovetz HaTakanot 5786 p. 2062

  1. The fee prescribed by the Regulations as amended for means the fee the Regulations as amended in 5786 prescribe for service, in new shekels, if any.
  2. The fee under means the fee under vintage for service.

reg. 2 -- what an appellant must tender in all

  1. The examination fee the appeal fee is added to under means the examination fee that vintage's appeal fee is charged in addition to.
  2. The total to be tendered under means the total to be tendered under vintage for service.
  3. The appeal fee plus its base under means the appeal fee under vintage for service, plus the examination fee it is charged in addition to.

reg. 3 Updating of fees

  1. Rounded to the nearest whole shekel means FLOOR with the sum of x and 0.5.
  2. The updated fee means the fee of fee shekels updated by the ratio of the index new index to the base index base index, rounded to the nearest shekel.

reg. 4 Payment of the fee as a condition of a licence or an examination

  1. The service may be provided holds if service may be provided under vintage to an applicant who has paid paid shekels.

reg. 5 Commencement

  1. The Regulations commence on means the Regulations commence seven days after their publication on published.
