# Encoding brief: ITO residence and sources (s 1, s 2(2)(b), s 2A, s 14(b)) with the residence and value Regulations, in L4

This brief is the specification for row IL-29 (run `IL-29-20261008`), written by the encoder `enc-il-29`.
The scope was pinned from the lead's task message and BACKLOG row IL-29 before any L4 was written; the coverage table in `NOTES.md` was finished once the sources had been read.

## The subjects

**Income Tax Ordinance [New Version]**, the provisions below, from the deposited Wikisource consolidation (unofficial; the Hebrew is authoritative):

- s 1 "תושב ישראל" (an Israeli resident): (a) for an individual (the centre of life, the day-count presumptions of paragraph (2), their rebuttal, the Minister's power in paragraph (4)), and (b) for a body of persons (incorporated in Israel; control and management exercised in Israel, with its exception);
- s 1 "תושב חוץ" (a foreign resident): not an Israeli resident, and also an individual meeting both limbs of the second part;
- s 1 "שנת מס" (a tax year);
- s 2(2)(b): the Minister fixes the value of the use of a vehicle or a mobile telephone put at an employee's disposal;
- s 2A: winnings from gambling, lotteries and prize-bearing activity;
- s 14(b): the adjustment year of a first-time resident or a veteran returning resident (and, in (b)(2)(b), its place in the ten years of s 1 (b)(2)).

**Regulations**, all deposited under `registers/source-bundle/regulations/`:

- the Income Tax Regulations (Determining Individuals Who Will Be Regarded as Israeli Residents and Individuals Who Will Not), 5766-2006 (RES): reg 2 deems some employees of the State and of listed bodies residents; reg 3 deems eight classes of individual foreign residents;
- the Income Tax (Value of Use of a Vehicle) Regulations, 5747-1987 (VEH);
- the Income Tax (Value of Use of a Vehicle) (Temporary Provision) Regulations, 5776-2015 (VTP);
- the Income Tax (Value of Use of a Mobile Radio Telephone) Regulations, 5762-2002 (TEL).

## What the row delivers

1. The residence status of an individual in a tax year, from the day counts and a finding of the centre of life, with the Regulations and s 14(b) applied; the same for a body of persons; and the foreign-resident status, including the second limb.
2. The monthly value of the use of a vehicle and of a mobile telephone, and the value for a tax year.
3. Whether s 2A reaches winnings, composed with the two statuses; the territorial charge of s 2 on both statuses.
4. What composing these into the capstone (row IL-07, later IL-55) needs, recorded in `NOTES.md` section 8, without editing IL-08 or the capstone.

The row closes the gaps recorded at IL-08 `NOTES.md` lines 130-131, 138, 172, 474, 526; IL-08 `INDEPENDENT-FINDINGS.md` lines 79, 269, 271; capstone `GAPS.md` line 27 and `NOTES.md` lines 371, 528.
