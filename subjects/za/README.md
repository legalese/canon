# south-africa/

South Africa legislation, one subject-id per body of law, following the subject-sidecar shape described in [`subjects/README.md`](../README.md).

The subjects here were laid out on 2026-09-22 for the legislation governing twelve everyday topics. Each was checked against the South African Government website (gov.za), Documents > Acts (run by GCIS); Department of Justice and Constitutional Development Acts list (justice.gov.za); the check is recorded in each subject's `registers/source-bundle/*.meta.json`. No statute text has been deposited and nothing is encoded yet.

**Licence category: unclear.** **Metadata only until resolved.** The register's website terms allow only non-commercial use. Section 12(8)(a) of the Copyright Act 98 of 1978 is generally read as excluding official legislative texts from copyright, but that has not been confirmed from an official source. Resolve before depositing or quoting text.

## Topics with no legislation here

- **Passenger compensation**: The only statutory coverage is carrier liability under the Carriage by Air Act, plus general Consumer Protection Act remedies. There is no dedicated statute for air, rail or bus passengers covering compensation for delay, cancellation or denied boarding. Any rules of that kind sit in civil aviation regulations or technical standards, not in primary legislation.
