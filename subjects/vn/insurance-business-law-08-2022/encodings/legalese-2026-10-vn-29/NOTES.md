## 1. What is encoded and what is not

**Scope: the whole Act.** All 157 articles of Law 08/2022/QH15 as made (V1) and the effect on them of all 25 numbered clauses of Law 139/2025/QH15 (V2, V3). Every unit of the three roadmaps has a disposition (section 2); none is `deferred`.

**Vintages.** `law08-vintage.l4` holds the three dates (V1 from 2023-01-01, V2 from 2026-01-01, V3 from 2026-07-01). Every rule that Law 139/2025/QH15 changed has one arm per vintage, selected on the rule-version axis (`RULES EFFECTIVE DATE`; a test pins it with `EVAL UNDER RULES EFFECTIVE AT`). A provision it did not touch has one arm. A provision repealed from V2 or V3 answers that it does not exist (`REFUSE`), never with the older vintage's answer. Where a question is "was it in force on day D" (the deferred commencements of Điều 156(2)) the day D is a second, explicit input. V1 is pinned in tests at 1 June 2025, V2 at 1 March 2026, V3 at 1 August 2026.

**Rule names.** Every rule that encodes a provision is named `Dieu N(...): ...` (Điều in ASCII), so `Dieu 87(3)` finds the encoding of Điều 87(3); the helpers under such a rule (for example `the founder meets limbs (b) and (c)`, `the risk is accepted against premium`) are not named that way. The text in the module is English; the Vietnamese appears only in the generated `-- src:` lines.

**Honest account of depth.** The Law is long and much of it is institutional. The encoding takes each article to the form the facts allow:

- *Definitions and tests of status* (Điều 4, 25, 34, 44, 64-67, 81, ...) are predicates over records of facts a witness could give (a record such as `An activity` has a Boolean per limb of the definition). Such a rule is a conjunction or disjunction of the Law's own limbs in the Law's own order; it adds no substance, and a reader should not take it for more than a check that the limbs are assembled as the text assembles them.
- *Arithmetic and deadlines* (Điều 27(4), 30, 31, 35, 37, 47-49, 51, 59, 93, 96, 98, 112, 113, 116) are computed; their expected values were worked by hand from the cited lines.
- *Lists of rights and duties* (Điều 20, 21, 128, 129, 137) are carried as enumerations or inert stubs, with the few conditional duties (e.g. Điều 20(2)(e): a refusal must be explained in writing) encoded as predicates.
- *Policy statements, definitions of titles, lists of State functions and duties of internal organisation* (Điều 2, 5, 10, 45, 78, 84-86, 102, 104, 108, 114, 118, 119, 121-124, 131, 135, 141) are inert, each with a reason in section 2.
- *Delegations.* Wherever an article that is encoded with rules hands a matter to the Government or the Minister of Finance, the encoding has a named definition that `REFUSE`s with a message, and a test that it does (`#ASSERT REFUSED`). Those delegations are: Điều 6(2), 7(2), 8(5), 11(5), 12(3), 14(3), 17(2), 69(6), 71(4), 74(4), 76(4), 77(2), 81(5)-(6), 83(4), 87(5), 87(6), 88(2), 89(1), 89(5), 93(3), 94(6), 95(4), 97(4), 99(4)-(5), 100(5), 101(3)-(4), 105(4), 106(3), 109(3), 115(3), 120(2), 128 (the cap on commission), 129(4), 130(3), 134(2) (V1), 134 (V2), 136(4), 139, 143(3)-(4), 145(3)-(4), 146(2), 148(3), 150(5), 156(4), 157(2), 157(5). The delegations inside articles carried as inert stubs are named in the stub only and have no refusal of their own: Điều 84(4), 85(4), 86(4), 89(3)-(4), 102(2), 137(1)(a) and (2)(c), 138(1) and (5), 152(1). Where an article states a figure but lets the Government fix another or a minimum (Điều 65, 64(2)(a), 67(2)(a), 133(2)(a), 138(3), 149(2), 98(2), 144(3)), the figure the Law states is applied and the Government's is an optional or a required input: a required one makes the rule refuse until it is supplied (fork F26, F38).
- *Other laws.* The Civil Code, the Maritime Code, the Law on Enterprises, the Law on Investment, the Bankruptcy Law, the Securities Law, the laws on accounting, associations, inspection, specialised examination and cybersecurity are never supplied. A reference to another law is an input flag (for example `the Maritime Code provides for the matter`) or a `REFUSE`, never encoded.
- *Not modelled.* Obligations between private parties are not simulated as regulative rules with `MUST ... WITHIN` (no `#TRACE`); deadlines are computed as dates from facts. The reason: nearly every deadline in the Law runs from an event the parties record, and the checks that matter are on the date, which a function gives directly. A reviewer who wants the deontic form can read each deadline function as the `WITHIN` of the obligation named in its title.

**Outside knowledge.** Outside knowledge is used nowhere to drive a rule. Statements that rest on it are marked "outside knowledge, unverified" (finding R8 only).

## 4. Findings

The hostile reading: the Law attacked as a lawyer for each side would. A finding is a defect in the instrument as written, not an ambiguity resolved (those are in section 3). Each has the source lines, a minimal scenario, and either evidence from the L4 (a section of `law08-tests-findings.l4`, which asserts the surprising answer) or "reading only".

Where I looked: internal contradictions between articles (W2, W3, W18, W21); defined terms that carry weight but are not defined or are used inconsistently (W16; the Law defines 'ngày' nowhere; 'hoạt động xây dựng' R10); deadlines that cannot all be met or run from events the Law does not fix (W11, W16, F17); time-bars shorter than the time allowed for another step (none found: Điều 30's one year is shorter than Điều 37(3)'s two years for restoration, but they concern different steps); discretion with no criteria (R7); a rule stated for one class and silent for another (W6, W9); amounts that do not close (W14, W15, R8); cross-references to an article that does not say what is cited (W3, R2); rules that apply 'by agreement' with no default (Điều 31(2) interest; Điều 48(2); Điều 51; Điều 59(4)); defects created by the amending Law (W1, W2, W4, W5, W11, W12, W13, W17, W19, R3, R4, R5, R6).

- **W1**. The replaced Điều 73 drops the bar on carrying on insurance business before the date of official operation.
  - Source lines: src:1377-1379; Law 139 Điều 1(3), src:a:40-68.
  - Minimal scenario: An insurer that trades before its opening date breaches Điều 73(4) in V1; in V2 and V3 no paragraph says so (Điều 9(1)-(2) still bar business without or outside a licence, which a licensed insurer has).
  - Evidence: law08-tests-findings.l4, section W1.
- **W2**. From 2028 to 2030 the V2 text has the capital adequacy ratio (Điều 95) in force but not the supervisory measures (Điều 109-114, 116), while old Điều 77-81, 83, 98 and the interim duty of Điều 156(4) run on.
  - Source lines: Điều 156(2), src:b:528-530; Law 139 Điều 1(14), src:a:292-310; Điều 91(1), src:1936-1937.
  - Minimal scenario: On 1 June 2029 the V1 text has the control measure in force and the V2 text does not; an insurer at the control level can be ordered nothing; Điều 91(1) lists a transfer required under Điều 113(8) as a case of portfolio transfer, which cannot arise.
  - Evidence: law08-tests-findings.l4, section W2.
- **W3**. Điều 153(1)(c) lets the Ministry ask banks about breaches of provisions not yet in force (Điều 95, 99(3)-(4), 109, 110).
  - Source lines: src:b:473-483 (file 2, lines 473-483); Điều 156(2).
  - Minimal scenario: On 1 June 2025 Điều 109 and 110 are not in force even in part, and under the V2 text they are not in force until 2031.
  - Evidence: law08-tests-findings.l4, section W3.
- **W4**. The repeal of Điều 155 from 1 July 2026 says nothing of the lines it added to the Investment Law's Appendix IV.
  - Source lines: src:b:516-522; Law 139 Điều 2(8), src:a:339.
  - Minimal scenario: Is insurance brokerage still a conditional investment line on 2 July 2026? The Law repeals the amending provision and does not say whether the amendment stays; V3 answers with silence.
  - Evidence: law08-tests-findings.l4, section W4.
- **W5**. V3 repeals Điều 143 (conditions for ancillary services) and strikes ancillary services from the bar in Điều 9(3), yet Điều 4(7), 63(1)(c), 131(2) and 140-142 still let insurers, brokers and others supply them.
  - Source lines: src:b:259-286; Law 139 Điều 2(6) and 2(8), src:a:333-339; Điều 63(1)(c), src:1147.
  - Minimal scenario: An adviser with no degree is outside Điều 143 in V2 (and fails it) but in V3 Điều 143 does not exist; an unqualified ancillary provider's act is prohibited in V2 and not in V3.
  - Evidence: law08-tests-findings.l4, section W5.
- **W6**. Điều 35 sits in the section on life and health contracts, but its words give every contract of more than a year a 21-day withdrawal.
  - Source lines: src:760-769; Mục 2 heading, src:737-739.
  - Minimal scenario: A ten-year property contract: the policyholder may refuse to go on on day 10 and recover the premium less reasonable costs.
  - Evidence: law08-tests-findings.l4, section W6.
- **W7**. Điều 26 gives either side the right to terminate on four grounds without saying which side may use which.
  - Source lines: src:623-635.
  - Minimal scenario: Read literally, a policyholder who has not paid is a side that 'may terminate' for the non-payment.
  - Evidence: law08-tests-findings.l4, section W7 (shows the reading the encoding takes).
- **W8**. Điều 58(1) reads as an occurrence rule or as a claims-made rule, with opposite answers.
  - Source lines: src:1083-1086.
  - Minimal scenario: A claim made after the term for an act within it: liability arises on one reading and not on the other; a claim within the term for an earlier act, the reverse.
  - Evidence: law08-tests-findings.l4, section W8.
- **W9**. Điều 148(2) names the general rules, life, health and property contracts of Chương II for a mutual organisation's contracts and is silent on damage and liability contracts, though Điều 15(1) lists damage contracts separately.
  - Source lines: src:b:358-361; Điều 15(1), src:370-377.
  - Minimal scenario: A mutual organisation's liability contract: no rule of Chương II is named.
  - Evidence: law08-tests-findings.l4, section W9.
- **W10**. Điều 65(3) lets the Government fix the minimum total assets 'for each period' on the basis of figures the Law itself states.
  - Source lines: src:1211-1228.
  - Minimal scenario: A Vietnamese company with 2,500 billion of assets passes the Law's 2,000 billion and fails a Government minimum of 3,000 billion; the Law does not say whether the Government may set it lower than the Law.
  - Evidence: law08-tests-findings.l4, section W10.
- **W11**. After Điều 69 is repealed (V2) the 60 days of Điều 70(1) still run from a 'hồ sơ hợp lệ' the Law no longer describes.
  - Source lines: src:1283-1305, 1307; Law 139 Điều 2(7), src:a:336-337.
  - Minimal scenario: The clock runs from receipt of a complete and valid dossier (an input) but the Law cannot say when a dossier is complete.
  - Evidence: law08-tests-findings.l4, section W11.
- **W12**. Điều 127(1) in V1 and V2 bars different pairs of principals.
  - Source lines: src:2735-2743; Law 139 Điều 1(9), src:a:224-233.
  - Minimal scenario: An individual who is an agent of a life insurer that also sells health insurance and of a health insurer: barred in V1, free in V2.
  - Evidence: law08-tests-findings.l4, section W12.
- **W13**. V2 drops 'the right to manage an enterprise under the Law on Enterprises' from the general conditions of an officer.
  - Source lines: src:1550; Law 139 Điều 1(5), src:a:79-85.
  - Minimal scenario: An officer without that right meets the general conditions in V2 and not in V1.
  - Evidence: law08-tests-findings.l4, section W13.
- **W14**. The deposit of Điều 96 is 2% of the MINIMUM charter capital at establishment, so it does not grow with the insurer.
  - Source lines: src:2031-2033.
  - Minimal scenario: An insurer that has grown tenfold deposits the same sum.
  - Evidence: law08-tests-findings.l4, section W14.
- **W15**. Điều 98 sets aside 5% of after-tax profit until a maximum the Government fixes; the Law gives no maximum, so the contribution cannot be computed from the Law alone.
  - Source lines: src:2074-2075.
  - Minimal scenario: A fund with no maximum supplied: the encoding refuses.
  - Evidence: law08-tests-findings.l4, section W15.
- **W16**. The Law counts 'ngày' without defining it and uses 'ngày làm việc' only in Điều 117.
  - Source lines: src:2559-2566 and passim.
  - Minimal scenario: Seven days from Monday 3 March 2025: Monday 10 March as calendar days, Wednesday 12 March as working days.
  - Evidence: law08-tests-findings.l4, section W16.
- **W17**. The V2 extension of agent certificates in Điều 157(2) restores a use the V1 text had ended.
  - Source lines: src:b:544-547; Law 139 Điều 1(15), src:a:312-317.
  - Minimal scenario: On 1 March 2026 an old agent certificate is usable under the V2 text and not under the V1 text.
  - Evidence: law08-tests-findings.l4, section W17.
- **W18**. Two rules for the same shortfall of assets on a transfer of portfolio: a pro rata refund (Điều 27(4)) and a negotiated reduction (Điều 92(3)).
  - Source lines: src:670-678, 1953-1960.
  - Minimal scenario: Assets at 80% of the reserve: the terminating policyholder gets 80% of the surrender value; a policyholder who stays must negotiate a reduction whose measure the Law does not give.

### 7.1 Source locators

Under each unit of `-- src:` quote lines that is followed by a declaration, the modules carry one `@ref` line that names the raw lines the unit quotes.
A unit is one quote run, or several runs separated only by blank lines and plain comments.
`src:A-B` is lines A to B of `law08-2022-qh15.txt`.
`src:ID:A-B` is lines A to B of `ID.txt` in `source/raw/`.
One line may carry several locators.
A real one, from `law08-ch1-general.l4`, under a run that quotes both the Law and the amending Law:

    @ref Dieu 6 (src:214-217; src:law139-2025-qh15:333-334)

The text outside the locators (`Dieu 6`) is the citation as a reader would write it.
It is generated from the raw text: it names every article that the unit's quoted lines lie in, by the `Điều N.` headings of the raw file, and nothing else.
A bare `Dieu N` is an article of Law 08/2022/QH15, which includes the second gazette issue (`law08-2022-qh15-577-578`).
A unit that quotes only the amending Law 139/2025/QH15 is labelled with that law's name, as in `Law 139/2025/QH15 Dieu 1`, and the article is Law 139's own.
A unit before the first `Điều N.` heading has no article and no label.
The label is written in ASCII like the rule names, so that Vietnamese appears only in the quote lines.
A locator does not reach over a raw line that its unit leaves out, other than a blank line, the gazette's running header or a page number: such a unit gets one locator for each stretch.
The quote lines are comments and are unchanged.
The `@ref` line is an L4 annotation, not a comment, and the parser reads it.
The lines were added by `tools/switch_to_ref_locators.py`, to the modules and to `templates/*.l4.in` alike so that `tools/expand.py` reproduces them.
The script only adds lines, and a second run changes nothing.
The `@ref` goes directly above the first declaration its unit supports, and never above a `§` / `§§` heading, a `#ASSERT` or the end of a file.
The pinned `l4` warns about an `@ref` at the end of a file and says nothing about one above a heading or a `#ASSERT`, so running the modules cannot find a misplaced one.
A unit that is followed by a heading, a directive or the end of a file therefore has no `@ref`, and the gate does not ask for one; the table below counts those runs by module.

The gate (it exits 1 on any problem):

`python3 -I tools/srcrefs.py check --strict ../../source/raw/law08-2022-qh15.txt *.l4`

`--strict` asks for more than `--refs`.
Each locator must be covered by the quote run above it, and not by a quote line elsewhere in the file.
Each run must be overlapped by a locator on an `@ref` in its own block, and not by a locator elsewhere in the file.
In each block, the locators must reach exactly the lowest and the highest raw line that the runs of the block quote.
This row's `@ref` lines are generated to meet it, so it is the command to run after any edit.

Loud failures, which the gate reports with file and line and exits 1 on:

- a quote line that is not a verbatim slice of the raw lines it names, or whose lines do not exist, or that quotes no text (rule R1);
- a locator whose first or last line no quote line covers, that runs past the end of the raw file, that names a raw file which does not exist, or that is malformed, including a range written with a dash other than `-` (R2);
- a quote run, followed by a declaration, that no locator overlaps, "orphan quotation" (R3);
- with `--strict`, a locator narrowed or shifted by a line, a locator that reaches the next run, and a run whose only overlapping locator is elsewhere in the file.

Silent failures, which exit 0 with a wrong or missing answer:

- running without `--refs` or `--strict`, which checks R1 only and does not say so in its summary line;
- running with `--refs` only, which accepts a locator from anywhere in the file, so that deleting an `@ref` whose run another `@ref` still overlaps, or one wide `@ref` at the end of a file, goes unnoticed;
- a locator that is wrong but lies inside the quoted lines;
- a label (`Dieu N`) that is wrong, because it lies outside the locators and nothing checks it (the switch script derives it from the raw headings, and `tools/srcrefs.py` never reads it);
- an `@ref` above the wrong declaration, or above a heading, because the gate checks that a locator overlaps a run and not where the `@ref` sits relative to the declaration;
- a quote run that skips lines inside its locator, which the gate allows because a footer is often left out of a quotation;
- a quote that is a verbatim slice of the wrong raw file;
- a run followed by a heading, a directive or the end of a file that should have had an `@ref`, which the gate exempts.
