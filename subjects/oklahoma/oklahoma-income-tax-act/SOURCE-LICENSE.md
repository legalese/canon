# Source license

The text of **Oklahoma Income Tax Act** is published on the [Oklahoma Legislature - Oklahoma Statutes (Oklahoma State Legislature, oklegislature.gov)](https://www.oklegislature.gov/OSSTATUESTITLE.HTML).

- **Terms**: The register asserts nothing. No copyright notice, terms of use, disclaimer or reuse restriction appears anywhere on the Oklahoma Legislature site: the titles index, the homepage and the statute search page were each read in full and contain no 'copyright' string and no (c) symbol, and there is no terms or privacy page (/terms.aspx, /privacy.aspx and /about.aspx all redirect away). Under the government edicts doctrine confirmed in Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org, 590 U.S. 255 (2020), the text, numbering and section headings of the Oklahoma Statutes are not subject to copyright in any event, so reuse and adaptation are lawful. The Legislature publishes these files itself rather than through a commercial publisher, although a separately copyrighted annotated edition (Oklahoma Statutes Annotated, Thomson Reuters/West) exists whose annotations, notes and index are not part of this material. Treated as open: reuse and adaptation permitted. Caveat for a human: because no terms page exists, the finding is an absence of any assertion rather than an affirmative grant.
- **Terms checked**: 2026-09-23, at https://www.oklegislature.gov/OSSTATUESTITLE.HTML
- **From the terms**: "The Oklahoma Constitution and Oklahoma Statutes were last updated on November 18th, 2025."
- **Reuse and adaptation permitted, per the index check**: yes
- **Attribution required**: none required and none offered by the register; good practice is to cite 'Oklahoma Statutes' with the title and section and the register's currency date
- **Source**: https://www.oklegislature.gov/OK_Statutes/CompleteTitles/os68.pdf

In the United States the text of a statute is not copyrightable: it is an edict of government (*Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org*, 590 U.S. 255 (2020)). A register's copyright notice can still cover a publisher's annotations, headnotes and numbering, which this corpus does not reproduce.
