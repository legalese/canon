## The child-allowance basic amounts and the s 1 updating clause

The printed amounts are 150, 188 and 140 NIS, "נקוב לשנת 2015".
Slot (2)(א) is the first and fifth-and-later child; (2)(ב) the second, third and fourth; (2)(ג) the amount for Income Support and the s 68(ג) supplement.
The clause (3) says: from 2015, on 1 January each year, "לפי שיעור עליית המדד שפורסם לאחרונה לפני אותו יום לעומת המדד שפורסם לאחרונה לפני יום 1 בינואר של השנה הקודמת", except that on 1 January 2025 the amounts are not updated and stay as on 1 January 2024.
The index published last before 1 January of year Y is the November Y-1 index (the CPI is published on the 15th of the following month, so December's is published in mid-January).
Ratios from the CBS file, November index over the previous November, linked to a common base: 2015: 0.99902; 2016: 0.99103; 2017: 0.99698; 2018: 1.00300; 2019: 1.01196; 2020: 1.00303; 2021: 0.99405; 2022: 1.02402; 2023: 1.05279; 2024: 1.03344; 2025: 1.03425 (not applied, frozen); 2026: 1.02361.
Decision on an index fall: the clause says "שיעור עליית המדד" (rate of the index's rise).
A fall is not a rise, so in a year the November index fell, the amount is left unchanged and does not go down (M; the alternative, a downward update, gives 167/210/156 for 2024 and contradicts the Law's own printed 169/214/158).
Decision on rounding inside the chain: the text updates "הסכום הבסיסי" without saying each year's figure is rounded; the printed notes 169/214/158 and 173/219/162 are reproduced only if each year's figure is rounded half-up to a whole shekel before the next update (M; the unrounded chain gives 170.14/213.24/158.80 for 2024 and 174.16/218.28/162.55 for 2026).
