## F. Grounds, limb by limb
s 5(2): (a) kept<14d, (b) resident<14d, (c) reg 9 exempt class -> s 5(1) does not apply. None -> 6-month-old unregistered cat contravenes.
reg 9(2): custody of (a) Cat Haven, (b) RSPCA, (c) Commonwealth biosecurity dept, (d) CMF, (e) vet premises -> exempt. reg 9(3)(a) foster by Cat Haven/RSPCA -> exempt; (b) SAFE entity <=12 weeks -> exempt.
s 9(2): each of (a) child, (b) exempt class, (c) not microchipped, (d) not sterilised, (e) 2+ convictions in 3 y -> MUST refuse. None -> must grant (must not refuse).
s 9(3): not microchipped but vet cert under s 14(2) -> not refuse. s 9(4): not sterilised but exempt under s 18(2) (vet cert; or owned by approved breeder for breeding) -> not refuse.
s 9(6): non-compliance with s 9(5) request -> may refuse to consider.
s 10: (a)(i) died, (ii) no longer kept in State, (iii) registered with another LG, (b) 2+ convictions in 12 months -> MAY cancel. None -> cannot cancel.
s 14(2)/(3): vet cert exempts from microchipping, but not for cat under 6 months.
s 18(2): (a) vet cert (not under 6 months, s 18(3)); (b) owned by approved breeder for breeding; (c) prescribed class (none prescribed) -> exempt.
s 23(1): transfer of unmicrochipped cat contravenes unless satisfied s 14(2) cert applies.
s 23(2): unsterilised transfer contravenes unless (a)(i) cert, (a)(ii) purchaser approved breeder for breeding, (a)(iii) prescribed class (none), or (b) voucher given.
reg 19: transfer to org/person in reg 9 (Cat Haven, RSPCA, CMF, vet premises, Biosecurity dept, SAFE entity) -> s 23(1),(2) don't apply.
s 34(1): (a) unidentified after 3 working days, (b) notified owner not reclaimed after later-of, (c) surrendered -> may transfer or destroy.
s 34(2)(a): feral/diseased/dangerous AND caused/likely serious injury -> may destroy immediately; only one of the two limbs -> not.
s 37(2): (a) child, (b) insufficient facilities, (c) unsuitable premises, (d) conviction 3y, (e) not fit and proper, (f) reg 22 -> MAY refuse ("only if"); none -> may not refuse.
s 37(5)/reg 23: 18+, no conviction in 3 y, current member of one of 4 orgs -> must not refuse even if (b)/(c)/(e) apply. Missing membership -> may refuse.
reg 22(1): infringement notice issued and paid in 12 months -> may refuse; reg 22(2) unresolved -> may defer.
s 6: registered cat in public without tag contravenes; reg 10 exhibited at listed show -> exempt; s 6(3) defence needs (a) cause and (b) precautions.
s 4: unregistered cat kept by 16-year-old -> owner is parent/guardian.
s 72: objection to refusal to RENEW registration -> suspended; refusal to GRANT -> not suspended.
s 30(2): need not scan if aggressive AND reasonable belief of danger.
