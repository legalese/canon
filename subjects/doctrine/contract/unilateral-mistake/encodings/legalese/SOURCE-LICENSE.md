# Source terms for this encoding row

This row quotes from four kinds of source, and they do not carry the same terms. Each is set out
separately because a single blanket statement would be wrong about at least three of them.

## 1. Singapore judgments — UNDETERMINED

*Chwee Kin Keong and others v Digilandmall.com Pte Ltd* [2005] SGCA 2 and *Quoine Pte Ltd v B2C2
Ltd* [2020] SGCA(I) 02, retrieved from eLitigation (`https://www.elitigation.sg/`), published by
the Singapore Judiciary.

**The applicable terms are UNDETERMINED**, on the same open question that `sg/pdpa-2012` and
`sg/penal-code-1871` record. No grant has been established and none is invented here. No
attribution is recorded in `NOTICE` because none has yet been determined to be required — not
because none is required.

**This row also VENDORS the captured pages**, under `source/` at the subject level — 948KB, the
two eLitigation pages plus tag-stripped text. That goes further than quoting: it reproduces the
judgments in full. It was done so that the sha256 digests in `registers/source-bundle.json` and
the 51-quotation check in `report/verify-quotations.py` have bytes to work on; without them
neither claim in this row can be verified by anyone but its author. See `NOTES.md` §2.

This row quotes the judgments **extensively and verbatim**: the doctrinal passages are reproduced
in the module comments, because an encoding of a judge-made rule that paraphrased the judgment
could not be checked against it. That places this row closer to `sg/penal-code-1871` (verbatim)
than to `sg/pdpa-2012` (cites and paraphrases), and it is a material fact for whatever assessment
eventually settles the question.

## 2. English and Australian judgments — quoted at second hand

*Hartog v Colin & Shields* [1939] 3 All ER 566, *Smith v Hughes* (1871) LR 6 QB 597, *Riverlate
Properties Ltd v Paul* [1975] Ch 133, *Great Peace Shipping Ltd v Tsavliris Salvage
(International) Ltd* [2003] QB 679 and *Taylor v Johnson* (1983) 151 CLR 422 are **not quoted from
their own reports**, which were not retrieved. Every quotation of them in this row is a quotation
of *Chwee Kin Keong*'s account of them, and is marked as such at the point of use.

The terms that matter for those passages are therefore the Singapore ones in §1 above. The
underlying reports carry their own publishers' terms, which this row does not engage because it
does not reproduce them.

## 3. Restatement (Second) of Contracts §§ 153 and 154 — ALI copyright, quoted from a judgment

The Restatement (Second) of Contracts is a copyrighted work of the American Law Institute. **Its
text is not reproduced here from the Institute's publication.** Sections 153 and 154 are quoted as
they appear in *Donovan v RRL Corp* (2001) 26 Cal 4th 261, 27 P 3d 702, an opinion of the Supreme
Court of California.

Opinions of United States courts are not subject to copyright, and quotation of a judicial opinion
including the material it quotes is the ordinary practice of legal writing. This is stated so that
a reader can see the route the text took rather than assume a licence from the Institute that has
not been sought.
