# Source license

The text of the **Encouragement of Aliyah and Return (Temporary Provision) Law, 5786-2026** (חוק עידוד עלייה לישראל וחזרה אליה (הוראת שעה), התשפ״ו–2026) is Israeli primary legislation.

- **Copyright**: none. Under the Israeli Copyright Act, 5768-2007, s 6, there is no copyright in
  statutes, regulations, Knesset records or judicial decisions.
- **Official version**: the publication in Reshumot, Sefer HaChukim 3511 (31 March 2026), pp. 416-418, where the Law is
  chapter D of the Economic Efficiency Law (Legislative Amendments for Achieving the Budget Targets for Budget Year 2026),
  5786-2026, enacted by its section 9. The Law has no separate publication: its sections are quoted in that section.
- **Working text**: the Knesset's own PDF of that issue, `25_lsr_12235101.pdf`, held (with its sha256) in the neighbouring subject
  `../income-tax-ordinance-new-version/registers/source-bundle/amending-laws/`; this subject deposits a transcription of PDF pp. 4-6
  and the raw pdftotext extraction (`registers/source-bundle/`). The transcription is a working text checked against the PDF, **not an official copy**.
- **Attribution**: none required for the statute text.

## Consequence for this subject

Statute text may be deposited and quoted in an encoding. Record which text was used and its date: the encoding quotes the transcription
of Sefer HaChukim 3511, as first published, with no later amendment (none is in the sources).

## Language

Hebrew is authoritative; English translations are unofficial. The English title above is a translation by the encoder from the Hebrew
(chapter D, s 8: "חוק עידוד עלייה לישראל וחזרה אליה (הוראת שעה), התשפ״ו–2026"); no enacted English title is in the sources.
