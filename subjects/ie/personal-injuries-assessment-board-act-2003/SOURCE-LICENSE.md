## Language

English is not the only authoritative language. Under Article 25.4 of the Constitution (text checked on eISB at /eli/cons/en/html), where the President signs a Bill in only one official language, an official translation is issued in the other. Where both texts are enrolled and conflict, 'the text in the national language shall prevail'; the national language is Irish. In practice, almost all modern Acts, including every one listed here, are signed in English, which makes English the authoritative signed text and the Irish version an official translation. Irish-language versions exist, or are due, and some older Acts were signed in Irish, in which case the Irish text is authoritative.
