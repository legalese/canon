## 1. What is encoded and what is not

**The whole document is in scope and every provision is accounted for** (section 2): the insuring clause with its basis of payment and proviso, Exclusions A1-A4 and B1-B5, the Under-insurance and deductible clauses, and General Conditions 1-13.

The encoding answers three questions.

- **Is a piece of property's Loss covered?** `the Loss to … is covered, in … under …` (in `uic-par-claim.l4`): the defined term, Exclusions A and B, General Conditions 2 and 8, and when the cover was running.
  `the exclusions that apply to` names the limbs that bite.
- **What does the Policy pay for one Loss?** `the amount the Policy pays for … under …`: per item the basis of payment, B4, Under-insurance, General Condition 6 and proviso (i); then the total, proviso (i) on the total sum insured, proviso (ii) on the limit, and the deductible last.
- **What does the Insurer owe, given the claim's history, on a given day?** `the amount the Insurer owes, as at …`: forfeiture (General Conditions 4(a) and 10), the time limits (4(b) and 12), and a named refusal for each broken term whose consequence the wording does not state.

The duties with a party and an act (General Conditions 3, 5, 7, 9, 10, 11 and 13, and the undertaking not to insure the deductible) are also regulative rules, exercised by `#TRACE` in the tests.

**What is an input, never a default.** Everything the wording leaves to the Schedule: the Period of Insurance, the items and their sums insured, the limit of liability, the deductible, the premium, the premises, and which property is "specially stated" as insured.
Every assessed figure: the value of property at the time of the Loss, the amount of the damage, what other policies would have paid, the cost of reinstatement.
Every finding of fact: the causal chain, the circumstances of the property, whether the occurrence was physical, accidental, sudden and unforeseen, whether each claim step was taken "at once".

**What is not encoded.** Nothing in the document is out of scope.
The eight page footers ("Bảo hiểm Mọi rủi ro Tài sản (PAR)" and the page number) are inert.
The Law on Insurance Business 08/2022/QH15 was read as an aid and is cited in the fork register as `LAW` forks; it is not encoded, and no conflict between it and the wording is resolved in the L4.

**Where the encoding declines** (named `REFUSE`s): a Loss on the day the first premium was paid (time of day unknown); the rateable proportion of General Condition 6 where other insurance is in force and an amount remains; the short-period refund to an Insured who cancels; who bears the burden of proof outside Exclusion A3's two clauses; and the effect of breaking General Conditions 5, 9 or 13 or the deductible undertaking.
