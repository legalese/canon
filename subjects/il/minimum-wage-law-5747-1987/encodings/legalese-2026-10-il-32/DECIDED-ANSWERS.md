# DECIDED-ANSWERS: Minimum Wage Law 5747-1987 (IL-32), written from the Hebrew source alone

Source: registers/source-bundle/minimum-wage-law-5747-1987.he.wiki.txt (Wikisource consolidation, line numbers below are that file's).
Notation: M = the monthly minimum in force; H/M/L = confidence.
All money in new shekels (ש״ח).
Exact values are given as exact decimals or fractions; the Law states no rounding rule (see A1).

## Standing readings and ambiguities (flagged before the cases)

A1. Rounding.
The text never says how daily and hourly amounts are rounded (lines 24, 25).
I decide: the exact rational value is the answer; any published figure is a rounded form of it.
Alternative: round to the agora (2 dp). Differences are below 0.005 shekel.

A2. The 47.5% reading of s 1 (line 23).
The Hebrew is: "שכר מינימום, לחודש – 47.5 אחוזים מהשכר הממוצע כפי שהוא ב־1 באפריל של כל שנה וכפי שהוא מוגדל על פי חוק זה".
So the text does support: M(year) = 0.475 x average wage on 1 April of that year (average wage as defined in s 1 of the National Insurance Law), "as increased under this Law".
Three qualifications, none of which the encoding may drop:
 (i) The figure is the average wage ON 1 April; the average wage itself is defined in another Law (line 12) and is not in this text, so it is an input.
 (ii) "וכפי שהוא מוגדל על פי חוק זה" attaches to the average-wage-or-minimum compound; the Hebrew grammar lets "כפי שהוא מוגדל" modify the minimum (as increased by s 4), which is how the Ministry reads it. The text does not decide whether s 4 increases are applied on top of the 47.5% figure or replace it. AMBIGUITY.
 (iii) s 5 (line 50): the minimum may never fall because of updating. So M(year) = max(0.475 x AW(1 April of year), M(previous)). The bare 47.5% rule alone is NOT the answer when the average wage falls.
The consolidation also shows the 47.5% definition as amended many times (line 9 lists 8 amendments); for any date before the last amendment the consolidated text does not tell what the definition then said. So 47.5% for dates before 2018 is NOT supported by this text. Cases before 2018 other than s 21 need a published figure.

A3. Which minimum applies: monthly, daily or hourly (line 28: "הכל לפי הענין", as the case may be).
I decide: it follows the basis on which the worker's pay is fixed (monthly-paid vs daily-paid vs hourly-paid).
Workers paid on none of the three bases, or partly on one and partly on another, are delegated to regulations (s 18(b)(1),(2), lines 158-159): expect refusal.

A4. Hourly divisor.
The deposited text says 1/186 of M (line 25).
Risk of source currency: I recall the official hourly rates (for example 30.61 for April 2023) match a divisor of 182, not 186. I could not verify that from the source, so I decide by the deposited Hebrew (186) and flag every hourly case at confidence M. If the encoder used 182 the disagreement is a SOURCE-CURRENCY ambiguity, not a plain error. 182-based values are given as the alternative.

A5. Age 18 (line 28: "שמלאו לו 18 שנים").
Completed 18 years. A worker is covered from the day of the 18th birthday. Day before: not covered by this Law, falls under s 16 regulations (not deposited): refusal. Leap-day birthdays: the text does not decide (AMBIGUITY).

A6. Absence (s 2(c), line 30). "בשיעור יחסי לזמן היעדרו": reduce in proportion to the time absent. The text does not say the denominator (calendar days, working days, hours). I decide: proportion of the working days (or hours) customary at the workplace in the month. Alternative: calendar days. Where the absence is paid by law, contract, collective agreement or fund rules, the payment follows that arrangement, not the minimum.

A7. Pay that counts (s 3, lines 33-40). Exclusions are introduced by "ואולם" and override the inclusions where a payment fits both.

A8. "Full position as customary at the workplace" (line 28). The workplace's own full-time norm is an input; the Law gives no hours.

## Cases

Part A: the monthly minimum (s 1, s 21, s 5)

1. Commencement day 1 April 1987: M = 525. (s 21(a)(1), line 175) H.
2. 31 March 1987: the Law is not yet in force (s 20, line 171): no entitlement under this Law. H (expect "not in force", not a figure).
3. 30 September 1987: M = 525 (s 21(a)(1) until 1 Oct), subject to a s 4 increase published (line 57 notes one for 5748; its figure is not in the text). M.
4. 1 October 1987: M = 551 (s 21(a)(2), line 176), "not detracting from s 4" so a published s 4 increase beginning that day or later is on top; the figure is not in the text. M.
5. Between 2 Oct 1987 and the s 1 definition's first application: the sources give no figure. Needs the published figure. Expect refusal. H.
6. 1 April 2023: M = 5,571.75 (the editorial note on line 23; consistent with 0.475 x 11,730 = 5,571.75). Confidence M because the note is not statutory text; the AW 11,730 is derived backwards.
7. 1 April 2025 with AW = 13,566: 0.475 x 13,566 = 6,443.85. M (AW is an input; text supports the 47.5% step, see A2).
8. 1 April 2026: needs the published average wage for 1 April 2026; the Law's text cannot give it. Refusal if no AW supplied. H.
9. Given AW = 12,000 on 1 April of a year with no previous M: 0.475 x 12,000 = 5,700. M.
10. s 5, AW falls: previous M = 6,443.85; AW(next 1 April) = 13,000 gives 6,175; M stays 6,443.85. M (A2(iii)).
11. s 5, AW rises: previous M = 5,571.75; AW = 13,566 gives 6,443.85; M = 6,443.85. H.
12. s 5 boundary: the formula figure equals the previous M exactly: M unchanged (no reduction, no change). H.
13. s 4: M = 5,571.75 and a general collective agreement raises pay by 2% for most covered workers from a date: M from that date = 5,683.185 (5,571.75 x 1.02). L (compounding on M is my reading; the text says increased "by the rates", A2(ii)).
14. s 4 increase is 0%: unchanged. H.
15. s 4: increase date is the date the supplements begin (line 47), not the date of publication. M.
16. s 6 (line 53): the minister publishes the updated M as of 1 April; publication is the notice, not a precondition in the text for the 47.5% figure being the legal one. Not a computation; do not expect an input for "published". M.

Part C: who is covered and how (s 2, s 16, s 17)

32. Age 18, full position, monthly pay below M: entitled to M. H.
33. Age 17 (day before 18th birthday): not under s 2; falls under s 16 regulations, which are not deposited: refusal. H.
34. Age 18 on the day of the 18th birthday: covered. M (A5).
35. Age 40: covered. H.
36. Age 16 or 15 apprentice: the Law's text has no apprentice rule; s 16 delegates: refusal. H.
37. Worker with a disability in a protected workshop: s 17(a), by regulations only: refusal. H.
38. Worker with a disability, not in a protected workshop: s 17(b) regulations, 2002 adapted-wage regulations referred to in a note but not deposited: refusal. H.
39. Full position, M = 6,443.85, pay 6,443.85 (all counted): exactly meets the minimum, no shortfall. H.
40. Full position, M = 6,443.85, pay 6,443.84: shortfall 0.01. H.
41. Full position, pay 7,000: no shortfall. H.
42. Part position 50% of the workplace's full position, M = 6,443.85: entitlement 3,221.925 (s 2(b), line 29). H.
43. Part position 30 hours where full position is 42 hours, M = 6,443.85: 6,443.85 x 30/42 = 4,602.75. M (A8: proportion of position, hours as the measure).
44. Part position 100% (works full): same as full. H.
45. Part position 0%: entitlement 0. H.
46. Employer is the State: same rules (s 13, line 111). H.
47. A contract or waiver setting pay below M: no effect, the worker is still entitled to M (s 12, line 108). H.
48. Worker already above M under a contract: this Law adds to, does not reduce, other rights (s 11). H.

Part D: absence (s 2(c))

49. Full month, 0 days absent: M. H.
50. Whole month absent, no entitlement to pay for the absence: 0. M (A6).
51. Half the customary working days absent, unpaid: M/2. M.
52. Absent 2 of 22 customary working days, unpaid: M x 20/22. M (A6; calendar-day reading gives a different answer: AMBIGUITY).
53. Absent, but entitled to pay for the absence under law, contract, collective agreement or fund rules: the payment is the one the arrangement gives, not reduced proportionally (line 30). H.
54. Absent with paid sick leave at 100% under the contract: that pay, not the proportional minimum. M (details of sick pay are not in this Law).

Part E: which pay counts (s 3)

Baseline for 55-66: M = 6,443.85, full position, monthly.

55. Base wage 6,443.85 only: counts in full, meets M. H.
56. Combined wage (שכר משולב) 6,500: counts. H.
57. Cost-of-living allowance (תוספת יוקר) separate from a combined wage: counts. H.
58. Cost-of-living allowance already inside the combined wage: counted once, not twice (s 3(b)(2) "if it is not included in the combined wage", line 36). H.
59. Fixed supplement paid because of the work (תוספת קבועה): counts. M (boundary with excluded "fixed premium" is label-driven).
60. Family allowance: excluded. H.
61. Seniority supplement: excluded. H.
62. Shift-work supplement: excluded. H.
63. Measured, agreed, fixed or group premium (פרמיה): excluded, including a FIXED premium. H.
64. Thirteenth salary: excluded. H.
65. Annual bonus: excluded. H.
66. Expense refunds, meals, per diem (אש״ל), travel: excluded. H.
67. Base 5,000 + COLA 500 (separate) + fixed work supplement 600 + seniority 400 + 13th salary share 300 + travel 300: counted = 5,000 + 500 + 600 = 6,100; shortfall against 6,443.85 = 343.85. M.
68. Same but all of the 5,000 is a combined wage that includes the COLA: counted = 5,000 + 600 = 5,600; shortfall 843.85. M.
69. Overtime pay is not listed in s 3(b) and s 3(a) speaks of pay for a regular working day: not counted. M.
70. Employer pension or provident-fund contributions: not a listed component: not counted. M.
71. s 3(d): pay is wholly by commission (no base or COLA components): "the regular work wage without supplements" is the test; supplements named in the exclusion list are removed. L; the Hebrew leaves what "regular wage" means open (AMBIGUITY).
72. A payment that is both an item in (b)(3) and an excluded type (fixed premium): the exclusion governs ("ואולם"). M.

Part F: delegation and out-of-scope (expect refusals)

73. Worker paid piece-rate / not on monthly, daily or hourly basis: s 18(b)(1), regulations not deposited: refusal. H.
74. Worker paid partly by month and partly otherwise: s 18(b)(2): refusal. H.
75. Supplementary daily or hourly computation rules (1998 regulations): s 18(c): refusal for any case that depends on them. H.
76. Youth rates / reduced rates: s 16(b): refusal. H.
77. Penalty amount for non-payment: s 14 says "imprisonment one year or the fine in s 61(a)(4) of the Penal Law": the fine amount is not in this text. Refusal on the amount; one year imprisonment is stated. H.
78. Penalty for an actual employer: six months or half the s 14 fine, only if a para of s 6A(a) is met (s 14A(a)). Amount: refusal; the imprisonment term and the condition are stated. H.

Part G: outer provisions (likely SCOPE for the encoding; recorded so the finding is classified)

79. Actual employer liability s 6A(a)(1): worker demanded in writing, notified the actual employer in writing, M unpaid at the end of 21 days from the notice: right arises against the actual employer. The day-count (is day 21 included) is not decided by the text. M.
80. Labour inspector notice, then 21 days: s 6A(a)(2). M.
81. Suit against both: right after 21 days from delivery to the actual employer (s 6A(a)(3)). M.
82. s 7B presumption: no attendance record produced when required: employer presumed not to have paid M unless proved otherwise. H.
83. s 7B(3): payslip lacks the hourly rate: the presumption applies only if both (1) and (2) hold, and not for a worker on a collective total wage approved under s 5 of the Wage Protection Law. H.
