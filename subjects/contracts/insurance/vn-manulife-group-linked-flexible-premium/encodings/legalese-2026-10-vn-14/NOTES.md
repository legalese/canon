## 4. Findings

Defects in the instrument as written. Evidence is an assertion in `vn14-findings.l4` (or the named test module) that states the surprising answer and passes, or the words **reading only**.

**X1. A birthday or a business trip ends the cover (28.4, 11.2 with 1.4).** src:37-47, 610-620, 1433-1437.
1.4 lists conditions met "vào thời điểm yêu cầu bảo hiểm": an age of 1 month to 65, presence in Vietnam. 28.4 ends a part "ngay" (immediately) when the member "no longer meets" 1.4. Read as continuing (F24), a member who turns 66, or who is abroad on the day it is tested, has lost cover, whatever Maturity Date the Certificate shows; read literally, the 28.4 and 11.2 triggers can never fire. Scenario: a member born 1 March 1960, tested 2 March 2026; a member of 40 abroad on business. Evidence: `vn14-findings.l4` § X1, both TRUE.

**X2. Temporary cover can pay a covered death less than an excluded one (3.1, 3.3).** src:270-289, 308-319.
A covered accidental death pays the lesser of the pending Sum Insured and 200,000,000 and "sẽ không hoàn lại số phí bảo hiểm đã đóng"; a suicide refunds the premium less costs. Scenario: pending Sum Insured 100,000,000, premium paid 150,000,000, costs 2,000,000: the accident pays 100,000,000, the suicide 148,000,000. Evidence: § X2. Also: for a death in the period that is not by Accident, Article 3 says nothing about the premium already paid (`Article 3 — the premium refunded` refuses, `vn14-tests-ch1.l4`).

**X3. The temporary benefit is payable to Beneficiaries the group product does not name (3.1).** src:280-281, 52-64, 502-504. It is paid to "Người Thụ Hưởng có tên trong hồ sơ yêu cầu bảo hiểm"; but the Application is the Policyholder's (2.2) and Beneficiaries are named in the Enrolment Form (1.6, 9.1). Reading only.

**X4. No temporary cover for a member added after issue (3.1 with 11.1).** src:290-295, 598-609. The period ends "vào Ngày Cấp Hợp Đồng", which for a member added later is already past: the period is empty. Evidence: § X4.

**X5. Article 5 covers two kinds of breach and no others.** src:385-414, 487-492. 5.2 needs a deliberate breach the Company would have declined; 5.3 a breach that did not affect the decision. An honest but material mistake, or a deliberate one that would only have raised the premium (the case 7.2 names), has no consequence in the document. Evidence: § X5, both `REFUSED`.

**X6. 6.2 deducts withdrawals twice.** src:433-440. The refund is the greater of the Account Value and the premium paid, "trừ đi … các khoản Rút Giá Trị Tài Khoản". The Account Value is already net of withdrawals (24.3). Scenario: 10,000,000 withdrawn, Account Value 100,000,000 after it, premium 80,000,000: refund 90,000,000, not 100,000,000. Evidence: § X6.

**X7. Death during account suspension pays nothing, not even the member's own account (20.2(iv)).** src:1072-1077, 955-957, 984-988. While suspended, "Tất cả quyền lợi còn lại … (bao gồm cả quyền lợi tử vong và quyền lợi chấm dứt …) sẽ không được thực hiện". The part ends on death (28.2); 17.3 returns the Policyholder's account to the Policyholder; nothing returns the Insured Member's account, over which 17.1 gives the member "toàn quyền". Evidence: § X7 (`no benefit: the part was suspended under Article 20`).

**X8. Two rules for one early payment (16.2.1 against 16.2.3).** src:821-825, 914-928. With nothing overdue, 16.2.1 makes the whole payment Top-up Premium; 16.2.3 applies it first to the next instalment. Scenario: 25,000,000 paid 10 days before the due date. Evidence: § X8.

**X9. Article 15: an unfinished list, the employer's crime, and no payee.** src:781-798. The list ends "Hưởng; hoặc" with no item after it. Exclusion (b) includes criminal acts of the Policyholder, an organisation (1.2), so an employer's crime directly related to an employee's death defeats the employee's death benefit, and one Beneficiary's crime defeats all (compare F32). The Account Value paid instead goes to no named person. Evidence: § X9.

**X10. No floor where deductions exceed the amount (3.3, 6.2, 10.2, 15).** src:308-313, 436-440, 570-572, 793-798. Only 5.2 says the Company will not claw back (src:395-398). Evidence: § X10 and the `REFUSED` tests in `vn14-tests-ch1.l4`, `vn14-tests-ch2.l4`.
