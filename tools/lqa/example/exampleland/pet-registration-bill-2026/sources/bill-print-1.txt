Pet Registration Bill 2026 (Exampleland), print 1, as introduced

FICTIONAL. Written for the LQA checker's worked example, with defects put there on purpose.

1. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Pet Registration Act 2026.

2. Definitions
In this Act -
council means the Exampleland Council;
owner, of a pet, means the person who owns it;
pet means a dog or a cat.

3. Pets to be registered
(1) The owner of a pet that has reached 3 months of age must ensure that the pet is registered
with the council.
Penalty: a fine of 10 penalty units.
(2) Subsection (1) does not apply if the person has kept the pet for less than 14 days.

4. Applications
(1) The council must grant an application to register a pet if the fee prescribed under
section 40 of the Local Councils Act 2001 has been paid.
(2) The council may refuse to consider an application.

5. Period of registration
A registration shall have effect until the 30 June next following the day on which it is granted.
