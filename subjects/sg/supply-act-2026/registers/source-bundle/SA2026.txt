An Act to provide for the issue from the Consolidated Fund and the
  Development Fund of the sums necessary to meet the estimated
  expenditure for the financial year 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027.
  Be it enacted by the President with the advice and consent of the

Parliament of Singapore, as follows:

              Informal Consolidation – version in force from 1/4/2026
                         SUPPLY                                        3

Short title and commencement

 1. This Act is the Supply Act 2026 and comes into operation on
1 April 2026.

Supply from Consolidated Fund

 2.—(1) A sum of not more than $158,962,520,000 is authorised to
be issued from the Consolidated Fund for the period starting 1 April
2026 and ending 31 March 2027.

 (2) The sum in subsection (1) is appropriated for the Heads of
Expenditure for the public service specified in the Schedule.

Supply from Development Fund

 3.—(1) A sum of not more than $49,014,559,600 is authorised to be
issued from the Development Fund for the period starting 1 April
2026 and ending 31 March 2027.

 (2) The sum in subsection (1) is appropriated for development
purposes under the Heads of Expenditure specified in the Schedule.
