## 1. What is encoded and what is not

**The brief was wrong about the document, and the lead has said so.**
`BRIEF.md` described the file as a summary that "defers to a document the team does not hold".
The 24-page PDF in fact holds two documents with the same product name and the same `V5_072020` footer:
the 2-page summary notes (src 1-97, pages numbered "01 /02" and "02 /02") and the product's full Rules and Terms (src 98-1103, pages "01 /22" to "22 /22": Part I, Articles 1-7; Part II, Articles 8-17; and the annex, the payout-rate table).
The lead was told on 2026-10-06 and ruled: encode both layers, keep them apart, label each rule, resolve a deferral by the notes only to the Rules and Terms in this same file, and record each disagreement as a finding.

## 4. Findings

A finding is a defect in the instrument as written; the forks above are ambiguities I resolved.
"Demonstrated" names the assertion in `pru-tests-findings.l4` (all of which pass: the surprising answer is the source's).

**F-01. An accidental death after day 180 is paid by no Article.**
8.1 (src 419) pays a death by Accident within 180 days; 8.7 (src 516) pays a death "không do Tai nạn".
A death on day 181 from an Accident is neither, and nothing else applies: no benefit, no premium refund.
The same death from illness gets 8.7 (the premiums), and the same Accident had the insured been drunk gets 11.2 (src 725-730, the greater of premiums and surrender value), because 11.2 has no 180-day limit.
Demonstrated: §§ F-01, three assertions (nothing; 30,000,000 under 11.2; 30,000,000 under 8.7).

**F-02. Claiming the permanent-injury maximum forfeits the death benefit for the same Accident.**
8.2 (src 442-443) and 16 (src 990-991) end the contract when 8.2 is paid to its maximum; 8.1 needs the death while the contract is in force (fork F-03).
Lose both legs, be paid 100%, die of the same Accident on day 40: 8.1 pays nothing; had the claim waited, 8.1 would pay 100% (300% on a scheduled flight).
8.10's rule deducting injury benefits from a death within 30 days (src 535-537) presupposes the opposite.
Demonstrated: §§ F-02 (1,000,000,000 paid; contract ends 2027-04-01; death on day 40 gets nothing; without the claim, 1,000,000,000).

**F-03. Accidental blindness and accidental paralysis fall between 8.2 and 8.8.**
Annex note (src 1033) defines loss of an eye as loss of the eyeball; 1.17 (src 201-202) counts total blindness as loss of an eye.
Paralysis appears only in 1.17. 8.8 covers total and permanent disability only "không do Tai nạn".
So total blindness with the eyeballs intact, or paraplegia, pays the premiums back if caused by illness, and nothing if caused by an Accident (unless a fracture row applies).
Demonstrated: §§ F-03, five assertions.

**F-04. The fingers of one hand are worth more itemised than whole.**
Annex 1 (src 1028-1030): thumb 15%, a finger 5%, all the fingers of one hand 25%; 8.2 (src 438-439) adds the rates of several injuries from one Accident.
Itemised, the same loss is 35%. Demonstrated: 250,000,000 against 350,000,000.

**F-05. The youngest band's fracture and burn cap has no VND ceiling.**
8.4/8.5 (src 470-476): 50% of the sum insured under 6; the lesser of 100% and 250 million from 6 to 18; the lesser of 100% and 500 million from 18.
Above a sum insured of 500 million a 5-year-old's cap exceeds a 10-year-old's; above 1 billion, an adult's.
Demonstrated: sum insured 2 billion gives caps of 1,000,000,000, 250,000,000 and 500,000,000.

**F-06. One break, two rows: the ankle.**
Annex 3 lists "Mắt cá chân" at 15% and "Một hoặc nhiều xương ở chân (xương đùi, xương bánh chè, xương chày và xương mác)" at 30%; an ankle fracture is a fracture of the tibia or fibula (outside knowledge, unverified).
The document does not say which row governs. Demonstrated: 150,000,000 against 300,000,000. The elbow and the arm bones overlap the same way, at the same 15%.

**F-07. Overlapping rows: kidneys and burns.**
Annex 2 has "Chấn thương thận" at 5% and "Chấn thương hai thận do cùng một Tai nạn" at 10%; read with 8.3's addition, both kidneys can be claimed as 20%.
Annex 4's rows overlap (fork F-06), and a mixed burn depends on fork F-07. Demonstrated: 200,000,000; and 25% for 10% at third degree with 12% at second.

**F-08. A document deadline that can fall before the event.**
8.6 (src 508-512) pays for an intensive care stay or a transfer with no time limit after the Accident; 10.5 (src 635) wants the documents within 60 days of the Accident.
A transfer on day 70 cannot be documented in time. Neither 10.4 nor 10.5 says what follows a missed 60 days, while 10.6 allows 12 months to claim.
Demonstrated: §§ F-08.

**F-09. Temporary cover.**
Article 3 says nothing of a death not caused by an Accident during temporary cover, not even whether the premium is returned (`REFUSE`, demonstrated in `pru-tests.l4`).
Its exclusions (src 335-344) omit war, which 11.1(a) excludes: a death in a riot is paid 200 million before the certificate issues and is excluded after.
The refund threshold compares the premium with 200 million, not with the benefit: premiums of 150 million against a sum insured of 100 million return 100 million and no refund.
Demonstrated: §§ F-09.

**F-10. The notes' duty-to-declare remedy has no criterion; the Rules and Terms' remedy keeps the premium.**
Notes item 1 (src 14-16): "tùy từng trường hợp", one of three outcomes. 12.2(a) (src 853-864) supplies the criterion and adds that no premium is refunded, which LAW-01 contradicts. Demonstrated: three options; the 12.2(a) consequence.

**F-11. The notes' exclusions are wider than the Rules and Terms'.**
Notes item 3 (src 58-84) excludes every benefit for an event "liên quan đến" a cause; a crime needs no authority's conclusion (src 65-66, its parenthesis is not even closed).
11.1 (src 696-713) excludes only 8.1-8.6, only for a direct cause, and a crime only "theo kết luận của cơ quan Nhà nước có thẩm quyền".
A suicide is excluded by the notes and paid under 8.7; an unproven crime and a merely related cause are excluded by the notes and paid under 8.4. Demonstrated: §§ F-10 to F-12.

**F-12. The notes print caps the Rules and Terms do not have.**
Fracture and burns (src 33-39): the lesser of 100% and 500 million with no age bands; for a 10-year-old the Rules and Terms cap is 250 million.
Emergency transport (src 41-42): 2 million a year with no 1 million per Accident.
For an insured aged 6 to 17 with a sum insured of 500 million or more, the notes print twice the cap the Rules and Terms allow; under 6, with a sum insured below 1 billion, they print more than the 50% cap too. Demonstrated: 500,000,000 against 250,000,000.

**F-13. Who is paid: an empty list, and an insured who is never paid.**
10.8.2 (src 665-675): for an individual policyholder the order is the policyholder, the beneficiary, then the policyholder's heirs "nếu Hợp đồng bảo hiểm không chỉ định Người thụ hưởng"; a predeceased beneficiary's share goes to the heirs only "Trường hợp có nhiều Người thụ hưởng".
A sole beneficiary who has died, with the policyholder dead, is caught by no limb (`REFUSE`).
For an organisation (src 676-693) the insured is not on the list at all: an injured employee's fracture benefit goes to the beneficiary the company named, or with none, to the company. 8.10 nonetheless says "Người được bảo hiểm có quyền yêu cầu giải quyết nhiều quyền lợi". Demonstrated: §§ F-13.

**F-14. Discretion with no criterion.**
Article 9 (src 543, 550, 558-559): a late notice of a change of occupation lets Prudential decline any later claim, with no link required between the occupation and the claim, and no criterion for its choice among three options; 12.2(d) gives the same three options on leaving Vietnam.
12.1(b) and 12.2(c) leave minimums and charges to "quy định của Prudential tại từng thời điểm"; 12.1(e)-(g) take effect only on Prudential's written approval, with no time for it; 10.7 lets Prudential ask for further documents without limit. Demonstrated in part (§§ F-14); the rest reading only.

**F-15. Incontestability protects against nothing that cancels.**
Article 6 (src 384-390) excepts "các thông tin quan trọng mà nếu biết được … Prudential đã không chấp nhận bảo hiểm": exactly the misstatements for which 12.2(a) cancels.
What it shuts off after 24 months is a non-material misstatement, which under 12.2(a) could only ever bring extra premium. Demonstrated: §§ F-15.

**F-16. A reinstatement requested in time can be defeated by Prudential's own delay.**
12.1(h) (src 817-819, 836-838): request within 24 months of the lapse; effect from the date of Prudential's confirmation letter. 16 (src 986): the contract ends after 24 months of lapse. No time is set for the letter.
Demonstrated: request on day 719 of the lapse, letter on day 735: never effective.

**F-17. The notes are not part of the contract** (reading only). 2.1 (src 297-306) lists the contract's documents; the summary notes are not among them, though the sales illustration is (src 303).

**F-18. 8.6 has no "in force" words** (reading only). 8.1-8.5, 8.7 and 8.8 each say "trong thời gian Hợp đồng bảo hiểm đang còn hiệu lực"; 8.6 does not. The encoding supplies the condition from 12.1(h) (src 840-842) and Article 16.

**F-19. Article 16's first ground cites 12.1(h)** (reading only). Src 983-984 lists "Điều 12.1 d), Điều 12.1 h), Điều 15.1" as early termination; 12.1(h) is reinstatement, which terminates nothing, though it does cancel a request for misdeclaration (src 827-829). 12.1(g), surrender, which does terminate, is not cited.

**F-20. 10.7's further documents and the 30-day clock** (reading only). 10.6 starts its 30 days from a file complete "theo các quy định nêu tại Điều 10.1 đến Điều 10.5"; 10.7's further documents are outside that list, so whether a request under 10.7 stops the clock is not said.

**F-21. Terms that carry weight and are undefined** (reading only). "phỏng" and its degrees, and how body-skin area is measured (annex 4); "Chuyển viện" (8.6); "hoạt động nguy hiểm" beyond its examples (fork F-32).

**F-22. Exclusions that cannot apply** (reading only). 11.1(c) and Article 3 exclude suicide from benefits for a death "do Tai nạn", but 1.16 requires an Accident to be "không chủ động và ngoài ý muốn", so a suicide is never one: the head is idle for death and matters only for self-injury.
