## Reading notes and genuine ambiguities, stated once

N1. The Wikisource Schedule J table for 2025 to 2026 prints "מדרגת הגבייה המופחתת" in most headings but col D's upper heading "על חלק השכר העולה על 60% מהשכר הממוצע".
The Budget-year Law (Amendment 256) s 19(6) replaces "60%" in Schedule J, ss 341, 342 and 345B(e) by "מדרגת הגבייה המופחתת", effective 1 January 2026, so for 2026 the tier is 7,703 NIS (s 334(a) note), and I use it throughout.
N2. The 2025 to 2026 temporary table (Amendment 252 s 7) governs 2026: employee total 5.55% below the tier and 14.50% above plus the 2024 to 2027 work-injury increase of 0.10 (14.60%); employee deduction (col D) 1.04% below and 7.00% above; self-employed 4.47% / 12.83%; non-employed 6.92% / 7.00%.
N3. Col D's printed branch cells above the tier add to 4.67, not the printed total 7.00 (the cells are 0.87, 0.07, 0.21, 1.86, 0.14, 1.52).
I use the printed total 7.00 and the cells only where one branch is asked (unemployment 0.21 above / 0.02 below).
No case below depends on a branch split of the 7.00.
N4. s 342(e)(3),(4) and reg 1 refer to "טור ה׳ בלוח י׳" for the deduction rate; in the present schedule the deduction columns are col D and col E is the Treasury allocation.
I read the reference as col D (7.00% above the tier).
N5. Rounding: the Rounding Regulations reg 6(b) rounds to whole shekels (50 agorot up) "הסכום הכולל של דמי ביטוח שמעביד מנכה משכרם של עובדיו".
I state both the exact amount and the whole-shekel amount for a single employee and treat the exact amount as decided; the whole-shekel figure is M.
N6. s 342(c)(1) and (d): deduction cases use the employee's total income of the month, capped at 5 x the paragraph-3 basic amount, 5 x 10,382 = 51,910 (Schedule K11 item 1; s 1 basic amount (3) note).
N7. The minimum wage (Schedule K11 item 1 minimum) is a published figure not in the sources; every case needing it is marked "needs the published figure".
