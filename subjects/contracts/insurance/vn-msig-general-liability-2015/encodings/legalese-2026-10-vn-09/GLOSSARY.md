# Glossary — MSIG Vietnam general liability policy (Revised CGL Policy Form, 12 January 2015)

Row `legalese-2026-10-vn-09`.

**The source is English only.** There is no Vietnamese text of this policy in the deposit, and none was found.
So the first column is not a quotation: every Vietnamese term below is this encoder's rendering of the English term, and every line that carries one is marked `[translator]`.
None of it is MSIG's wording, and a Vietnamese counterpart of the form, if MSIG publishes one, may use other words.
Where Vietnamese insurance law uses a term of art for the concept, the rendering follows it and the risk column says which article; the Law's own text is not quoted here (the article and the line of the aid file are cited instead).

`src:N` is line N of `../../source/raw/msig-cgl.txt`; "Law" is the Law on Insurance Business 08/2022/QH15, read from the aid `../../../../../../.aids/law-08-2022-qh15.txt`, cited by article and line.
