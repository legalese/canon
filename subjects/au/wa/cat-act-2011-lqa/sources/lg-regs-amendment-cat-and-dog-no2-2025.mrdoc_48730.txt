Cat Act 2011 Dog Act 1976
Local Government Regulations Amendment (Cat and Dog) Regulations (No. 2) 2025
Made by the Governor in Executive Council.
	Part 1 — Preliminary
1.	Citation
These regulations are the Local Government Regulations Amendment (Cat and Dog) Regulations (No. 2) 2025.
2.	Commencement
These regulations come into operation as follows —
(a)	Part 1 — on the day on which these regulations are published on the WA legislation website;
(b)	the rest of the regulations — on the day after that day.
	Part 2 — Cat Regulations 2012 amended
3.	Regulations amended
This Part amends the Cat Regulations 2012.
4.	Regulation 6 amended
Delete regulation 6(b).
	Part 3 — Dog Regulations 2013 amended
5.	Regulations amended
This Part amends the Dog Regulations 2013.
6.	Regulation 7 amended
Delete regulation 7(d).
