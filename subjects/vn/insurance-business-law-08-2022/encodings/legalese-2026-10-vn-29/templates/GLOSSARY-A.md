## Part A. The terms the Law defines, and the other terms that carry weight

A `src:N` in the fourth column is a line of `law08-2022-qh15.txt`; `src:b:N` a line of `law08-2022-qh15-577-578.txt`; `src:a:N` a line of `law139-2025-qh15.txt`.
The English identifier is the name of the rule or type in the `.l4` modules; the rule names begin `Dieu N(...)` (ASCII for Điều), so a search for `Dieu 4(2)` finds the rule that encodes Điều 4(2).
Every Vietnamese term in the first column was checked by `tools/vnsrc.py check` to occur verbatim in a source.
