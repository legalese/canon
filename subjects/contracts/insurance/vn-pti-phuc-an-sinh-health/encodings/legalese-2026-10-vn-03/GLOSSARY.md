Vietnamese (source) to English (encoding). Every Vietnamese term below is copied from the raw text and checked by script to occur in it verbatim (case aside).
Source lines are lines of `../../source/raw/pti-phuc-an-sinh.txt`. "def N" is definition N of Part 1; "excl N" exclusion N of Part 3.
Translation risk is "low" where the term has one plain English rendering; otherwise the column says what could go wrong.
