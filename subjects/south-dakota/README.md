# south-dakota/

South Dakota (US state) legislation, one subject-id per body of law, following the subject-sidecar shape described in [`subjects/README.md`](../README.md).

The subjects here were laid out on 2026-09-23 for the legislation governing twelve everyday topics. Each was checked against the South Dakota Legislature - South Dakota Codified Laws (Legislative Research Council); the check is recorded in each subject's `registers/source-bundle/*.meta.json`. No statute text has been deposited and nothing is encoded yet.

**Licence category: unclear.** **Metadata only until resolved.** No human has recorded a licence decision for this jurisdiction in `tools/topics/round3/licence-decisions.json`. The register's own terms, quoted above, are the evidence to decide on.

## Register access

Fully accessible. The statute browser is a JavaScript single-page application, so a plain GET of /Statutes/<ref> returns only the app shell; the pages are backed by the Legislature's own public JSON API on the same host, which serves the data the page displays. Endpoint shapes were discovered by loading the site's own pages in a browser and reading the requests the page itself makes, not by guessing or scraping around anything: /api/Statutes/Title lists the 71 Titles, /api/Statutes/Statute/<ref> returns one Title, Chapter or Section, and /api/Statutes/LastStatuesEffectiveDate returns the code's currency date. Requests used the honest user agent 'canon-legislation-indexer/1.0 (legalese/canon corpus metadata survey)' with Accept: application/json; every request returned 200 with no challenge, no rate limiting and no block. The API response carries a Repealed boolean and a CatchLine, which is what in-force status here was taken from, alongside the [Repealed] and [Repealed And Transferred] markers the Title pages print in their chapter index. The API also returns the full statutory text in an Html field: that field was parsed only for chapter names, section numbers and section catchlines and was never copied into this record. About 25 requests in total, spaced 2 s apart. One thing to note honestly: the site's app shell loads Google reCAPTCHA (a site-wide script, presumably for its contact and MyLRC forms); no challenge was ever presented on any statute page or API call, and nothing was bypassed.

## Topics with no legislation here

- **Personal tax/withholding**: South Dakota levies no individual income tax, so there is no personal income tax statute and no state wage-withholding scheme to encode. Verified against the register's own Title 10 (Taxation) chapter index at https://sdlegislature.gov/api/Statutes/Statute/10: the only income tax chapter is ch. 10-43 (Income Tax on Banks and Financial Corporations), which is a corporate franchise tax on financial institutions; the Title is otherwise property tax (chs. 10-4 to 10-27), sales and use tax (chs. 10-45, 10-46), excise and severance taxes. The inheritance and estate tax chapters (10-40, 10-40A, 10-41) are marked [Repealed] on that index. Personal income tax withholding for a South Dakota worker is therefore federal only: 26 U.S.C. ch. 24 and FICA under 26 U.S.C. ch. 21.
- **Passenger compensation**: No state statute. Compensation for denied boarding, delay and cancellation is federal: 14 C.F.R. part 250 and 49 U.S.C. ch. 417 for air, 49 U.S.C. for rail. Verified against the register's Title 49 index: the passenger-carrier chapters 49-04 (Carriers of Persons) and 49-09 (Passes Issued by Carriers) are both marked [Repealed], ch. 49-02 (Carriers' Powers and Duties in General) and ch. 49-28 (Motor Carrier Regulation) deal with safety, licensing and rates, and no chapter creates a passenger compensation entitlement.
- **Customs/duties**: Federal matter. Customs duties and tariffs are exclusively federal (Tariff Act of 1930, 19 U.S.C.); U.S. Const. art. I, sec. 10, cl. 2 bars states from laying imposts or duties on imports or exports.
- **Work permits/immigration**: Federal matter. Immigration status and work authorisation are governed by the Immigration and Nationality Act, 8 U.S.C., and I-9 verification by 8 U.S.C. sec. 1324a. The Codified Laws contain no immigration or work-permit chapter; the Title 60 (Labor and Employment) index carries nothing on work authorisation, and the nearest provisions are alien land-ownership restrictions in SDCL chs. 43-2A and 43-2B, which are not a work-permit scheme.
