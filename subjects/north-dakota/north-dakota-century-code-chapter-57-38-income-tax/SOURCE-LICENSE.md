Under the government edicts doctrine confirmed in Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org, 590 U.S. 255 (2020), the text, numbering and section headings of the Century Code, being the work of legislators acting in their legislative capacity, are not subject to copyright at all, so reuse and adaptation are lawful notwithstanding the footer notice.
