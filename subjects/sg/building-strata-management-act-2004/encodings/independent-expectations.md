# Independent expectations: Building (Strata Management) Act 2004

Written from `../source/BSMA2004.txt` (SSO consolidation in force from 1 Oct 2026) and `BRIEF.md` only, **before** any `.l4` file was opened. Each item gives a concrete scenario, the answer worked from the text, and the provision. "REFUSE" means I think the Act gives no answer on the facts given.

Conventions: "notice day N" means the meeting is held on the Nth day after the day notice of the motion is given (notice 1 Mar, meeting 16 Mar = day 15). Share values (SV) are integers. Where I am unsure of a reading, I say so.

## A. The six resolutions (s 2(2)-(8))

- **E01** Ordinary resolution, meeting on day 15, show of hands 10 for and 9 against: **passes**. s 2(2)(a), (b)(i)
- **E02** Ordinary, meeting on day 14, 10 for and 9 against: **fails** (needs the 15th day or later). s 2(2)(a)
- **E03** Ordinary, day 15, show of hands 10 for and 10 against: **fails** (needs more for than against). s 2(2)(b)(i)
- **E04** Ordinary on a poll, day 20, SV for 501 and SV against 500: **passes**. s 2(2)(b)(ii)
- **E05** Ordinary on a poll, SV for 500 and against 500: **fails**. s 2(2)(b)(ii)
- **E06** Ordinary on a poll where more *hands* are for but more SV is against (hands 10:2, SV 300:700): **fails**. The poll counts SV. s 2(2)(b)(ii)
- **E07** Special resolution, day 22, SV for 750 and against 250 (exactly 75%): **passes**. s 2(3)
- **E08** Special, day 22, for 749 and against 251: **fails**. s 2(3)(b)
- **E09** Special, day 21, for 900 and against 100: **fails** (needs the 22nd day). s 2(3)(a)
- **E10** Special, day 22, for 750, against 200, plus 100 SV of unmarked or abstain slips: **passes**. Unmarked votes are not valid votes and drop out of the denominator. s 2(3)(b), 2(8)(b)
- **E11** Special: votes cast both for and against are excluded. For 750, against 250, plus 200 SV marked both ways: **passes** (750/1000). s 2(8)(a)
- **E12** 90% resolution, day 22, for 900 and against 100 (exactly 90%): **passes**. s 2(5)
- **E13** 90%, day 22, for 899 and against 101: **fails**. s 2(5)(b)
- **E14** 90%, day 21, for 1000 and against 0: **fails**. s 2(5)(a)
- **E15** Unanimous resolution, day 22, 20 valid votes for and 0 against: **passes**. s 2(4)
- **E16** Unanimous, day 22, 20 for and 1 valid vote against: **fails**. s 2(4)(b)
- **E17** Unanimous, day 22, 20 for, 0 against, 3 void-for-uncertainty slips: **passes**. Void votes are not valid. s 2(4)(b), 2(8)(c)
- **E18** Unanimous, day 21, all for: **fails**. s 2(4)(a)
- **E19** Comprehensive resolution: considered on day 22; at the end of the 12 weeks, poll SV for is 9,000 of the aggregate SV of all SPs then constituting the MC (10,000): **passes** (exactly 90%). The denominator is *all* lots, not votes cast. s 2(6)
- **E20** Comprehensive, same but SV for is 8,999 of 10,000: **fails**. s 2(6)(b)
- **E21** Comprehensive: 100% of the votes cast are for, but those votes are only 5,000 of 10,000 aggregate SV: **fails**. s 2(6)(b)
- **E22** Comprehensive, considered on day 21: **fails**. s 2(6)(a)
- **E23** Resolution by consensus, day 22: at the end of the 12 weeks all 50 SPs then constituting the MC support it in writing: **passes**. s 2(7)
- **E24** Consensus: 49 of 50 SPs support in writing: **fails**. s 2(7)(b)
- **E25** Consensus, considered on day 21, all support: **fails**. s 2(7)(a)
- **E26** The comprehensive and consensus 12-week period runs from the day the general meeting is convened. A meeting on 1 Mar 2026 gives an end date of **24 May 2026**. s 2(6)(b), 2(7)(b)
- **E27** A vote by someone not entitled to vote (for example a defaulter on an ordinary motion) is not a valid vote and is not counted. s 2(8) opening words

## B. The resolution each matter needs

- **E28** Improving common property under s 29(1)(d) (install a facility, change use, erect a structure): **special**. s 29(1)(d)
- **E29** Installing a fixed EV charger on common property under a lease or licence of 10 years or less: **ordinary**, not special. ss 29(1B), 34A(1)(a)
- **E30** Making or amending a general by-law (pets, parking, floor coverings): **special**. s 32(3)
- **E31** A by-law on parking lots that have fixed EV chargers installed: **ordinary**. s 32(3A)
- **E32** Exclusive use by-law for 6 months: **ordinary**. s 33(1)(a)
- **E33** Exclusive use for exactly 1 year: **ordinary** ("not exceeding one year"). s 33(1)(a)
- **E34** Exclusive use for 2 years: **special**. s 33(1)(b)
- **E35** Exclusive use for exactly 3 years: **special**. s 33(1)(b)
- **E36** Exclusive use for 3 years and 1 day, or for 4 years: **90%**. s 33(1)(c)
- **E37** Exclusive use for 2 years with a 2-year renewal option (3 years aggregate exceeded): **not** under (b), which requires that the term "cannot be extended ... to exceed an aggregate of 3 years". It falls under (c): **90%**. s 33(1)(b)-(c)
- **E38** An exclusive use by-law without the written consent of the SP of the lot concerned: **not validly made**, whatever the resolution. s 33(1) opening words
- **E39** Transferring part of the common property: **90%** (and relevant authority approval). s 34(1)(a)
- **E40** Leasing common property for 5 years: **90%**. s 34(1)(b)
- **E41** Leasing common property for 2 years with no renewal beyond 3 years: **special**. s 34(2)(a)
- **E42** Leasing common property for 1 year: **ordinary**. s 34(2)(b)
- **E43** Accepting a transfer of abutting land as additional common property: **special**. s 34(3)
- **E44** Approving the subdivision of a lot that creates new common property: **ordinary**. s 34(4)
- **E45** Amalgamating the common property of 2 MCs: **special by each MC**. s 34(5)
- **E46** EV charger lease for 11 years: outside s 34A(1), which is capped at 10 years, so s 34(1)(b) applies: **90%**. This is my reading; s 34(2A) only excludes matters *within* s 34A(1). ss 34(1)(b), 34A(1)
- **E47** Granting an easement or restrictive covenant burdening the parcel: **unanimous**. s 35(1)(a)
- **E48** Authorising an improvement that increases floor area: **90%**. s 37(2)
- **E49** Authorising an improvement that affects appearance: the Act names no resolution. s 37(4) only requires the MC to be satisfied on (a) and (b). If the question is "which resolution?": **REFUSE**. s 37(4)
- **E50** Installing safety equipment (window grille): **no authority needed**. s 37A(1)
- **E51** Determining contributions under s 39(1) or (2): **ordinary**. s 39(1), (2)
- **E52** Levying contributions on a different formula after a change in use of common property: **comprehensive**. s 41(6)
- **E53** Levying contributions by another method on all or any SPs: **consensus**. s 41(8)
- **E54** A discount for early payment: **special**. s 41(9)
- **E55** Selling a lot under a s 43 charge: **special**. s 43(3)(a)
- **E56** Altering common property during the initial period: **special**. s 50
- **E57** Removing a council member for misconduct: **ordinary** at a general meeting. s 54(2)(b)
- **E58** Reserving matters for the general meeting: **ordinary**. s 59
- **E59** Appointing a managing agent at a general meeting: **ordinary**. s 66(1)(a)
- **E60** Giving the managing agent the chairperson's, secretary's, treasurer's or council's powers: **ordinary**. s 67(3)
- **E61** A direction not to insure the building under a damage policy: **consensus**. s 70(1)
- **E62** Further insurance decisions under s 71: **ordinary**. s 71(3A)
- **E63** Designating limited common property after constitution: **comprehensive**. s 78(1)(a)(ii), (2)(b)
- **E64** Terminating the strata scheme: **consensus**. s 84(1)
- **E65** Dissolving an SMC: **comprehensive by both the MC and the SMC**. s 84(2)
- **E66** Instituting proceedings about common property: **ordinary**. s 85(1A)
- **E67** Non-lot acquisition: an appeal or a compensation claim needs **ordinary**; distributing the compensation needs **special**; expressing a desire that the whole land be acquired needs **consensus**. s 85A(1), (2), (4), (5)
- **E68** Shortening the notice period for a meeting: **special**. First Sch para 1A(6)
- **E69** Amending a special resolution: needs a **special** resolution; ordinary is not enough. First Sch para 13
- **E70** Approving an annual budget for social activities and legal services: **ordinary**. s 38(3A)
- **E71** Requiring physical meetings: **ordinary**. First Sch para 1C(2)(b)

## C. General meetings, voting entitlement, proxies, requisitions (First Schedule)

- **E72** Notice must be served at least 14 days before the meeting (para 1A(1)). Notice served 1 Mar and meeting 15 Mar (exactly 14 days, so day 14): this satisfies para 1A(1), but an ordinary resolution still needs day 15 under s 2(2).
- **E73** Quorum: SPs owning exactly 30% of the aggregate SV present in person or proxy: **quorum**. para 3(2)(a)
- **E74** SPs owning 29.9% (299 of 1000): **no quorum**. para 3(2)
- **E75** No quorum after half an hour, but 2 SPs are present in person: the meeting **may proceed** as if quorate. para 3(3)
- **E76** Same, but only 1 SP is present in person: **may not**. para 3(3)
- **E77** Same as E75, but the meeting was convened on a requisition: **may not** proceed. para 3(4)
- **E78** Notice of a resumed (adjourned) meeting must be given at least 14 days before it. Given 13 days before: **non-compliant**. para 3A(3)
- **E79** An SP in arrears 3 days before the meeting may not vote on an ordinary, special or 90% motion. para 2(1), 1A(2)(e)(ii)
- **E80** An SP in arrears **may** vote on a unanimous resolution or a resolution by consensus. para 1A(2)(e)(ii). This sits uneasily with para 2(1), which has no such exception; I follow the specific carve-out.
- **E81** An SP who paid everything at least 3 days before the meeting may vote; one who paid 2 days before may not (outside unanimous and consensus). para 1A(2)(e)(ii)
- **E82** The first mortgagee on the strata roll votes: the SP's vote on that proposal **is not counted**. para 2(2)
- **E83** Joint SPs both vote: the vote of the one named first on the strata roll is accepted. para 2(3)
- **E84** A company SP's vote has no effect unless the MC has received written notice of its representative. s 65(10)
- **E85** A person who has not complied with a s 65(8) notice may not vote. s 65(9)
- **E86** Proxy limit with 100 lots: 2% of 100 is 2, and the greater of 2 and 2 is **2 lots**. para 17(5)
- **E87** Proxy limit with 50 lots: 2% is 1, so **2 lots** (the higher). para 17(5)
- **E88** Proxy limit with 249 lots: 2% is 4.98, rounded down to 4, so **4 lots**. para 17(5)
- **E89** Proxy limit with 250 lots: **5 lots**. para 17(5)
- **E90** A proxy holding instruments for 3 lots in a 100-lot development: the 3rd instrument is **void**. para 17(6)
- **E91** A proxy instrument is void if the appointer is present. para 17(4)
- **E92** A proxy instrument deposited 47 hours before the meeting: **not valid**. One deposited 48 hours before: **valid**. para 18(2)(b), (3)
- **E93** On a poll, a proposed lot with SV 7 has a vote value of 25% × 7 = 1.75, ignoring the fraction: **1**. para 9(4)
- **E94** A special or 90% motion is decided by SV even when no poll is demanded. para 9(2)(b)
- **E95** A council election: one vote per lot, and no poll. para 8(4), (5)
- **E96** Requisition for an EGM signed by holders of exactly 20% of the aggregate SV: the council **must convene**. para 14(1)(a)
- **E97** Requisition by 19% of SV and 24% of the SPs by number: **not** a valid requisition. para 14(1)
- **E98** Requisition by 5% of SV but exactly 25% of the SPs by number: **valid**. para 14(1)(b)
- **E99** The EGM on a requisition received 1 Mar 2026 must be held no later than 6 weeks after: **12 Apr 2026**. para 14(1)
- **E100** If the council does not convene within 14 days of the deposit, the requisitionists may convene, but not later than 3 months from deposit. para 14(3)
- **E101** Requisition before the first AGM: needs **at least 25% of SV** (20% is not enough). para 15(1)(b)
- **E102** Candidates nominated equal to or fewer than council seats: **all declared elected**. para 8(3)

## D. Initial period and AGMs (ss 2(1), 26, 27)

- **E103** MC constituted 1 Mar 2025 with no AGM held: the initial period ends **1 Mar 2026** (12 months later). s 2(1) "initial period"
- **E104** The same MC holds its first AGM on 1 Oct 2025: the initial period ends **1 Oct 2025**, whichever is first. s 2(1)
- **E105** First AGM deadline, no request: one month after the end of the initial period (1 Mar 2026): **1 Apr 2026**. s 26(1)(a)
- **E106** A request from SPs of 10% of the lots (10 of 100) is received 1 Jun 2025: the deadline is the earlier of 1 Apr 2026 and 8 weeks after receipt (**27 Jul 2025**), so **27 Jul 2025**. s 26(1)(b)
- **E107** A request from 9 of 100 lots does not trigger s 26(1)(b); the deadline stays **1 Apr 2026**. s 26(1)(b)
- **E108** An owner developer who fails to hold the first AGM in time, without reasonable excuse: fine up to **$5,000**, and **$100** per day continuing. s 26(9)
- **E109** Documents under s 26(4) must be delivered at the end of the first AGM or within 2 weeks after it is convened. AGM 1 Mar 2026: by **15 Mar 2026**. s 26(4)
- **E110** First annual budget: 12 months starting on the first day of the month after the first AGM. AGM 15 Mar 2026: the budget starts **1 Apr 2026**. s 26(4)(b)
- **E111** Last AGM 15 Mar 2025, next AGM 10 Jun 2026: within 15 months (15 Jun 2026), and one was held in 2026: **compliant** (but see E113).
- **E112** Last AGM 15 Mar 2025, next AGM 20 Jun 2026: more than 15 months: **contravention**, an offence by the chairperson and secretary. s 27(1), (4)
- **E113** Last AGM 1 Dec 2025, next AGM 15 Jan 2027 (within 15 months, but none in calendar year 2026): **contravention** ("in each calendar year"). s 27(1). Note on E111: the 2025 AGM was 15 Mar 2025, and 2025 itself had one, so it is fine.
- **E114** The s 27(4) penalty: no penalty is expressly provided, so s 128 applies: **$10,000**. A reasonable-steps defence is available. ss 27(5), 128

## E. The owner developer (Parts 4 and 5 Division 1, s 23)

- **E115** Selling a lot before the schedule of strata units is accepted: offence, **$10,000 or 12 months or both**. ss 11(1), 14
- **E116** The Commissioner must accept or reject a schedule within 6 weeks of filing. Filed 1 Mar 2026: by **12 Apr 2026**. s 11(7)
- **E117** A lease of 21 years with no option is outside s 11(1); a lease of 22 years is inside it (a sale needing an accepted schedule). s 13(1)(a)
- **E118** Division 1 applies to a development with 5 lots (more than 4) once 2 or more lots are sold to more than one purchaser; it **does not** apply with exactly 4 lots. s 15(1)(b), (2)
- **E119** With 10 lots but only 1 lot sold, or 2 lots sold to the same single purchaser: Division 1 **does not yet apply**. s 15(2)
- **E120** Failing to establish maintenance funds: **$10,000**, and **$100** per day continuing. s 16(4)
- **E121** Earliest date the developer may pay maintenance charges into the fund: the later of 4 weeks after the first TOP and the fund's establishment. TOP 1 Mar 2026, fund established 10 Mar 2026: **29 Mar 2026**. s 17(2)
- **E122** Maintenance fund accounts must be audited within 4 months after the MC is constituted, and filed not later than the 28th day after audit. s 17(5)(c), (d)
- **E123** A copy of the register of purchasers is due not later than the 14th day after the Commissioner's request. s 17(8)
- **E124** Penalties: s 17(1), (4) or (5) carries **$10,000** + $100/day (s 17(9)); s 17(6), (7) or (8) carries **$2,000** + $100/day (s 17(10)).
- **E125** Collecting maintenance charges without the Commissioner's approval: offence, no express penalty, so **$10,000**. ss 18(2), 128
- **E126** The Commissioner must give at least 14 days' notice before appointing a managing agent; the developer may appeal not later than the 21st day after Gazette publication. s 19(3), (4)
- **E127** A Commissioner-appointed managing agent who fails to submit the statement within 2 months, or to pay moneys in: **$5,000**. s 20(6), (8)
- **E128** Purchaser's maintenance charges unpaid 28 days after service of demand: they become a debt recoverable by the developer. Served 1 Mar: a debt from **29 Mar** (end of the 28 days). s 22(1)
- **E129** The developer must transfer control of money within one week after the first AGM: AGM 1 Mar 2026, by **8 Mar 2026**. s 23(4)
- **E130** The developer keeps financial records for 2 years after the transfer of control. s 23(5)

## F. The management corporation: contributions, charges and records (ss 38-48)

- **E131** Contribution due 1 Mar 2026 and paid 31 Mar 2026 (the 30th day after): **no interest**. s 40(6)(b)
- **E132** The same contribution paid 1 Apr 2026: interest accrues from **31 Mar 2026** (expiry of 30 days), at the rate the MC determines; none if the MC has determined in general meeting that it bears none. s 40(6)(b)
- **E133** The MC may serve a written demand only once the contribution is unpaid after the 30th day, so on or after **1 Apr 2026** for a 1 Mar due date. s 40(9)
- **E134** Demand served 1 Jun 2026: the SP commits an offence if not paid **before** the 14th day after service (15 Jun 2026). Paid 14 Jun: **no offence**. Paid 15 Jun or unpaid: **offence**, fine up to **$10,000** and **$100** per day while unpaid after conviction. s 40(10)
- **E135** Contributions on a proposed lot while the building is uncompleted are reduced by 75%. 1,000 becomes **250**. A building is completed once a TOP is issued for any proposed lot in it. s 41(1), (2)
- **E136** Normal apportionment is by SV. Total levy 100,000 and lot SV 10 of 1,000: **1,000**. s 40(2)
- **E137** Charge on the lot: the amount must remain unpaid on the expiry of 30 days after the demand. Demand 1 Mar 2026: chargeable from **31 Mar 2026**. The charge arises only on lodgment and registration. s 43(1)
- **E138** Sale under the charge requires all four of: a special resolution, newspaper notice, 6 weeks after publication with no payment, and no pending legal action. Missing any one: **may not sell**. s 43(3)
- **E139** The management fund may pay for legal services and for social activities for the benefit of all (s 38(3)(d), (e), in force 1 Oct 2026). It may not fund collective-sale purposes other than convening a general meeting under the LT(S)A Second Schedule. s 38(3), (9A)
- **E140** An auditor not appointed at the AGM may be appointed by the council not later than the 90th day after the AGM. s 45(3)(b)
- **E141** Inspection under s 47: failing agreement by the 7th day after receipt, the MC fixes a date not later than the 21st day after receipt, between 9 a.m. and 6 p.m. s 47(2)
- **E142** Delivering records on a council resolution: not later than the 7th day after service of notice. Default without reasonable excuse: **$5,000**. s 48(1), (1A)
- **E143** Records are retained for at least 5 years from the end of the financial year in which the transactions are completed. Failure: offence, so **$10,000** (s 128). s 48(2), (3)
- **E144** By-laws have no force until lodged with the Commissioner; they must be lodged not later than the 45th day after the resolution. s 32(4), (5)
- **E145** Address change: notify the Registrar and the Commissioner, and serve the SPs, not later than the 7th day. s 28(3)

## G. The initial period (ss 49-51)

- **E146** During the initial period, granting an easement needs a Commissioner order under s 51 (and the s 35 unanimous resolution). A special resolution alone is **not** enough. ss 49(1)(c), 51(1)
- **E147** During the initial period, altering common property is permitted by special resolution, and the Commissioner cannot authorise it (s 51 omits para (b)). ss 49(1)(b), 50, 51(1)
- **E148** During the initial period, borrowing money needs a Commissioner order. s 49(1)(f)

## H. Windows, improvements, entry, insurance

- **E149** A sliding window on the exterior wall of a lot: **part of the lot**. s 2(9)(a)
- **E150** A fixed glass panel on the exterior wall: **common property**, unless the strata title plan describes it otherwise. s 2(9)(b)
- **E151** Entry for s 29(1)(b) work: in an emergency **at any time**; otherwise at a reasonable time **on notice** to the occupier. Non-emergency without notice: **not authorised**. s 31(1)(e), (f)
- **E152** Obstructing entry: offence, so **$10,000** (s 128). s 31(2)
- **E153** Mortgaged-lot insurance payout: the least of the contract amount, the loss, and the amount needed to discharge the mortgages. Contract 500k, loss 300k, mortgages 400k: **300k**. s 73(1)(a)(ii)
- **E154** The MC must insure under a damage policy **unless** directed otherwise by a resolution by consensus. s 70(1)

## I. The council (ss 53-60, Second Schedule)

- **E155** Council size: up to 14 natural persons. 15: **not allowed**. s 53(1)
- **E156** An MC with 3 or fewer SPs: the council consists of each SP (or nominee); with 4 SPs, s 53(1) election applies. s 53(2)
- **E157** A 20-year-old SP: **ineligible**. A 21-year-old SP not in arrears: **eligible**. s 53(6)
- **E158** An SP whose lot is in arrears on the 3rd day before the election: **ineligible**, and the same for that SP's nominee or family nominee. s 53(7)
- **E159** An immediate family member (sibling) nominated by the SP: **eligible**. A cousin: **ineligible** (not "immediate family"). ss 2(1), 53(6)(c)
- **E160** Threshold number, council of 9, SP holding 30% of the SV: floor(2.7) = 2 against floor(4.41) = 4, so **2**. s 53(12)
- **E161** Council of 9, SP holding 60%: floor(5.4) = 5 against 4, so **4**. s 53(12)
- **E162** Council of 10, SP holding 5%: floor(0.5) = 0, so the threshold is **0** (no nominee eligible under (8)(b)). s 53(12)
- **E163** An undischarged bankrupt is eligible only if the status is declared in writing at nomination. s 53(9)
- **E164** A person already elected secretary cannot be elected chairperson or treasurer. s 53(9A)
- **E165** A treasurer who has served 2 consecutive terms cannot be re-elected treasurer (a 3rd consecutive term); one with 1 prior term **can**. s 53(9B)
- **E166** A written nomination given 47 hours before the meeting: **no effect**. 48 hours: **effective**. s 53B(3)(b)(ii)
- **E167** A member who misses 3 consecutive council meetings without permission **vacates** office; 2 missed meetings, or 3 with permission: no. s 54(1)(c)
- **E168** Removal without a general meeting: arrears for **more than 3 months**. Exactly 3 months: **no**. s 54(2)(a)
- **E169** Council quorum: 9 members gives **5**; 8 gives **5**; 2 gives **2**; 1 gives **1**. Second Sch para 2(1)
- **E170** Council decision: a majority of the members voting, at a quorate meeting. 5 present, 3 for and 2 against: **decided**. Second Sch para 2(3)
- **E171** Notice of a council meeting: at least 3 days before. Second Sch para 4(1)
- **E172** One-third opposition: 100 lots, written notice from SPs owning 34 lots: the council **must not** decide; 33 lots: **may** decide. With 99 lots, 33 lots is exactly one-third, so **must not**. s 58(3)
- **E173** Restricted matters (those needing special or other higher resolutions) are not council decisions. s 58(1), (4)
- **E174** A council member who fails to disclose an interest: **$5,000 or 12 months or both**. s 60(10)
- **E175** A breach of s 61 duties: **$5,000 or 12 months or both**. s 61(3)(b)
- **E176** Minutes on the notice board: council minutes not later than the 14th day after the meeting; GM minutes not later than the 45th day; kept up for 14 days. Second Sch para 3(2), (3)
- **E177** A managing agent appointed under s 66 holds office until the conclusion of the 3rd AGM after appointment, unless its term expires or it is terminated earlier. s 66(2)

## J. Strata Titles Boards (Part 6)

- **E178** A Board of the president and 2 members: **valid**. President and 4: **valid**. President and 3: **invalid**. s 89(3)
- **E179** The mediation phase is at most 3 days; then the Board arbitrates. s 92(1)(b)
- **E180** The final order is due within 6 months of constitution, unless the Minister extends. s 92(9)
- **E181** Deemed refusal: an application to the MC to exercise a discretion with no response within 2 months is deemed a decision not to act. s 101(5)
- **E182** The Board cannot order under s 101 on a matter that needs a special, 90%, unanimous, comprehensive or consensus resolution. s 101(6)
- **E183** Refusing to invalidate a resolution under s 103 requires both no prejudice **and** that compliance would not have changed the result; otherwise the Board must not refuse. s 103(2)
- **E184** A s 104 application 21 days after the meeting: **in time**; 22 days: **out of time**. s 104(2)
- **E185** The Board may dismiss an SP's application where the SP has not paid all contributions. s 116(d)
- **E186** An interim order lasts at most 3 months; renewal must be requested not later than 3 months after it is made; a renewed order expires 6 months from the making. s 118(1)(b), (5)
- **E187** Contravening a Board order: **$10,000 or 5 years or both**. s 120(2)
- **E188** Disobeying a Board summons: **$5,000 or 2 years or both**. s 96(2)
- **E189** A false statement in a request for an interim order: **$5,000 or 6 months or both**. s 118(7)

## K. Offences, composition, service

- **E191** False information to the Commissioner: **$5,000 or 6 months or both**. s 127(3)
- **E192** Offences with no express penalty (ss 18(2), 27(4), 28(4), 31(2), 48(3), 68(3), 134(4)): **$10,000**. s 128
- **E193** Regulations on lifts and escalators: up to **$20,000 or 12 months or both**; other regulations: **$5,000**. s 136(4)
- **E194** Composition cap: lower of half the maximum fine and $5,000. Max $10,000: **$5,000**. Max $2,000: **$1,000**. Max $20,000: **$5,000**. Max $5,000: **$2,500**. s 131(1)
- **E195** Composition only for offences prescribed as compoundable; if not prescribed: **cannot compound**. s 131(1)
