# Source license

The text of **Maine Revised Statutes Title 35-A - Public Utilities** is published on the [Maine Revised Statutes, Office of the Revisor of Statutes (official)](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/).

- **Terms**: The Office of the Revisor of Statutes asserts no copyright anywhere on the statutes pages - there is no copyright line in the footer and no terms-of-use or permissions page; the only notices are a currency statement, a warning that the text may change without notice, and a disclaimer that the office cannot give legal advice. Combined with the government edicts doctrine (Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org, 590 U.S. 255 (2020)), under which the text of the Maine Revised Statutes as enacted is not copyrightable, both reuse and adaptation are permitted. Maine's official consolidation is produced in-house by the Revisor's Office and served free on the Legislature's own site, so there is no commercial-publisher layer over the statute text (the annotated print edition, Maine Revised Statutes Annotated, is a Thomson Reuters product and its annotations are separately copyrighted - use the Revisor's site, not the annotated edition).
- **Terms checked**: 2026-09-23, at https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/
- **From the terms**: "The text reflects changes made through the First Special Session of the 132nd Maine Legislature, and is current through October 1, 2025. The text is subject to change without notice."
- **Reuse and adaptation permitted, per the index check**: yes
- **Attribution required**: Maine Revised Statutes, Office of the Revisor of Statutes, Maine Legislature.
- **Source**: https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/35-A/title35-Ach0sec0.html

In the United States the text of a statute is not copyrightable: it is an edict of government (*Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org*, 590 U.S. 255 (2020)). A register's copyright notice can still cover a publisher's annotations, headnotes and numbering, which this corpus does not reproduce.
