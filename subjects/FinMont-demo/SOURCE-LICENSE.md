## Terms

Reuse of European Commission documents, including legislation published in the Official
Journal, is governed by **Commission Decision 2011/833/EU of 12 December 2011 on the reuse
of Commission documents** (OJ L 330, 14.12.2011, p. 39). Reuse is authorised free of charge,
including for commercial purposes, **provided the source is acknowledged** and provided the
reuse does not distort the original meaning.

The acknowledgement required by that Decision is carried in the repository
[`NOTICE`](../../NOTICE) file and in the header comment of every `.l4` module in this
subject.

## Authenticity

Only the electronic edition of the *Official Journal of the European Union* published on
EUR-Lex is authentic and produces legal effects, under Council Regulation (EU) No 216/2013
of 7 March 2013 on the electronic publication of the Official Journal.

**Nothing in this directory is authentic legal text.** The PDF this encoding was built from
is a copy; the `.l4` modules are an encoding of that copy, not a reproduction of it; and
neither is a substitute for the instrument on EUR-Lex. Where this encoding and the Official
Journal differ, the Official Journal governs, and the difference is a defect in this
encoding to be reported and fixed.
