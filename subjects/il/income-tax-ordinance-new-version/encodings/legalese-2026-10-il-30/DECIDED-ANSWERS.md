## A. Section 120B(d) and the Rounding Order 5746-1986

Reading adopted for every article that rounds to a multiple of N (articles 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12, 13).
The adjusted amount is first taken in whole new shekels, then cut down to the largest multiple of N that does not exceed it (the 'round amount', הסכום העגול).
The excess over the round amount is then tested: an excess under N/2 is disregarded (לא יובאו בחשבון), an excess of N/2 or more adds N (יווסף סכום של N).
The result is therefore the nearest multiple of N, with exact halves rounding up.
The word 'הקרוב' in sub-article (a) does not change this reading, because an excess is only meaningful against a round amount that lies below the adjusted amount.
A fractional excess cannot matter: N/2 is a whole number, so an excess of 59.99 against 60 and an excess of 59 against 60 both fall short.
Articles 3 and 9 round to the nearest whole shekel, with half a shekel and above counting as a shekel.
The published adjusted figures for any year are not in the deposited sources, so every case below states the unrounded adjusted amount as an input.
The Order took effect on 1 January 1986 (article 17, 'כ׳ בטבת התשמ״ו (1 בינואר 1986)').

## B. Section 88: relatives, material shareholder, capital-gain arithmetic, day counts

Relative (קרוב): clause (1) lists the spouse, sibling, parent, parent's parent, descendant and spouse's descendant, 'ובן זוגו של כל אחד מאלה'.
Clause (2) adds a sibling's descendant and a parent's sibling, and nothing else: the spouse-of limb sits inside clause (1) only.
In-laws other than those reached by 'spouse of each' are not relatives: a spouse's parent and a spouse's sibling are not listed.
For the s 97 exemption only clauses (1) and (2) count ('ואולם לענין פטור ממס לפי סעיף 97 יראו כקרוב רק את המנויים בפסקאות (1) ו־(2)').
Holding for clause (3) is at least 25% of any one kind of control means, directly or indirectly, alone or together with another.
Material shareholder: at least 10% of any one kind of control means, directly or indirectly, alone or together with another ('יחד עם אחר' means with a relative, or a non-relative who cooperates regularly under an agreement on material matters of the entity).
Indirect holdings are multiplied through the chain (the text says 'במישרין או בעקיפין' without a formula; flagged as an ambiguity where it matters).
Day counts: a period 'from the purchase day until the day before the Determining Date' counts the purchase day and ends on 31 December 2002, so it is (Determining Date minus purchase day) days.
A period 'from the purchase day until the sale day' includes both end days, so it is (sale day minus purchase day plus 1) days.
This pairing is the only one under which the three parts of the real gain add exactly to the whole when the sale falls on 31 December 2011; the text does not say it outright, so these cases carry M.
The Determining Date is 1 January 2003 ('מועד קובע'), the Change Date is 1 January 2012 ('מועד השינוי').

## C. Sections 91, with 121 and 126(a): the tax on capital gains

Scope of the individual scale: tax year 2026-2027 section 121(a) is 31% to 301,200, 35% to 560,280, 47% above; 121(b)(1) gives 10/14/20/31 to 301,200 to personal-effort income and to any income of an individual aged 60 or more.
A capital gain is not personal-effort income, so for an individual under 60 the gain is taxed on the 121(a) scale: its own layers are all at 31% or more, so the cap of 25% (30%) binds and the rate is flat.
For an individual aged 60 or more the 121(b)(1) scale applies to the whole taxable income and the gain, stacked on top ('כשלב הגבוה ביותר'), pays each layer at the lower of the layer's rate and the cap.
Mixed personal-effort income plus a gain for an individual under 60 is a genuine ambiguity (which scale prices the layers the gain occupies); the cases avoid it except where flagged.
The surtax of s 121B (3% above 721,560, and 2% more on capital income) is outside this row; every case keeps the total taxable income below 721,560, or states that surtax is excluded.
Where the purchase day is before the Change Date (1 January 2012), s 91(b1) splits the real gain by days (see section B for the day counts): up to the Determining Date at the s 121 rates without the 25% cap; from the Determining Date to the Change Date at the s 121 rates up to 20% (25% for a material shareholder selling securities); after the Change Date at the s 91(b) rates.
The order in which the three parts stack among the gain's layers is not stated; cases keep all layers within one bracket, or are flagged.
The taxable inflationary amount ('סכום אינפלציוני חייב') is only the inflation up to 31 December 1993, and only for assets bought before then; inflation after 1993 is neither taxed nor deductible.
The index for 31 December 1993 is not in the sources, so the case supplies it.
Year-count readings (flagged M): s 91(f)(1) '1% for each year from 1949 to the year of purchase' is counted as purchase year minus 1949 (a 1949 purchase adds 0); s 91(f)(2) 'from 2005 add 1% for each tax year or part' is counted as sale year minus 2004 (a sale in 2005 adds 1).

## D. Section 125B: tax on dividends

125B overrides 121 and 126: the dividend rate is fixed by the recipient and by the recipient's holding in the paying company.

## E. Section 125C: tax on interest and discount

For an individual, subsection (b) fixes 'not more than 25%' and treats the interest as the highest layer; subsection (c)(1) fixes 15% for interest on an asset that is not linked to the index.

## F. Section 122: residential rent

An individual may elect to pay 10% of the gross rent from a dwelling in Israel instead of tax under s 121, if the rent is not business income under s 2(1); no deductions or credits are allowed against the income or the tax (122(c)).

The income-tax exemption under the separate 1990 law has a ceiling that is not in the deposited sources: cases that depend on it are refusals.
Payment is due within 30 days of the tax year end unless advances were paid under s 175 (122(a1)).

## G. Section 8(c) and (d): spreading of arrears, leave redemption and grants

The spreading is by the taxpayer's request ('לפי בקשת הנישום או יורשיו'), so a case without a request has no spreading.
(c)(1) arrears of pay or pension go to the years they relate to, but not more than six tax years ending in the year of receipt.
(c)(2) leave redemption is spread in equal annual parts over a period of not more than six tax years ending in the year of receipt, and not more than the employee's working years.
(c)(3) a grant or commutation (paras (5) or (6) of 'personal-effort income') is spread in equal annual parts over the working years for which it is paid, or the period in which the pension right arose, but not more than six tax years ending in the year of receipt.
Where arrears relate to more than six years the text does not say where the older years' share goes; the cases that need it are flagged.
Working years are taken as whole years; fractional years are not decided by the text.
The (d) provision for death of the taxpayer or the start of winding up is said to be unfinished in the encoding: those cases are expected to be refused.
The Director's power to allow another period (para (3) proviso, (a), (b)) is discretionary and not decidable.

## H. Section 64A1: the transparent company

The opening note says the section applies 'מיום כניסתן לתוקף של תקנות על פי סעיף זה'.
No regulations are in the sources, so whether any company is a transparent company on a given date is not decidable from them: that question is a refusal.
All other cases below are conditional on the section being in force and test the conditions and the arithmetic.
All eight conditions of the definition must hold.
Number of shareholders: not more than 50, with spouses and their children, heirs of a shareholder, and involuntary purchasers each counted as one.
The notice to the assessing officer must be given within 60 days of incorporation: the day of incorporation is day 0.
On a sale of a share the undistributed accumulated profits of the benefit years (the years in which it was transparent) are subtracted, in the seller's proportion, from the consideration for the seller and from the original price for the buyer (b)(8)(a).
The losses attributed to the seller reduce the adjusted original price, up to its amount (b)(8)(c).

## I. Section 64A2: real-estate investment fund definitions

## J. Sections 57, 55(b), 60A(b)(2): kibbutz

A shared kibbutz ('קיבוץ שיתופי') pays the sum of the tax its members would pay if its whole taxable income were split equally among them as their only taxable income, applying ss 34-46A, 47 and Part D Chapter 3 except s 66, by family composition (s 57(a)).
The tax scale used is not stated for the kibbutz: income from business is not personal-effort income, so the 121(a) scale is read (31%, 35%, 47%); this is an ambiguity flagged in each arithmetic case.
A resident adult gets the 2 credit points of s 34 and the quarter point of s 36; a woman gets a further half point (s 36A); the credit point is 2,904 NIS in 2024-2027.
The cases using credit points are M or L confidence, because family composition and the other credits are outside the row.
Separate calculation (57(b)): the kibbutz may claim it if both spouses mainly do qualifying work, each one's work is needed for the kibbutz's income, and the kibbutz kept an exact record of every member's work.
55(b): a shared-kibbutz member's assessment on income he did not transfer to the kibbutz is outside the section, and the kibbutz's rates, credit points and losses are not taken into account.
60A(b)(2): the same for a renewed-kibbutz member's income that he did not report to the kibbutz.
'Member' of a shared kibbutz: a person on whom the bylaws impose the rights and duties of a member at the end of the tax year, including a candidate; of a renewed kibbutz the same, aged at least 21, other than a member in economic independence.

## K. Real Estate Taxation Law s 6(b), s 47 definitions

Betterment (שבח) is the amount by which the sale value exceeds the remaining purchase value (6(b)).
Remaining purchase value is the purchase value plus the permitted improvement deductions (ss 39, 39A) spent by the sale day plus the value of expenses paid after the sale day, less depreciation (s 47 'יתרת שווי רכישה').
Adjusted remaining purchase value: the remaining value excluding those added deductions and the later expenses, times the index at the sale day over the index at the purchase day, plus each expense times the index at the sale day over the index at its date (s 47).
Inflationary amount: the part of the betterment equal to the excess of the adjusted remaining purchase value over the remaining purchase value, capped at the betterment.
Real betterment: the betterment less the inflationary amount.
Day counts: the real betterment up to the Starting Day (7 November 2001), between the Starting Day (or the purchase day, if later) and the day before the Change Date (1 January 2012), and after the Change Date, follow the same pairing as section B: numerator days to the boundary exclusive of the boundary day, denominator days from the purchase day to the sale day inclusive of both.
'Tax year' in s 47 is defined as twelve months starting on 1 April; it is not clear that s 48A(d) uses that definition, so the year-count cases use sale dates in the second half of the year and one flagged boundary case tests the Jan-Mar ambiguity.

## L. Real Estate Taxation Law s 48A: the tax on betterment

Rates for an individual: 25% on real betterment (30% for an association operation by a material shareholder at the operation or in the 12 months before); 10% on the taxable inflationary amount; nil on both for the exempt part of a qualifying dwelling.
A company pays the s 126(a) rate of 23% on the real betterment (s 48A(a)).
For an individual whose purchase day was before the Change Date (1 January 2012) the real betterment is split by days into three parts (b1).
Part one (up to the Starting Day, purchase before 7 November 2001): 'בשיעור המס הגבוה ביותר הקבוע בסעיף 121', which is 47%.
AMBIGUITY: s 48B(b)(1) says the ITO s 121 rates apply in the income-tax assessment, i.e. the marginal layered rate, so the figure may be lower in the assessment than the 47% payable under the RE Tax Law; the cases state the 47% reading and the alternative.
Part two (Starting Day to the Change Date): the s 121 rate up to 20%.
Cases use a seller whose marginal rate is 31% or more, so the part is at 20%.
Part three (after the Change Date): subsection (b)(1) or (1a): 25% or 30%.
A dwelling that is a 'qualifying dwelling' (s 49(a)) bought before 1 January 2014 (the Transition Day) takes the (b2) track instead of (b1): the real betterment up to the Transition Day is exempt, the balance is taxed at 25%.
The taxable inflationary amount is 10% in general (c)(1) and 0% in the (b2) track (c)(2).
Subsection (d): ceiling on old assets: the 12% base plus 1% per year of purchase from 1949 for 1949-1960; for a company or liquidation recipient (d)(2) add 1% per tax year from 2005 to the sale year; for others (d)(3) add 1% for 2011, a further 1% for 2012 and then 2% for each year from 2013 to the sale year; if the result exceeds the ordinary rate of (a) or (b) the ordinary rate applies.
Year counts: the same readings as section C (purchase year minus 1949; sale year inclusive).
(d)(4): no ceiling for rights acquired under s 70 or Part E2, without consideration, in an exchange under Chapter 5C, from a relative, or that were business inventory converted to fixed assets.
Subsection (e) (spreading) and its cross-reference are said to be unfinished in the encoding: those cases are expected as refusals or no entry point.

## M. Real Estate Taxation Law s 9: purchase tax on residential dwellings

Purchase tax on a dwelling is by slices of the purchase price.
The bands in the deposited text for sales from 1 August 2013 are annotated by tax year from 16 January: the table has columns for 2013 (nominal), 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025-2027.
Three tracks for a purchase on or after 1 August 2013 (s 9(c1c)): (1) the general scale 5/6/7/8/10 for a buyer to whom (2) does not apply; (2)-(3) the single-dwelling scale 0/3.5/5/8/10 for a resident individual with a sole dwelling, or who sells the former sole dwelling within 24 months, or a replacement dwelling; (c1f) the additional-dwelling scale 8% to 6,055,070 and 10% above for purchases up to 31 December 2026 where (c1c)(2) does not apply.
For purchases from 1 January 2027 (after the period of (c1f), which the text note says was extended to 31 December 2026) the general scale (c1c)(1) returns, with the amounts the text marks 'adjusted to 2025'.
The base index date in the source is garbled (a future date), so the 2027 scale is flagged.
Amounts for a purchase on 16 January 2025 or later: single-dwelling bands 1,978,745; 2,347,040; 6,055,070; 20,183,565, which are also the 2024 amounts and are frozen for 2025 to 2027.
Amounts for years 2014 to 2021, and for 2028, are not in the deposited sources: expected refusals.
Non-dwelling land: the rate is set by the Minister's regulations, not in the sources: refusal.
A couple (excluding a spouse who lives separately permanently) and their children under 18 (excluding a married child, or an orphan) count as one buyer.
