# Source license

The text of **Minnesota Statutes, Chapter 504B - Landlord and Tenant** is published on the [Minnesota Office of the Revisor of Statutes - Minnesota Statutes (official publisher)](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/).

- **Terms**: No copyright is asserted over Minnesota Statutes on the register. The chapter and section pages carry no copyright notice, there is no terms-of-use or copyright page (/copyright/ returns 404), and the 'About Minnesota Statutes' page addresses only official/authentic status, not reuse. The Revisor's office is the official publisher (not a commercial publisher), and current Minn. Stat. ch. 3C contains no copyright provision - only s. 3C.13, which makes certified volumes prima facie evidence, and s. 3C.10, subd. 3, which permits contracting a commercial publisher for editing/printing of the print edition. Under the government edicts doctrine (Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org, 590 U.S. 255 (2020)) the statutory text itself is not copyrightable, so reuse and adaptation of the text are permitted. Treated as open. In the United States the text of a statute is not copyrightable: it is an edict of government (*Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org*, 590 U.S. 255 (2020)). A register's copyright notice can still cover a publisher's annotations, headnotes and numbering, which this corpus does not reproduce.
