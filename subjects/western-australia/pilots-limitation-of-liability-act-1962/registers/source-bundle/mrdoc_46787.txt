Western Australia
Pilots’ Limitation of Liability Act 1962
An Act relating to the liability of pilots.
1.	Short title
			This Act may be cited as the Pilots’ Limitation of Liability Act 1962.
2.	Interpretation
		In this Act, unless the contrary intention appears —
	pilot means a person who does not belong to, but has the conduct of, a ship;
	ship means a vessel that is not ordinarily propelled by oars only;
	vessel means a vessel used in navigation, other than air navigation, and includes a barge, lighter or like vessel.
3.	Limitation of liability of pilots
		Notwithstanding the provisions of any other Act or law, but subject to the Navigation Act 2012 (Commonwealth), a pilot is not liable for neglect or want of skill in piloting a ship beyond the amount of $200 together with the amount payable to him on account of pilotage in respect of the voyage in which he was engaged when he became so liable.
	[Section 3 amended: No. 113 of 1965 s. 8(1); No. 24 of 2023 s. 108.]
4.	Application of Act to the Crown
		This Act binds the Crown.
