# GLOSSARY — Bao Minh fire and special perils rules (row VN-20)

Every Vietnamese term below occurs verbatim in `../../source/raw/baominh-fire.txt` (checked by `tools/vnsrc.py check`).
"src" is the line of that file; "Điều" references use the document's own numbering (Roman Parts, Arabic clauses, lettered risks).
Identifiers are those in the `.l4` modules; `DAMAGE` in capitals renders the defined term "TỔN HẠI".
