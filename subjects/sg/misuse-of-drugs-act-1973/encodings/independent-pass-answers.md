# Independent pass: answers decided from the statute alone (MDA 1973, SSO current version at 1 Oct 2026)

Written before any .l4 file was opened. Source places: MDA1973.pdf / .txt; Second Schedule read from the rendered PDF pages (PDF pp. 117-126) and PyMuPDF text.
"band a" = rows (a) "not less than X and not more than Y"; "band b" = rows (b) "more than Y". "life/30" = "30 years or imprisonment for life".

Notes on the printed Schedule that bear on the brief:
- Row "8(b)" is "[Deleted by Act 1 of 2019]": consumption has NO row in the Second Schedule. Consumption is punished by s 33(3A) (1-10 years + fine up to $20,000, offences on/after 1 Apr 2019), s 33(4)/(4AA) (min 3 years), s 33A.
- Row "8(a)(9)" is the general possession row (max 10 years or $20,000 or both, subject to s 33(3B)).
- The sixth column for traffic/import/export has only a death band and a "not less than" band; there is no sixth-column band below the lower figure (falls back to the Class A/B/C columns, s 33(2)(a), (4D)).

## I. s 17 trafficking presumption ("more than": equal does NOT engage)
opium 100 / 100.1; morphine 3 / 3.1; diamorphine 2 / 2.1; cannabis 15 / 15.1; mixture 30 / 30.1; resin 10 / 10.1; cocaine 3 / 3.1; methamphetamine 25 / 25.1; ketamine 113 / 113.1; MDMA, MDA, MDEA combined 10 / 10.1 (combination counts: 5 + 5 = 10 does not engage; 5 + 5.1 engages). At figure: no presumption; above: presumed possession for purpose of trafficking, rebuttable on proof (balance) possession not for that purpose. Source s 17.

## J. s 18 / 18A
- J1 possession (custody, control) of anything containing controlled drug: presumed possession of drug (18(1)(a)); keys (b), keys of premises (c), document of title (d) likewise; rebuttable.
- J2 proved or presumed possession: presumed to know nature of drug (18(2)); rebuttable.
- J3 never had physical possession is not a rebuttal (18(3)).
- J4 two or more persons, one has it with knowledge and consent of the rest: deemed possession of each (18(4)) (deemed, not rebuttable presumption).
- J5 18A mirrors for psychoactive substances; 18A(2): presumed to know capacity for psychoactive effect and not to believe it an excluded substance.
- J6 s 19(1): pipe/syringe found in premises: presumed used for consumption; 19(2) person found in or escaping from such premises: presumed consuming. s 20 ship/aircraft: presumed imported with master's/captain's knowledge. s 21 vehicle: presumed possession of owner and person in charge. s 22 urine: drug in urine on BOTH tests: presumed consumed in contravention of 8(b) (or 11I(1)(b)); only one test positive: no presumption.
- J7 s 22A: prosecution need prove only capacity for psychoactive effect; burden of proving excluded substance on the asserter (balance). s 22B knowledge established by knowing capacity + not believing excluded.

## K. Offences made out / not made out
- K1 5(1): trafficking or offering or preparatory act, unauthorised: made out; authorised: not. 5(2): possession for purpose of trafficking is trafficking.
- K2 s 6 manufacture unauthorised; s 7 import or export unauthorised (import into Singapore); s 8(a) possession unauthorised; 8(b) consumption unauthorised.
- K3 s 9 possession of pipe etc intended for consumption, unauthorised. s 10 cultivation: cannabis, papaver somniferum, erythroxylon (no authorisation exception in s 10).
- K4 s 11: owner/tenant/occupier/person in charge permits or suffers premises to be used for consumption or trafficking/manufacture; a mere visitor is not.
- K5 s 11A: gathering of 2 or more persons, arranger knows drug is or is to be consumed or trafficked: made out even if he supplies none; gathering of 1: not.
- K6 s 11E: person 21 or over causes or procures young or vulnerable person to commit 5(1)/7 offence: age 20 not made out; age 21 made out.
- K7 s 11B(1): 21+, in possession of drug, leaves it exposed or unlocked and knows a child (under 16 for 11N; the Act's definition applies) has access: made out; 11B(2): permits young person to consume.
- K8 s 12 abetment/attempt: same punishment. s 13(e) aiding offence within Singapore from abroad: same punishment as the offence.
- K9 s 14: officer of body corporate with consent, connivance or neglect: guilty.
- K10 s 24: officer may enter and search without a warrant premises where he reasonably suspects drugs; s 25: arrest without warrant on reasonable suspicion; woman searched only by woman (25(3)); s 31(1): officer not below rank of sergeant requires urine specimen on reasonable suspicion of 8(b); a constable below sergeant may not; s 31(2): failing without reasonable excuse is an offence.
