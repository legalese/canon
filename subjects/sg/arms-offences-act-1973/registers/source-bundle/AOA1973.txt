Unlawful possession of gun with criminal intent

 3.—(1) A person commits an offence if —

       (a) the person carries a gun intending to —

               (i) injure, or endanger the safety of, another person;

              (ii) cause reasonable fear of the infliction of injury to
                   another person;

             (iii) destroy or damage any property; or

             (iv) facilitate another person to injure, or endanger the
                   safety of, another person, cause reasonable fear of
                   the infliction of injury to another person, or destroy
                   or damage any property, using the gun; and

       (b) the person is then in unlawful possession of the gun.

 (2) A person who is guilty of an offence under subsection (1) shall
on conviction be punished with imprisonment for a term of not less
than 5 years and not more than 10 years and shall also be punished
with caning with not less than 6 strokes.

 (3) However, where a person who is convicted of an offence under
subsection (1) is proved to have been previously convicted of a
scheduled offence, the person shall on conviction be punished with
imprisonment for a term of not less than 5 years and not more than 20

Aggravating circumstances for scheduled offence

 3A. Where a person is convicted of a scheduled offence, the person
shall be punished with imprisonment for life and shall also be
punished with caning with not less than 6 strokes if the person, at the
time of his committing or at the time of his apprehension for the
scheduled offence —

       (a) is carrying any gun; and

       (b) is in unlawful possession of the gun.

                                                                                 [Act 3 of 2021 wef 01/07/2025]

Using or attempting to use guns

 4.—(1) Subject to any exception mentioned in Chapter 4 of the
Penal Code 1871 which may be applicable (other than section 95),
any person who uses or attempts to use any gun intending to injure, or
endanger the safety of, another person, to cause reasonable fear of the
infliction of injury or to destroy or damage any property shall be
guilty of an offence and shall on conviction be punished with death.

                                                                                 [Act 3 of 2021 wef 01/07/2025]

 (2) In any proceedings for an offence under this section, any person
who uses or attempts to use any gun shall, until the contrary is proved,
be presumed to have used or attempted to use the gun intending to
injure, or endanger the safety of, another person, to cause reasonable
fear of the infliction of injury or to destroy or damage any property.

                                                                                 [Act 3 of 2021 wef 01/07/2025]

Using or attempting to use guns to commit scheduled offence

 4A. Subject to any exception mentioned in Chapter 4 of the Penal
Code 1871 which may be applicable (other than section 95), any
person who uses or attempts to use any gun at the time of his or her
committing or attempting to commit any scheduled offence shall,
whether or not the person has any intention to cause physical injury to
any individual or property, be guilty of an offence and shall on
conviction be punished with death.

Punishment for accomplices

 5. Where any gun is used by any person in committing or in
attempting to commit any offence or where an offence under
section 4A has been committed by any person, each of the person’s
accomplices present at the scene of the offence who may reasonably
be presumed to have known that the person was carrying or had in the
person’s possession or under the person’s control the gun, shall,
unless the accomplice proves that he or she had taken all reasonable
steps to prevent the use of the gun, be guilty of an offence and shall on
conviction be punished with death.

                                                                                 [Act 3 of 2021 wef 01/07/2025]

Trafficking in guns

 6.—(1) Any person trafficking in guns shall be guilty of an offence
and shall on conviction be punished with —

       (a) death; or

       (b) imprisonment for life and with caning with not less than
            6 strokes.

Consorting with person carrying guns

 7. Any person who consorts with, or is found in the company of,
another person who is unlawfully carrying or is in unlawful
possession of any gun in circumstances which raise a reasonable
presumption that he or she knew that that other person was carrying
or had in his or her possession or under his or her control any such gun
shall, unless he or she proves that he or she had reasonable grounds
for believing that that other person was not unlawfully carrying or
was not in unlawful possession of the gun, be guilty of an offence and
shall be liable on conviction to the like punishment as that other
person with whom he or she was consorting or in whose company he
or she was found.

Penalty for exhibiting imitation gun when committing
scheduled offence

 8. Any person who at the time of committing or attempting to
commit or abetting the commission of any scheduled offence exhibits
any imitation gun in a manner likely to put any other person in fear of
death or hurt shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on
conviction to imprisonment for a term which may extend to 10 years
and shall also be punished with caning with not less than 3 strokes.

                                                                                 [Act 3 of 2021 wef 01/07/2025]

Presumption

 9. Where any gun is found in or on any premises, the occupier of the
premises is deemed to be in possession of the gun unless the occupier
proves that some other person was in possession thereof, or the
occupier had no knowledge or reasonable means of knowing that the
gun was in or on the premises and that the occupier had taken all
reasonable precautions against the gun being kept in or on the
premises.

                                                                                 [Act 3 of 2021 wef 01/07/2025]

 10. [Repealed by Act 3 of 2021 wef 01/07/2025]

Saving of other written law

 11. The provisions of this Act have effect without affecting the
provisions of the Guns, Explosives and Weapons Control Act 2021 or
of any other written law for the time being in force in Singapore
relating to unlawful possession of guns or ammunition.

 12. The Minister may at any time by order add to, amend or vary
