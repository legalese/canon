## 1. What is encoded and what is not

**The whole document is in scope** (brief): the cover's approval chain, the table of contents, and Chapters 1 to 9, Articles 1 to 33.
Every Article is encoded or recorded as inert; nothing is out of scope or deferred.

Encoded as rules: the definitions that fix a date, an age, an amount or a class of event (1.2, 1.3, 1.6-1.9, 1.11, 1.16, 1.17, 1.21, 1.25, 1.29-1.32); eligibility and insurable interest (Article 2); the term (4); temporary insurance (5); signature, misstatement, incontestability, set-off, confidentiality and the duty to inform (6); every benefit (7.1-7.7) and both exclusion clauses (8.1, 8.2), combined into what a death or a disability pays; every policyholder request (9-13); premium modes, payment allocation, the allocation table, grace, lapse, the no-lapse guarantee and the monthly account step (14-16); the four charges (17-20); the free look, changes, misstatement of age or sex, reinstatement and termination (21-25); payees, documents, the claim time-bar and the duty to settle (26-29); the minimum-rate table and the crediting floor (32); and the forum and limitation period (33).
Two regulative rules (the free-look right, Article 21; the Company's duty to settle, Article 29) are exercised with `#TRACE`.

Inputs, never defaults: the sum insured, the premiums, the account values, the debts, the cost-of-insurance rates (not printed), the interest credited (its base and conversion are not stated), the Company's minimums and maximums "from time to time", any published increase of the 10-billion limits, and every fact a witness or a record would supply.

Not encoded, because the source sends the reader elsewhere: the Criminal Code (1.33), the Law on Insurance Business as the test of an "other person's" insurable interest (2.2, declined by name), the alcohol limit "theo qui định liên quan của pháp luật" (8.2, taken as a recorded fact), and "other cases provided by law" (25.2(e), a constructor with no further rule).

## 9. A note on the quote checker

`tools/vnsrc.py check` joins a Vietnamese word, up to two plain tokens, and a following Vietnamese word into one run.
An English word between two Vietnamese words is therefore swallowed into the run, and the run is not in the source: BRIEF.md line 23 fails this way, on an English "to" between two Vietnamese headings.
This deposit keeps Vietnamese terms apart with quotation marks or punctuation for that reason.
