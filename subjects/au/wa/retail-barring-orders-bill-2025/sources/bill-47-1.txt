                          LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY

           Retail Barring Orders Bill 2025

                                      A Bill for
An Act --
· to provide for orders to prevent people from committing retail

     violence or otherwise stalking or harassing retail workers by
     imposing restraints on their behaviour and activities; and
· to make related amendments to the Children's Court of Western
     Australia Act 1988 and the Courts and Tribunals (Electronic
     Processes Facilitation) Act 2013; and
· for related purposes.

The Parliament of Western Australia enacts as follows:

 2 1.   Short title

 3      This is the Retail Barring Orders Act 2025.

 4 2.   Commencement

 5      This Act comes into operation as follows --
 6        (a) Part 1 -- on the day on which this Act receives the
 7               Royal Assent;
 8        (b) the rest of the Act -- on a day fixed by proclamation.

 9 3.   Act binds Crown

10      This Act binds the Crown in right of Western Australia and, so
11      far as the legislative power of the Parliament permits, the Crown
12      in all its other capacities.

13 4.   Objects of Act

14      The objects of this Act are to --
15        (a) protect retail workers and other persons on retail
16               premises from retail violence; and
17        (b) protect retail businesses from the effects of retail
18               violence; and
19        (c) protect retail workers, while not on retail premises, from
20               stalking and harassment by persons who have
21               committed retail violence; and
22        (d) help ensure members of the public can be confident that
23               retail premises will be free from retail violence; and
24        (e) provide for a legally enforceable mechanism to prevent
25               retail violence; and
26        (f) hold persons who commit retail violence accountable for
27               their conduct.

8 6.        Retail violence

9      (1) A person commits retail violence if the person, while on retail

10          premises, engages in any of the following behaviour in relation

11          to another person on the retail premises --

12          (a) physical violence or abuse;

13          (b) sexual violence or abuse;

14          (c) threatening behaviour;

15          (d) stalking;

16          (e) harassing, intimidating or offensive behaviour.

17     (2) A person who procures another person to commit retail violence

18          is taken to have also committed the retail violence.

 2 10.  Making RBO

 3      A court may make an RBO against a person if --
 4        (a) the court is satisfied that --
 5                 (i) there are grounds for making the RBO under
 6                        section 11; and
 7                (ii) the terms of the RBO are the least restrictive of
 8                        the personal rights and liberties of the person as
 9                        possible that still achieve the objects of this Act;
10               and
11        (b) the person is an individual who has reached 10 years of
12               age.

13 11.  Grounds for making RBO

14      Grounds for making an RBO against a person exist if --
15        (a) the person has committed retail violence on the retail
16               premises stated in the RBO application; and
17        (b) the person is likely to --
18                 (i) commit retail violence on those retail premises
19                        again; or
20                (ii) otherwise stalk or harass a person who works as
21                        a retail worker on those retail premises;
22               and
23        (c) making the RBO is appropriate in the circumstances.

24 12. Matters to which court must have regard

25      (1) In considering whether to make an RBO against a person and

26      the terms of the RBO, a court must have primary regard to the

27      need to ensure that retail workers and other persons on retail

28      premises are protected from retail violence.

3 49.  Breach of RBO

4      A person who is bound by an RBO commits an offence if the

7 50. Breach of RBO with intention or knowledge of harm

8      (1) In this section --

9      mental harm includes --

10     (a) psychological harm; and

11     (b) suicidal thoughts.

12     (2) A person who is bound by an RBO commits a crime if the

13     person breaches the RBO and the person --

14     (a) intended their conduct to cause --

15     (i) physical or mental harm, including self-harm, to
