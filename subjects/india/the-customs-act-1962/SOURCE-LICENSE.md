## Consequence for this subject

Reproduction is permitted on the conditions above, but the terms do not expressly grant a right to ADAPT. An L4 encoding that quotes the statute is arguably an adaptation. **Confirm before depositing text or quoting it in an encoding** -- a human decision recorded here.

## Language

English is authoritative. Under Article 348(1)(b) of the Constitution, the authoritative texts of Acts of Parliament are in English. Under the Authoritative Texts (Central Laws) Act 1973 and s.5 of the Official Languages Act 1963, Hindi translations published under the President's authority are deemed authoritative Hindi texts. Constitutionally, though, the English text is the authoritative one. India Code's disclaimer (Wayback copy of disclaimer.jsp) says that in any discrepancy 'the English version of the Acts shall prevail'. Article 348 itself was cited from knowledge and not re-fetched this run.
