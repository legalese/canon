## 1. What is encoded and what is not

**Encoded: the whole document.** Decision 168/2024 (its date of signature, effective date and the decision it replaces), all six chapters of the rules (Articles 1 to 31 and the fifteen coded additional clauses), and the appendix: every printed row of the injury table (1,136 rows, 819 of them with a figure), the table of rates for reduced sight, and the six special cases. The four answers a reader asks for are top-level rules: `Chapter II — the own-damage answer under …` (wrapped with the clauses by `the own-damage answer under … with the clauses, given …`), `Chapter III — the accident answer under …`, `the goods answer under … with the clauses`, and `Chapter V — the third-party answer under …`. Each says either why the claim is not covered (naming the articles) or what is payable, step by step.

**Inputs, not encoded.** What the document leaves to the policy or certificate: the period, the sums insured and limits, the premium, a deductible above the minimum, the additional clauses chosen, the countries named for BS05, the added equipment listed for BS10. What the document leaves to an assessment: the market value of the car, the cost of each repair, the injury table row and the rate within its range, Tasco's choice of a rate inside a reduction band, the salvage value, the share of fault. What the document takes from the compulsory third-party scheme (Decree 67/2023, cited by the brief; the rules call it "bảo hiểm bắt buộc trách nhiệm dân sự của chủ xe cơ giới"): its limits, its table of rates, its exclusions. And the tariff ("Biểu phí", named on PDF page 59), which is not in the document.

**Declined by name (`REFUSE`).** A contract concluded before 15 November 2024 (governed by Decision 53/2024, which this row does not encode); a whole-car theft before the investigation is concluded or suspended; a traction battery 15 years old or more; an overload of a special-purpose car; a reduction band with no rate decided by Tasco; an assessed rate outside its table row; an own-damage loss the rules do not cover, under a policy that also carries an "other" clause (item 16) whose terms are not in the rules; a deadline whose starting event has not happened.

**What the document says about Decision 53/2024.** Article 2 of Decision 168/2024 says the decision takes effect on 15/11/2024 and replaces ("thay thế") Decision 53/2024/QĐ-BHTASCO of 22/05/2024 of Tasco's General Director. It says nothing else about it: no transitional rule for contracts concluded under it.

## 4. Findings

The hostile reading: defects in the instrument as written, not ambiguities resolved (those are §3). Each gives the source lines, a minimal scenario, and the evidence: an assertion in a tests module, or "reading only". Ordered by how much they matter to a policyholder.

**X-04. The notice duty excuses an objective obstacle; the 10% penalty for the same failure does not.** Art. 5.2.7(c) (src 402-405) requires written notice within 5 days "trừ trường hợp bất khả kháng, hoặc trở ngại khách quan". Art. 14.1.1(b) (src 815-820) cuts the payment by 10% for no written notice within 05 days "trừ trường hợp bất khả kháng hoặc đã được Bảo hiểm Tasco giám định thiệt hại", with no objective-obstacle exception. Scenario: the owner, kept from writing by an objective obstacle, sends no notice in 5 days; the duty was met, and the payment is still cut. Evidence: `tasco-tests-general.l4` asserts the duty met (`Article 5.2.7(c) — the duty to give written notice was met` … `conduct with an objective obstacle and no notice`); `tasco-tests-own-damage.l4` asserts the collision pays 26,950,000 instead of 30,000,000 on the same conduct. Article 24.1.1(b) (src 1147-1153) repeats it for goods. `LAW:` see F-30: the Law excuses both.

**X-19. The general exclusions reach passengers and third parties, who control none of them.** Art. 10 (src 617-680) applies to Chapters II to V; Art. 19.2 (src 1009) and 30.3 (src 1258) import it. Scenario: a passenger is killed in a collision; the car's inspection certificate had expired, or the driver ran a red light. Nothing is paid under the passenger accident cover. Evidence: `tasco-tests-liability-and-accident.l4`, the two assertions under "Findings shown as answers"; and for third parties, the assertion with `circumstances with blood alcohol of 51`.

**X-07. No exclusion in Article 10 asks whether its circumstance caused the loss.** Src 621-680 has no causal words ("do", "gây ra") in items 4 to 11. Scenario: a car parked where parking is prohibited burns from an electrical fault. Excluded by 10.8. Evidence: `tasco-tests-own-damage.l4`, `a fire` with `circumstances with parking where prohibited` is `own damage not covered` (10.8); the same fire in clean circumstances pays 30,000,000.

**X-17. Theft-of-parts cover (BS04) on a policy shorter than 12 months can never pay.** Src 1330-1333, confirmed on PDF page 25: "Hợp đồng bảo hiểm dưới 12 tháng: 0 vụ." A buyer who adds BS04 to an 11-month policy pays its premium for no possible claim. Evidence: `BS04 — the part thefts allowed under` a policy ending on 8 January 2026 is 0 (`tasco-tests-own-damage.l4`).

**X-13. A stolen car is not a total loss until the police conclude or suspend the investigation, and the claim must be made within a year of the theft.** Art. 15.2.1(b) (src 970-971) makes the theft a total loss only on that decision; Art. 9.1 (src 598-601) bars every claim a year after the loss, except time lost to force majeure or an objective obstacle; Art. 7.1.3(c) (src 510-523) requires the decision to prosecute in the file. A police investigation that runs past a year leaves the owner to argue that the wait was an objective obstacle. Evidence: the claim before the decision is declined (`tasco-tests-own-damage.l4`, `REFUSED … not a total loss until the investigation is concluded or suspended`); the time-bar is reading only.

**X-06. The reduction bands give Tasco discretion with no criteria.** Art. 14.1.2 "từ 20% đến 50%" and 14.1.3 "từ 50% đến toàn bộ" (src 824, 842; again 1157, 1175). Scenario: the car was moved before Tasco consented. Whether 20% or 50% comes off is Tasco's choice; the rules give no factor. Evidence: the claim is declined until a rate is recorded (`REFUSED … Tasco has not determined a rate within the band of Article 14.1.2`); with 30% recorded it pays 20,850,000; with 60% it is declined as outside the band.

**X-03. "Xe kinh doanh" decides the depreciation column and is never defined.** Src 907, 932. A car 1 to 3 years old is depreciated 0% if not a business car and 10% if it is. Article 1 defines neither. Evidence: the same collision pays 30,000,000 for a private car and 28,500,000 for the same car `used for business` (`tasco-tests-own-damage.l4`).

**X-15. Article 29's formula pays without regard to what the compulsory insurance pays; Article 28 confines the cover to the excess.** Src 1222-1242. Scenario: death of a third party, owner fully at fault, compulsory limit 150 million, owner paid 200 million. Art. 29 alone: 100% × 100 million × 100% = 100 million; Art. 28: only 50 million lies above the compulsory limit. The encoding pays the lesser (F-25); a reader of Art. 29 alone expects twice as much. Evidence: `tasco-tests-liability-and-accident.l4`, 50,000,000.

**X-16. The table's special case 6 cuts the death benefit of Article 20.1.** Art. 20.1 (src 1023-1024) pays the whole sum insured on death. Special case 6 (src 4128-4130) pays only the burial and identification costs where the victim is unidentified or has no lawful heir. Art. 20 does not refer to it. Evidence: death benefit 100,000,000, or 30,000,000 (the burial costs) without an heir (`tasco-tests-liability-and-accident.l4`).

**X-12. Refunds on termination are asymmetric, and one has no insured-event exception.** Art. 3.1 (src 227-228) and 3.2.2 (src 269-271): no refund once an insured event has happened. Art. 3.2.3 (src 273-276): when Tasco ends the contract it refunds 100% of the pre-tax premium for the time left, with no such exception. Evidence: half a year left: Tasco's termination refunds 6,000,000; the policyholder's 4,200,000, or nothing after a claim (`tasco-tests-general.l4`).

**X-05. The one-year claim bar runs from the loss even for liability covers.** Art. 9.1 (src 598-601). A third party may claim against the owner more than a year after the accident (Chapters IV and V); the rules' bar has already run. `LAW:` F-10. Reading only (the date rule is tested in `tasco-tests-general.l4`).

**X-01. No deadline for Tasco's own verification.** Art. 4.2.5 (src 304-312): where Tasco cannot verify, the file is complete only when the authority concludes; after 90 days Tasco must verify itself "và xem xét giải quyết bồi thường", with no period. Art. 4.2.4's 15 or 30 days run only from a complete file. The payment clock can be stopped indefinitely. Evidence: reading only; `Article 4.2.5 — the day from which Tasco must verify the file itself` gives the start, nothing gives the end.

**X-02. The claim file is "one or more" documents, and Tasco decides which.** Art. 7 (src 471-473): "Đối với từng vụ việc cụ thể thì hồ sơ bồi thường bao gồm một hoặc nhiều loại tài liệu sau". With X-01, whether the file is complete, and so whether the payment period has begun, is Tasco's call. Evidence: reading only; `Article 7 — the documents the items name for` lists what the items' own conditions name.

**X-11. The battery depreciation table stops at 15 years.** Src 938: its last row is "từ 10 năm đến dưới 15 năm"; the parts table has "từ 15 năm trở lên". A battery 15 years old has no rate. Evidence: `REFUSED … no row for a traction battery 15 years old or more`.

**X-14. A stolen car found later is wholly Tasco's, even when Tasco paid only part of its value.** Art. 16.2.1 (src 984-985) gives Tasco only the insured proportion of an under-insured car after a total loss; 16.2.3 (src 991-992) gives it the whole of a stolen car that is found. Evidence: `tasco-tests-own-damage.l4`, 0.75 against 1.

**X-08. Article 10.10 names no basis for measuring the overload of a special-purpose car.** Src 674-678 names goods vehicles (load), passenger cars (persons) and vehicles carrying both; the special-purpose cars of Art. 1.7.4 (a crane truck) are none of these. Evidence: `REFUSED … names no basis for measuring the overload of a special-purpose car`.

**X-09. The speeding band and the speeding exclusion overlap at 50%.** Art. 14.1.2(c) (src 834-835) reduces "từ trên 20% đến 50%"; Art. 10.11 (src 680) excludes "50% … trở lên". At exactly 50% the reduction can never apply (F-21). Evidence: 90 km/h in a 60 zone is not covered (`tasco-tests-own-damage.l4`).

**X-10. BS12 adds nothing on a literal reading.** Art. 11.1.5 (src 693-696) already covers malicious damage by anyone but the insured parties; BS12 (src 1478-1486) covers vandalism in riots and excludes bombs, which 11.1.2 (fire, explosion) does not. Evidence: riot damage pays 30,000,000 without BS12 (`tasco-tests-own-damage.l4`).

**X-18. The injury table has three names.** Art. 20 calls it "Bảng tỷ lệ trả tiền bảo hiểm thương tật về người" (src 1029); its own title (PDF page 30) "BẢNG TỶ LỆ TRẢ TIỀN BỒI THƯỜNG THƯƠNG TẬT"; special case 5 "Bảng quy định trả tiền bồi thường thiệt hại về sức khỏe, tính mạng" (src 4124-4125), the name of the compulsory scheme's table, which Art. 29.1 (src 1229-1232) uses for that other table. Read literally, special case 5 points at the compulsory scheme's table. Reading only. [page-read p.30] (the title)
