## 1. What is encoded and what is not

**Encoded: the whole document.**
The insuring clause, its definition of "Tổn hại" (Damage) and its proviso; all twelve numbered exclusion blocks (1 to 11, with two numbered 10), every limb and every write-back; General Conditions 2 to 15 (General Condition 1 is an interpretation rule, carried inert).
The three layers the brief asks for are kept apart: cover (`baominh-par-cover.l4`, `baominh-par-exclusions.l4`), amount (`baominh-par-quantum.l4`), and duties (`baominh-par-conditions.l4`), joined only in `baominh-par-claim.l4`.
Two duties with deadlines are regulative rules (`DEONTIC`): General Condition 9's claims procedure and General Condition 7's appointment of a second arbitrator.
Bare time-bars and dates (GC 3, 4(b), 8(b), 15) are functions returning a date or a boolean.

**Inert, with a reason** (section 2): the title and page footers, General Condition 1 (an interpretation rule), exclusion 3's severance sentence, the last sentence of exclusion 5 (it withdraws liability cover the document never grants), exclusion 11 Part 3 (it switches off an "event" concept the document does not otherwise use), the procedural remainder of General Condition 7 (umpire, death of an arbitrator, costs), the sentence of General Condition 11 that no act is an election, and the closing note.

**Inputs, not gaps.** Everything the document leaves to the Schedule ("Bản tóm lược hợp đồng bảo hiểm") is an input with no default: the period, renewal periods, the items and their sums insured, the total sum insured, the limits of liability, the deductible, the annual premium and the premium for the period, and what has already been paid in the period.
So is every fact a loss adjuster records: the chain of causation of the Damage to each item, its valuation, its circumstances, what the Insured said, the changes under GC 8, and the Insured's conduct.
Where a rule needs one of the conditionally needed facts and the record lacks it, the rule refuses, naming it (eight such refusals; for example "the actual value of the item at the time of the loss is not in the record").
Two more refusals mark where the document itself is silent or a branch cannot be reached: the burden of proof outside exclusion 3(a)-(c), and a short-period scale with no row for a period (unreachable on the printed scale).

**Not encoded.**
The Law on Insurance Business 08/2022/QH15 (an aid, not in scope): where it fills or overrides the document, the fork register says so with a `LAW:` tag and the Law's line in `.aids/law-08-2022-qh15.txt`, and the encoding follows the document.
The document states no figure of its own except periods (7 days, 30 days, three months, 2 months) and the short-period percentages; every amount in the tests is a scenario value and says so.
No source outside `source/raw/` was needed or fetched.

## 4. Findings

The hostile reading, as a policyholder's lawyer and as the insurer's.
Each finding gives the source lines, a minimal scenario, and the evidence: an assertion that passes because the encoding, faithful to the text, gives the surprising answer, or the words "reading only".
Assertions are in `baominh-par-findings.l4` under a `§§` heading with the finding's number, unless another module is named.

**1. Property insured elsewhere is excluded, so the contribution clause never applies.**
Exclusion 8(i) excludes "các tài sản đã được thu xếp bảo hiểm theo cách khác" (src:168); General Condition 6 (src:298-306) pays a rateable proportion of a loss to property that other insurance also covers.
Scenario: stock worth 500,000,000, insured here and elsewhere for 500,000,000 each, burns.
Evidence: GC 6 would pay 50,000,000 of 100,000,000; the claim answers `nothing is payable because` "exclusion 8".
The two clauses cannot both operate: whichever governs, the other is dead text.

**2. One late notice, or one omitted precaution, defeats the whole claim.**
The insuring clause makes every condition a condition precedent (src:9-11); GC 9(a) asks for written notice "ngay lập tức" (src:337-340), which the document never quantifies; GC 12 asks for "mọi biện pháp đề phòng tổn thất" (every measure to prevent loss, src:401-402).
Scenario: a covered fire of 100,000,000 (payable 90,000,000 after the deductible); the same fire with notice judged not immediate; the same fire with one unrelated precaution not taken.
Evidence: 90,000,000; then `nothing is payable because` the two GC 9(a) reasons; then `nothing is payable because` GC 12.
The Law's softer rule for late notice is fork F28.

**3. The second arbitrator can be appointed on one day only, and the award is not final.**
GC 7 (src:310-314): each party appoints "sau 2 tháng" (after two months) from receiving the other's request, and a party that has not appointed "trong vòng 2 tháng" (within two months) loses its choice to a sole arbitrator named by the other side.
Scenario: the Insured receives the request at month 0 and appoints at month 1, 2 or 3.
Evidence: the three `#TRACE` directives under `§§ General Condition 7` in `baominh-par-tests-conditions.l4`: month 1, a nullity ("before the window opened at 2"); month 2, `FULFILLED`; month 3, `PARTY the Insurer MAY appoint an arbitrator in writing`.
Read with "sau" strictly, no day complies at all (fork F21).
The same sentence opens with "Nếu hai bên không nhất trí với quyết định của trọng tài" (if the parties do not agree with the arbitrator's decision), so either party can reopen a sole arbitrator's award by disagreeing with it (reading only).

**4. The premium warranty has no limb for a short policy as written.**
GC 15 1(a) covers a period of insurance of 30 days or more (src:423); 1(c) speaks of "thời hạn thanh toán phí bảo hiểm dưới 30 ngày" (a premium payment period under 30 days, src:443), which is the very period the clause is defining.
Scenario: a 20-day policy.
Evidence: reading only; the encoding reads 1(c) as the period of insurance (fork F26) and asserts the result in `baominh-par-tests-conditions.l4` (`§§ General Condition 15`).

**5. The Insured must mitigate at once, and nothing says who pays.**
GC 9(a)(i) requires "tất cả các biện pháp cần thiết để giảm thiểu tổn thất" immediately (src:338); GC 5 puts subrogation acts at the Insurer's cost ("bằng chi phí của Bảo Minh", src:292) and GC 9's last paragraph and GC 11 put documents and plans at the Insured's own cost (src:349, 390-391), but the mitigation clause is silent, and the insuring clause pays only the value of Damage.
Evidence: reading only. LAW art 51(3) (aid line 991-996) obliges the insurer to pay necessary and reasonable costs of preventing and limiting loss as the contract agrees: the document records no agreement.

**6. Cover ends without any link to the loss.**
GC 8 (src:320-333): if the building is left unattended for 30 days or more, or the Insured's business changes, or its interest passes otherwise than by will or by law, the policy ceases to attach to the property unless the Insurer endorsed the change before the loss; no causal connection with the later loss is asked for.
Scenario: the building is unattended for 30 days, then an earthquake damages it.
Evidence: `nothing is payable because` "General Condition 8: ...". With 29 days the cover stands (`baominh-par-tests-conditions.l4`).
GC 8(b) also uses a different test, "không có người trông coi" (unattended), from exclusion 1(c)(vi)'s "bỏ trống hoặc không được sử dụng" (empty or not in use, src:57-58), so a building in use but unguarded, or guarded but empty, falls under one and not the other (reading only).

**7. The pollution exclusion is garbled.**
"Tổn hại phát sinh từ ô nhiễm hoặc nhiễm bẩn loại trừ (trừ phi đã bị loại trừ theo cách khác) phá hủy, hoặc tổn hại cho tài sản được bảo hiểm gây ra bởi" (src:120-121) is the standard clause with its words out of order; read as it stands, it does not say what is excluded and what written back.
Evidence: reading only; resolved as fork F8.

**8. The short-period scale's rows overlap.**
"Đến 3 tháng" (up to 3 months) and "Từ 3 đến 6 tháng" (from 3 to 6 months) both contain 3 months, and likewise 6 (src:278-280): a cover ended at exactly 3 months is charged 30% or 60%.
Evidence: each generated row's upper edge equals the next row's lower edge (two assertions); fork F20 takes the lower charge.

**9. The two lists of perils written back do not match.**
Exclusion 6's list (src:135-139) and exclusion 11's (src:233-237) render overlapping perils in different words ("biểu tình" and "bạo loạn"; "đâm va với các phương tiện giao thông hoặc súc vật trên đường" and "xe cộ đâm va"; "bão biển, bão lụt" and "giông bão, ... lốc xóay") and each has perils the other lacks (hail, falling objects, volcanic eruption, tsunami, freezing on the second only; water damage on the first only); exclusion 10's write-back has fire and explosion alone.
Scenario: a computer damaged by a riot, or by hail.
Evidence: both excluded by exclusion 6; the same computer damaged in a demonstration is not.

**10. The numbering is defective.**
Two exclusions are numbered "10." (src:173, 177); exclusion 3 has "d)" without its parenthesis (src:90); exclusion 11 is divided into "Phần 1-3" (src:216, 238, 245) and lettered "a., b."; GC 15 restarts numbering at "1." (src:418) inside condition 15; and the write-back of 1(c) numbers its limbs "(i)" and "(ii)" (src:60, 62), the same marks as the exclusion's own items, so "trừ khi (i)" can be read as a reference to theft.
Evidence: reading only. A citation to "exclusion 10" is ambiguous.

**11. Read literally, exclusion 11 Part 2 takes out the cost of replacing any damaged computer.**
Part 2, "Cho dù có phần 1 nêu trên" (notwithstanding Part 1), excludes costs "liên quan đến việc thay đổi hoặc bổ sung bất kỳ hệ thống máy điện toán" (relating to changing or supplementing any computer system, src:239-244), with no link to a date change.
Scenario: fire destroys the office computers; replacing them is a change to a computer system.
Evidence: reading only; the encoding takes the narrow reading (fork F12).

**12. Terms the document relies on are undefined or used inconsistently.**
"sự kiện" (event) in exclusion 11 Part 3 (src:247) has no other use, so Part 3 switches off nothing; "tổn thất" (loss), the unit of the deductible (src:405), is undefined, so how several items or several days of damage group into one loss is not stated (fork F31); the defined "Tổn hại" alternates with lower-case "tổn hại", "thiệt hại" (src:167, 413) and "tổn thất"; the Schedule is named three ways (section 2, row 3; nouns module); the insurer is "Bảo Minh" except three times "Người bảo hiểm" (src:361, 420, 453), never defined; and the document calls itself "Quy tắc bảo hiểm", "Đơn bảo hiểm" and "hợp đồng bảo hiểm" by turns.
GC 1 says a word given a special meaning keeps it everywhere (src:257-259): the document does not give most of its key words one.
Evidence: reading only.

**13. The electronic-data exclusion has lost its causal link in translation.**
Exclusion 10(a)(i) excludes data loss "hoặc việc mất công dụng, giảm chức năng, chi phí các loại" (or loss of use, reduction in functionality, costs of any kind, src:183-184) with nothing tying those to the data: the standard clause's "resulting therefrom" is missing.
Read literally, any loss of use, reduced functionality or cost, whatever its cause, is excluded.
Evidence: reading only; the encoding ties them to a data matter in the chain.

**14. Money confirmed as insured is not covered against burglary.**
Exclusion 6(a) covers confirmed money and valuables only for the listed perils (src:127-130), and theft is not among them, though exclusion 1(c)(i) itself allows burglary with force from the insured building (src:48-49).
Scenario: a safe broken open inside the insured building.
Evidence: `excluded by` "exclusion 6".

**15. The write-back of exclusion 1(c) is harder to meet than that of 1(a).**
1(a) writes back the part of the Damage from a cause not excluded (src:34-36); 1(c) asks for that "và" (and) that the excluded loss itself resulted from Damage by such a cause (src:59-63).
Scenario: a machine damaged both by a latent defect and by fire, or by a breakdown and by fire.
Evidence: the first is apportioned, the second wholly excluded, for the same fire.

**16. Tanks and pipes are excluded property.**
Exclusion 8(d) excludes Damage to "bể chứa" and "đường ống" (src:157-158), rendered reservoirs and pipelines, but the same words are the tanks and pipes whose bursting exclusion 6 writes back (src:138-139) and whose leaking 1(c)(vi) excludes (src:57).
Scenario: a pipe or a water tank in the factory, damaged by fire.
Evidence: `excluded by` "exclusion 8" for both kinds (translation-dependent; GLOSSARY.md).

**17. An innocent and unconnected omission takes the property out.**
GC 2 (src:262-268) removes cover for property "bị ảnh hưởng bởi" (affected by) a false statement or an omission of a fact on which the risk was assessed, with no intent and no link to the cause of loss.
Scenario: a fact about the building was omitted; lightning strikes it.
Evidence: `nothing is payable because` "General Condition 2: ...". LAW art 22(2) requires intent (fork F18).

**18. The sum insured is used up by earlier losses, but average still counts it in full.**
The proviso caps liability over the whole period (src:20-23) and nothing reinstates the sum insured after a loss; GC 14 (src:412-416) still compares the value with the full sum insured.
Scenario: 900,000,000 already paid on the building; a second fire of 500,000,000 to a building worth 1,200,000,000.
Evidence: average gives over 416,666,666; 100,000,000 is paid. The Insured is penalised for under-insurance on cover it no longer has.

**19. An "all risks" policy excludes Damage from an unknown cause.**
Exclusion 1(c)(iii) lists "không rõ nguyên nhân" (unknown cause, src:51); the write-back needs a cause not excluded, which an unknown cause cannot supply.
Scenario: stock found damaged, cause unknown.
Evidence: `excluded by` "exclusion 1(c)". The Insured must prove the cause of the loss to recover under "all risks".

**20. The Insurer may cancel at will.**
GC 3 (src:270-272) lets the Insurer cancel at any time on 7 days' notice by registered letter.
Evidence: reading only. LAW art 26 (aid line 623-635) lists the grounds on which an insurer may end a contract unilaterally, and this is not one of them; LAW art 20(1)(c) (aid line 452-453) gives the insurer termination only under art 26.

**21. One fraudulent claim forfeits every benefit under the policy.**
GC 4(a) (src:283-286): "Tất cả quyền lợi bảo hiểm theo hợp đồng bảo hiểm này sẽ bị bãi bỏ", all benefits under the policy, not only the fraudulent claim.
Evidence: reading only; the encoding models one loss at a time, so it cannot show the effect on another claim.

**22. The time limits are short, and one is missing.**
GC 9(b) gives 30 days to file the claim (src:343); GC 4(b) three months to sue after a rejection (src:287-288); the Insurer has no deadline to decide or pay.
Evidence: reading only. LAW art 30 (aid line 706-714) gives one year to file, and art 31 (aid line 718-724) 15 days to pay; neither is applied here (forks F24, F34).

**23. The Insurer's powers over the property come with no standard.**
GC 10 (src:361-379) lets the Insurer take possession of, sell or dispose of the property "không làm phát sinh trách nhiệm" (without incurring liability), and forfeits every benefit if the Insured does not comply with "các yêu cầu của Bảo Minh" (the Insurer's requirements) — any requirement, with no word of reasonableness, while the Insured may not abandon the property (src:380-381).
Evidence: reading only (the forfeiture itself is asserted in `baominh-par-tests-conditions.l4`).

## 9. What was not done, and notes on the tooling

- **The independent test pass** was not run: the brief is one session with no sub-agents.
  Every expected value was decided from the source text before its assertion was run, by the same session that wrote the rules.
- **HG1**, a human who knows Vietnamese insurance law reading the modules against the source, has not been sought.
- **No source was fetched**; the Law on Insurance Business was read only from the aid file, and no other law (the Civil Code, the Maritime Code) was read, so nothing is said about them.
- **Import graph.** Each module imports only the one before it (imports are transitive). With every rule module importing every earlier one, typechecking the claim tests took about 29 seconds; as a chain, about 10. Nothing in the rules changed.
- **Shared scratchpad.** This session's scratchpad directory was shared with other encoders running at the same time; this row's scratch work was kept in its own subdirectory (`enc-vn-08/`), which holds the template expander, the scale generator and the mutation copy.
