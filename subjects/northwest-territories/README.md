# northwest-territories/

Northwest Territories (Canada) legislation, one subject-id per body of law, following the subject-sidecar shape described in [`subjects/README.md`](../README.md).

The subjects here were laid out on 2026-09-23 for the legislation governing twelve everyday topics. Each was checked against the Legislation of the Northwest Territories - the consolidated statutes and regulations service of the Legislation Division, Department of Justice, Government of the Northwest Territories; the check is recorded in each subject's `registers/source-bundle/*.meta.json`. No statute text has been deposited and nothing is encoded yet.

**Licence category: unclear.** **Metadata only until resolved.** No human has recorded a licence decision for this jurisdiction in `tools/topics/round3/licence-decisions.json`. The register's own terms, quoted above, are the evidence to decide on.

## Register access

Fetched over plain HTTPS with an honest, self-identifying user agent ('canon-legislation-indexer/1.0 (legislation metadata indexing; michael.fairweather@gmail.com)'). No block, no bot challenge, no rate limiting, no CAPTCHA - every request returned 200. About 14 requests in total, spaced roughly 2 seconds apart: the A-Z index page (one page, 739 KB, carrying all 239 consolidated Acts plus their regulations as a file-browser list), both paginated halves of the site's terms of use (/en/terms-of-use/ and /en/terms-of-use/page/2/), the 'Acts of the Northwest Territories - Administering Departments' table (dated October 13, 2025), and the consolidated PDFs of the nine Acts listed below plus the Official Languages Act. The register publishes each consolidation only as a PDF; there is no per-Act HTML page. The index shows, for each consolidation, a 'Modified Date' - the date of that consolidation - and each PDF's cover page prints the Act's chapter citation, its commencement, and the full chain of amending chapters with their in-force dates. The site's own disclaimer states that 'These consolidations are updated monthly and may not be the most current version available' and that the consolidations 'have been prepared for convenience of reference only, and are not an official statement of the law'; the authoritative text is the Revised Statutes of the Northwest Territories, 1988 and the Annual Volumes of the Statutes of the Northwest Territories. CanLII was not used (it refuses this network).

## Topics with no legislation here

- **Passenger compensation**: Federal, not territorial. Air carriers and passenger compensation are governed by the Canada Transportation Act, S.C. 1996, c. 10 and the Air Passenger Protection Regulations, SOR/2019-150, administered by the Canadian Transportation Agency; interprovincial and international rail and bus carriage is likewise federal. The register's Motor Vehicles Act and Public Airports Act regulate vehicles and airport operations, not compensation for delayed or denied carriage.
- **Customs/duties**: Federal and exclusive. Constitution Act, 1867, s. 91(2)-(3) reserves trade and commerce and the raising of money by customs duties to Parliament; the operative statutes are the Customs Act, R.S.C. 1985, c. 1 (2nd Supp.) and the Customs Tariff, S.C. 1997, c. 36. A territory cannot legislate customs duties.
- **Work permits/immigration**: Federal. Entry, status and work authorisation are governed by the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act, S.C. 2001, c. 27 and the Immigration and Refugee Protection Regulations, SOR/2002-227. The Northwest Territories Nominee Program operates administratively under a federal-territorial immigration agreement; there is no territorial immigration statute on the register.
