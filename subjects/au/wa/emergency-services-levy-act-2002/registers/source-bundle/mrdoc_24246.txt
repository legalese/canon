Western Australia
Emergency Services Levy Act 2002
An Act to impose the emergency services levy determined under the Fire and Emergency Services Act 1998.
	[Long title amended: No. 22 of 2012 s. 120.]
1.	Short title
		This Act may be cited as the Emergency Services Levy Act 2002.
2.	Commencement
		This Act comes into operation on the day on which section 15 of the Fire and Emergency Services Legislation (Emergency Services Levy) Amendment Act 2002 comes into operation.
3.	Emergency services levy imposed
		The emergency services levy determined under Part 6A of the Fire and Emergency Services Act 1998 is imposed.
	[Section 3 amended: No. 22 of 2012 s. 121.]
