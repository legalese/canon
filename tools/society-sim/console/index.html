Cradle to grave: Simone Ng

One sunny-day life, 2003 to 2088, played forward through the encoded statute book. Every event is an act by somebody; every consequence names the row that produced it, or the requirement it raised when no row could.

Nature mode. Actors appear on their date of birth and age against the calendar. Nothing here is set by hand: the clock is the only control, and the law is read from the encodings as they stood on . Mock-up: adjudications are replayed from the scenario's facts file, not evaluated live in L4.

What happened

What this is. A mock-up of the society console, made from the LQA scheme console's parts. The world, the clock, the cast and the chapter navigation are the real design; the adjudications are read from facts.json, which records for every event the rows it engages and the figures they should produce. In the working console those come from L4, live.

Taint. Where an event engages law that has no row, or a row whose scope stops short, the console shows the event tainted and the requirement the ledger holds for it. The population does not wait for the law to be written.

What it is not. Nobody in it is real, nothing in it is advice, and no row it names has been through a human gate.
