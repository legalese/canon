Evidence Act 2025
Evidence Act 2025 Commencement Proclamation 2026
Made by the Governor in Executive Council.
1.	Citation
This proclamation is the Evidence Act 2025 Commencement Proclamation 2026.
2.	Commencement
The Evidence Act 2025, other than Part 1, section 384 and Part 12 Divisions 1, 8 and 17, comes into operation on 18 September 2027.
C. DAWSON, Governor	L.S.
A. BUTI, Attorney General
