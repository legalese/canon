# nunavut/

Nunavut (Canada) legislation, one subject-id per body of law, following the subject-sidecar shape described in [`subjects/README.md`](../README.md).

The subjects here were laid out on 2026-09-23 for the legislation governing twelve everyday topics. Each was checked against the Nunavut Legislation website (Consolidated Law of Nunavut) - Legislation Division, Department of Justice, Government of Nunavut; official consolidations are published by authority of the Nunavut Territorial Printer under the Legislation Act; the check is recorded in each subject's `registers/source-bundle/*.meta.json`. No statute text has been deposited and nothing is encoded yet.

## Register access

Fetched over plain HTTPS with an honest, self-identifying user agent ('canon-legislation-indexer/1.0 (legislation metadata indexing; michael.fairweather@gmail.com)'). The site is Drupal 10 behind Cloudflare. About 37 requests in total, spaced roughly 2 seconds apart, and all but one returned 200: the home page, the 'Welcome to the Nunavut Legislation website' page (which carries the reproduction terms), the 'Current Consolidated Statutes and Regulations' index and its per-letter pages (?title=C,E,H,I,L,N,O,P,Q,R,S,U), the per-Act pages for the nine Acts below, five 'Notice under subsection 65(2) of the Legislation Act' PDFs, and the consolidation PDFs themselves (served from /en/file-download/download/public/<id>). ONE BLOCK to record: the letter-W page, https://www.nunavutlegislation.ca/en/consolidated-law/current?title=W, returned a Cloudflare interstitial ('Just a moment...', noindex/nofollow, Turnstile challenge) instead of the listing. I did not retry it and did not attempt to solve the challenge; nothing in my final selection depends on a W-letter Act (the Workers' Compensation Act was therefore not checked). Everything else was reachable. The register gives, for each Act, an English page plus Francais and, for some Acts, Inuktitut and Inuinnaqtun versions; the consolidation PDF's cover page prints the C.S.Nu. chapter citation and the consolidation date, and where the consolidation has been overtaken the Act's page carries a red 'Note: This consolidation is not up to date. See subsequent amendments: ...' with links to the amending chapters. CanLII was not used (it refuses this network).

## Topics with no legislation here

- **Passenger compensation**: Federal, not territorial. Air carriage and passenger compensation are governed by the Canada Transportation Act, S.C. 1996, c. 10 and the Air Passenger Protection Regulations, SOR/2019-150, administered by the Canadian Transportation Agency. This bites harder in Nunavut than anywhere else in Canada - every community is fly-in - but it remains entirely federal; the register's Public Highways Act and Motor Vehicles legislation do not touch compensation for delayed or denied carriage.
- **Customs/duties**: Federal and exclusive. Constitution Act, 1867, s. 91(2)-(3); Customs Act, R.S.C. 1985, c. 1 (2nd Supp.) and Customs Tariff, S.C. 1997, c. 36. A territory cannot legislate customs duties.
- **Work permits/immigration**: Federal. Immigration and Refugee Protection Act, S.C. 2001, c. 27 and the Immigration and Refugee Protection Regulations, SOR/2002-227 govern entry, status and work authorisation. Nunavut has no territorial immigration statute and, unlike the other territories, has historically not run a provincial/territorial nominee program of its own.
