# Comparables — Decree 67/2023/NĐ-CP, the Motor Vehicle Insurance Fund and the insurer's duties (row VN-27)

One row per parameter, for the later cross-insurer analysis.
The Vietnamese is verbatim from the gazette text; "src line N" is line N of `../../source/raw/nd67-congbao-1017-1018.txt`.
"not stated" means the decree does not state it; where the Law on Insurance Business fills it, NOTES.md §3 (LAW forks) says so, and nothing here is filled from that Law.
Parameters the decree states in Articles 5 to 13 (term, who is insured, limits, exclusions, premium, claim file) are row VN-10's; its `COMPARABLES.md` is the reference, and this table repeats only those this row's rules depend on.
The two vintages agree on every row: Decree 220/2026 amends none of the provisions below.
