Western Australia
Reserve (Kwinana Freeway) Act 1975
An Act to excise portion of Reserve No. 21288 for the Kwinana Freeway.
1.	Short title
		This Act may be cited as the Reserve (Kwinana Freeway) Act 1975.
2.	Reserve No. 21288
		Reserve No. 21288 classified as of Class A and set apart for “Park Lands” is hereby amended by excising an area of 0.511 5  hectare as delineated and coloured pink on Lands and Surveys 2 Miscellaneous Plan No. 736 and the area of the reserve is reduced accordingly.
