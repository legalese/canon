# Encoding brief: ITO ss 48 and 48A, the Area order of 5755-1995, the foreign worker Regulations of 5775-2014, in L4

This brief is the specification for row IL-28 (run `IL-28-20261008`), written by the encoder `enc-il-28`.
The scope was pinned from the lead's task message and BACKLOG row IL-28 before any L4 was written; the coverage table was finished once the sources had been read.

## The subjects

**Income Tax Ordinance [New Version]** ss 48 and 48A (Part C, Chapter Three, the last two sections).
s 48 lets the Minister of Finance, with the Knesset Finance Committee's approval, order that ss 34, 36 and 37 apply to residents of the Area who are not Israeli citizens as if they were Israeli residents.
s 48A lets the Minister determine that the chapter's credits do not apply to a foreign worker, or apply partially on conditions, even if he counts as a resident.

**The Order** (Income Tax (Credits for Residents of the Area) Order, 5755-1995, K.T. 5755 p. 1356, signed 1 March 1995): one section applying ss 34, 36 and 37 to residents of the Area who are not Israeli citizens.

**The Regulations** (Income Tax (Credits for a Foreign Worker) Regulations, 5775-2014, K.T. 5775 p. 14, amended K.T. 5777 p. 472 and 5778 p. 2656): reg 1 definitions, reg 2 "legal foreign worker", reg 3 the credits (the substance), reg 4 start (income of tax year 2015 onwards).

**The Foreign Workers Law, 5751-1991**, s 1 only: "עובד זר" is a worker who is not an Israeli citizen or a resident of Israel.
The rest of that Law (permits, levies, deposits) does not bear on credit points and is not encoded.

## What the row delivers

For a person who is a resident of the Area (not an Israeli citizen) or a foreign worker: the credit points that replace those of ss 34, 36, 36A and 37, which provisions of Chapter Three the instrument disapplies or leaves alone, and the NIS amount before set-off given the year's credit-point value.
The refusals in rows IL-01, IL-02 and IL-08 that name ss 48 and 48A are replaced by these answers (NOTES.md, "Replacing the earlier refusals").
