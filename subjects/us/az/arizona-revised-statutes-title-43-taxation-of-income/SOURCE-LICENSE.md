# Source license

The text of **Arizona Revised Statutes, Title 43 - Taxation of Income** is published on the [Arizona State Legislature - Arizona Revised Statutes](https://www.azleg.gov/arsDetail/).

- **Terms**: Not open as the register presents it: every page carries '© 2026 Arizona State Legislature. All Rights Reserved', with no terms-of-use or licence page and no permission to copy or adapt. The register also disclaims being the official text - it says the online compilation is 'primarily maintained for legislative drafting purposes' and that 'The official version of the Arizona Revised Statutes is published by Thomson Reuters', i.e. the official code is a commercial publication under contract. Against that, the text of the Arizona Revised Statutes as enacted is a government edict and not copyrightable under US law (Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org, 590 U.S. 255 (2020)), so the statutory text itself can be reused and adapted; a Thomson Reuters copyright claim would reach only the publisher's annotations, headnotes, indexing and arrangement.
- **Terms checked**: 2026-09-23, at https://www.azleg.gov/arsDetail/?title=23
- **From the terms**: "This online version of the Arizona Revised Statutes is primarily maintained for legislative drafting purposes and reflects the version of law that is effective on January 1st of the year following the most recent legislative session. The official version of the Arizona Revised Statutes is published by Thomson Reuters."
- **Reuse and adaptation permitted, per the index check**: no, or not established
- **Attribution required**: No attribution formula is published. Courtesy citation: Arizona Revised Statutes, Arizona State Legislature.
- **Source**: https://www.azleg.gov/arsDetail/?title=43

In the United States the text of a statute is not copyrightable: it is an edict of government (*Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org*, 590 U.S. 255 (2020)). A register's copyright notice can still cover a publisher's annotations, headnotes and numbering, which this corpus does not reproduce.
