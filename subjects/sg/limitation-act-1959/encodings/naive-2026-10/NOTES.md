## Why this Act

**20 of the 527 Singapore Acts** deposited here cite it, and it sets the time limit
for every civil claim that has no period of its own. At 44,000 characters it is
encoded nearly whole; the detailed accrual rules for land are left out.

## What the Act turns out to say

### 1. Limitation is a defence the defendant must plead

s 4: "Nothing in this Act shall operate as a bar to an action unless this Act has been
expressly pleaded as a defence". An out-of-time claim succeeds against a defendant who
does not plead it. Asserted.

### 2. A written "I owe you" revives an expired debt

s 26(2): where a right to recover a debt "has accrued" and the debtor acknowledges it
or pays something, "the right shall be deemed to have accrued on and not before the
date of the acknowledgment or the last payment". s 27(1): the acknowledgment must be
**in writing and signed**. Nothing confines s 26 to claims still in time, and s 28(5)
and (7) expressly contemplate acknowledgments and payments made "after the expiration
of the period of limitation". A debtor who signs an acknowledgment in year 10 starts a
fresh six years; an oral one does nothing. Asserted.

### 3. The 15-year longstop can bar a claim before it exists

s 24B: negligence claims "shall not be brought after the expiration of 15 years from"
the negligent act, "notwithstanding that the cause of action has not yet accrued". A
building defect that first causes damage in year 16 never gives a claim. Within the 15
years, s 24A gives the later of 6 years from accrual (3 for personal injury) or 3
years from knowledge. Asserted.

### 4. No limitation against a fraudulent or property-holding trustee

s 22(1): no period applies to a beneficiary's action for a trustee's fraud, or to
recover trust property the trustee holds or converted. Other breach-of-trust claims:
6 years. Asserted.

### 5. The periods

Contract and tort, 6 years; personal injury, 3 (from accrual or knowledge); a
judgment, 12; a statutory penalty, **1**; contribution between wrongdoers, **2** from
the judgment or settlement (s 6A); land, 12 (not against a registered Land Titles
proprietor, s 9(2)); a deceased's estate, 12; rent arrears, 6. Asserted.

### 6. Smaller things worth recording

- **s 24:** a claimant under a disability when the right accrued has 6 years (3 for
  personal injury) after it ends; never more than 30 years for land.
- **s 29:** fraud, concealment by fraud, and mistake postpone the start until
  discoverable with reasonable diligence; a bona fide purchaser keeps the property.
- **s 33(2):** the Act does not apply to Government recovery of taxes or duties.
- **s 35:** 15 February 1942 to 30 September 1949 is excluded from every period.
