# Source license

The text of **Kentucky Revised Statutes Chapter 205 - Public Assistance and Medical Assistance** is published on the [Kentucky Revised Statutes, Legislative Research Commission (official)](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/).

- **Terms**: The register asserts blanket copyright in its footer ('Copyright Kentucky Legislative Research Commission / All rights Reserved') and the statute pages add that catchlines and headings are not part of the law. The formal Disclaimers page is 403 to scripted access, so the full terms could not be read. Under the government edicts doctrine (Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org, 590 U.S. 255 (2020)) the text of the Kentucky Revised Statutes as enacted is not copyrightable and is freely reusable and adaptable; what the LRC can plausibly claim is its editorial apparatus (chapter titles, centred headings, section catchlines, explanatory and annotation notes), which the site itself says 'do not constitute any part of the law'. Marked not-open because the register makes an unqualified all-rights-reserved assertion that a human should resolve before relying on it.

In the United States the text of a statute is not copyrightable: it is an edict of government (*Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org*, 590 U.S. 255 (2020)). A register's copyright notice can still cover a publisher's annotations, headnotes and numbering, which this corpus does not reproduce.
