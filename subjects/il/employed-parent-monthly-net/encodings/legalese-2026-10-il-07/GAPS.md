Line numbers are lines of the two deposited sources: `ITO` is `../../../income-tax-ordinance-new-version/registers/source-bundle/income-tax-ordinance-new-version.he.wiki.txt`, `NII` is `../../../national-insurance-law-consolidated-version-5755-1995/registers/source-bundle/national-insurance-law-consolidated-version-5755-1995.he.wiki.txt`.

## Gaps the brief listed that the capstone did not meet

- **ITO s 121A**: repealed; its heading is ITO line 4452 and its only text, at line 4453, is the editorial "(בוטל)". Row IL-03 records it. Nothing for the capstone to need.
- **NII s 67A**: there is no section 67A in the deposited consolidation. "67א" occurs only in the table of old and new section numbers at NII line 5326, where it maps the old s 67A to the present s 108. Checked by `grep -n '67א'` over the file on 2026-10-07.
- **NII s 65 beyond what IL-06 took**: IL-06 encodes s 65(a) and (b) in full; the capstone needed nothing more of it.
- **ITO ss 38 and 39**: excluded by s 66(c)(2) in a separate calculation, so reached only through s 65 (item 3).

## Not a gap but a fork

Some places where the capstone declines are forks, where the text does not decide, rather than missing provisions; they are in `NOTES.md` section 4: whether credit points are set off against the s 121B additional tax (K3), and which tax year's additional-tax liability excludes a parent from the allowance (K5, IL-06's open fork F5).
