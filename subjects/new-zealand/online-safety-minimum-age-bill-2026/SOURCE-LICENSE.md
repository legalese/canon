`.l4` module and in `registers/source-bundle/LMS1592864.meta.json`, so that a reader can trace
any encoded rule to the sentence it encodes.

## Note for NOTICE

Unlike every other subject in this corpus to date, this one adds no attribution obligation to
the repository's [NOTICE](../../../NOTICE) file. New Zealand is neither an open-licence case
(cf. Western Australia, CC BY 4.0) nor a permission-grant case (cf. Singapore, SSO Terms of
Use cl.13): the material is simply outside copyright, in the same way United States federal
material is under the edicts-of-government doctrine.
