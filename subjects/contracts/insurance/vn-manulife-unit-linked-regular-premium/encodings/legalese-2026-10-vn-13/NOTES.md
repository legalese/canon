## 1. What is encoded and what is not

**Encoded: the whole document**, Articles 1 to 20, Annex 1 and Annex 2, every clause given a disposition in section 2.
The mechanics the brief names are functions with worked tests: the account value and surrender value (Article 1), unit prices (11.2), unit allocation of premiums and top-ups (9.1, 9.2, 13.1), the monthly deduction and its sharing among funds (13.3, 13.6), switches and withdrawals (11.5, 11.6, Annex 2 D, E), surrender (12), the loyalty bonus and its units (Article 1, 3.3), and what a death claim pays, which runs through Articles 3, 6, 7, 8, 9.4, 18 and 20 (`what the Company pays on`, in `vn13-art03-benefits.l4`).
The three layers of the brief: whether a death is covered (in force, time limit, Articles 6, 7, 8, 3.2), how much is paid (3.1, 3.2, 6, 7, 8, less grace-period charges), and what each party must do by when (free look, grace and lapse, reinstatement, notices, claims and interest, suit), the last partly as regulative rules with `#TRACE`.

**Inputs, never defaults.** Everything the document leaves to the Policy Schedule or an endorsement (sum assured, premium, frequency, rider premium, allocation, every charge level, the risk premium rate) is an input. So are the Company's records (unit prices, net asset values, holdings, dates) and its calendar (working days). So are the minimums and maximums Annex 2 relies on but never states (minimum top-up, withdrawal, switch, balance and sum-assured change; maximum number of withdrawals); that they are stated nowhere is a finding.

**Declined by a named refusal** where the document does not answer: what follows a first-year surrender-value shortfall, when riders end on an unnotified delay from the second year, the premium after a holiday, a closed fund's units without a request, the minimum payment period of Annex 2 C, the default split of a switch, the time a switch takes effect, the interest rate on late claims, the payee of a death benefit with no beneficiary, transaction receipt dates before 8:30 or on a non-working day or with a transaction pending, the effect date of a sum-assured change approved on or after the paid-to date, an equity band for a later fund, and any case whose valuation the caller has not supplied.

**Not encoded.** Nothing in scope is out of scope. Not encoded as rules: the Law on Insurance Business (an aid; where it bears on a clause it is a `LAW:` fork, section 3), the riders (named in the document but not part of it; their premiums and benefits are inputs), and the Company's standard mortality table (an input to the 13.9 d cap).

Modules: `vn13-nouns.l4` (DECLARE only), `vn13-art01-02-definitions.l4`, `vn13-art03-benefits.l4`, `vn13-art04-08-conditions.l4`, `vn13-art09-10-premiums.l4`, `vn13-art11-12-funds.l4`, `vn13-art13-charges.l4`, `vn13-art14-20-general.l4`, `vn13-annex1-funds.l4`, `vn13-annex2-procedures.l4`; `vn13-fixtures.l4` (named cases, no directives); tests `vn13-tests-cover.l4`, `vn13-tests-mechanics.l4`; evidence for section 4, `vn13-findings.l4`.

## 4. Findings

The hostile reading. Evidence is the module and assertion that shows the answer, or `reading only`. A fork (section 3) is an ambiguity resolved; a finding is a defect in the instrument as written. All figures are from the fixtures' hypothetical scenario.

1. **The premium debt is deducted twice.** src 30-32 makes the surrender value the account value less "các khoản nợ phí"; 3.1 adds that surrender value (ii, src 145-147) and subtracts the premium debt again (v, src 154); 3.2 does the same (src 165-168). Scenario: debt 2,000,000. Evidence: `vn13-findings.l4`, Finding 1: the death benefit as written is 757,300,000 against 759,300,000 with the debt taken once; 3.2 pays 207,000,000 where 209,000,000 is the surrender value. Article 18 (src 761-763) then deducts "các khoản phí khác" too: a third deduction is arguable (reading only).
2. **The offence exclusion has no subject.** src 156-158. Read literally, a death caused by anyone's offence (a murder, a drunk driver) pays only the surrender value. Scenario: the Life Insured is murdered. Evidence: Finding 2: the literal rule excludes the death; the reading taken (F6) pays 757,300,000 instead of 207,000,000.
3. **A grace period that never ends.** src 368-377: 60 days "from the day the surrender value was most recently found not enough"; a shortfall is found again at every monthly deduction. Scenario: shortfalls on the 10th of each month from March 2028. Evidence: Finding 3: on 30 June 2029 the contract has ended on the reading taken (on 10 May 2028) and has not on the literal one, nor at any later day while shortfalls continue. For the Policyholder: free cover; for the Company: unbounded exposure.
4. **No rule for a first-year shortfall.** src 353-367: the first-year rule speaks only of an unpaid premium; the shortfall rule starts "from the second policy year". With a 90% initial charge (src 623) the first-year account is small, so premiums can be paid and the account still fail a monthly deduction. Evidence: Finding 4, `#ASSERT REFUSED` on a shortfall in June 2026.
5. **The beneficiary carries the market from the death to the claim; some charges after death are kept.** src 145-151: the surrender value is taken after the CLAIM date; only risk and rider premiums deducted between the death and the NOTICE come back. The policy fee after death is not refunded, nor is anything deducted between the notice and the complete claim. Evidence: Finding 5: the same death pays 757,300,000 or 768,300,000 by the claim date alone; the rest reading only.
6. **Avoidance is not restitution.** src 243-253 refunds charges collected plus the surrender value, not premiums; the spread, fund fees and investment losses stay with the Policyholder, and no intent is needed for avoidance (src 234-240). Evidence: Finding 6: 19,900,000 back on 20,000,000 paid with flat prices. `LAW:` Article 22(2) (law 543-551) asks intent and refunds premiums.
