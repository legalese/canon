# Source licence — AI-content labelling sources

The status differs by source, so each is recorded separately.
"Determined" means a publisher's statement, or a rule of law, was read and is quoted or cited here; "undetermined" means it could not be, and says why.

## European Commission documents — DETERMINED: CC BY 4.0, attribution required

`source/eu-code-of-practice-transparency-2026-06-10.pdf` and `source/eu-guidelines-art50-2026-07-20.pdf` are published by the European Commission.
The Commission's legal notice, read at https://commission.europa.eu/legal-notice_en on **2026-10-01**, verbatim:

> © European Union, 1995-2026 The Commission's reuse policy is implemented by the Commission Decision of 12 December 2011 on the reuse of Commission documents . Unless otherwise indicated (e.g. in individual copyright notices), content owned by the EU on this website is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) licence . This means that reuse is allowed, provided appropriate credit is given and changes are indicated.

Neither document carries an individual copyright notice to the contrary (not checked page by page beyond the title pages).
The attribution is recorded in the repository `NOTICE`.

## Official Journal texts — UNDETERMINED

`source/eu-ai-act-2024-1689-oj.xhtml` (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) and `source/eu-omnibus-2026-1744-oj.xhtml` (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744) were fetched from the Publications Office cellar.
EUR-Lex's own legal notice, which states the terms for Official Journal texts, could not be read on 2026-10-01: eur-lex.europa.eu served a bot-challenge page (HTTP 202, empty body) to every request.
No grant is recorded here because none was read.
The Commission notice above covers content on the Commission's site, not the Official Journal, so it is not relied on for these two files.
Canon's `NOTICE` entry for `subjects/eu/FinMont-demo` records Official Journal text as reused under Commission Decision 2011/833/EU. That decision's reach to Official Journal texts was not verified for this deposit, so the entry is pointed to rather than borrowed.

## Washington and California enactments — DETERMINED: not subject to copyright

`source/wa-e2shb-1170-session-law-2026.pdf` is a session law of the Washington State Legislature; `source/ca-sb1000-2026-enrolled.html` and `source/ca-ab853-2025-chaptered.html` are bills of the California Legislature as enrolled and as chaptered.
Under the government edicts doctrine, works created by legislators in the course of their official duties are not copyrightable (Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org, Inc., 590 U.S. 255 (2020)).

**What was not checked.** No terms page of `leg.wa.gov` or `leginfo.legislature.ca.gov` was read for this deposit. The conclusion rests on the doctrine, not on either site's terms, and is recorded so that nobody reads this file as saying more was checked than was.
