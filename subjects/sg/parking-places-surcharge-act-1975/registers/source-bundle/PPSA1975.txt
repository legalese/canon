An Act to provide for the imposition of surcharge on parking places.
                                                            [15 October 1975]

Short title
 1. This Act is the Parking Places (Surcharge) Act 1975.

Interpretation
 2. In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires —
      “designated area” means any area or place which the Minister
         may, by order in the Gazette, specify as being a designated
         area for the purposes of this Act;
      “motor vehicle” means a mechanically propelled vehicle
         intended or adapted for use on roads;
      “parking lot” means the space marked out in a parking place for
         the parking of one motor vehicle;
      “parking place” means any land or premises, or any part of the
         land or premises, owned by any person, statutory board or

      institution and used for the parking of 5 or more motor
      vehicles;

   “Superintendent” means the Superintendent of Car Parks or a
      Deputy or an Assistant Superintendent of Car Parks
      appointed under the Parking Places Act 1974;

   “surcharge” means a surcharge imposed in respect of any
      parking place.

Levy of surcharge

 3.—(1) There is to be levied a surcharge in respect of every parking
place in any designated area at such rate or rates as the Minister may,
by order in the Gazette, determine.

 (2) Such rate or rates must be on the basis of the number of parking
lots or otherwise as the Minister may determine.

 (3) The surcharge is payable at such time and in such manner as the
Minister may, by order in the Gazette, determine.

 (4) The surcharge is payable, without demand, at the office of the
Superintendent or any other prescribed place.

 (5) All surcharges required to be paid under this Act or any order
made under this Act are recoverable as a debt due to the Government.

 (6) Despite the provisions of any other written law, the
Superintendent must not refund any surcharge except in such
special circumstances as the Superintendent may approve.

Power to seek information

 4.—(1) For the purposes of enforcing the provisions of this Act, the
Superintendent may, and as often as the Superintendent thinks
necessary, require any person —

       (a) to submit or give to the Superintendent, within such period
            as the Superintendent thinks fit, any form, return or
            information; or

       (b) to answer any question raised by the Superintendent.

 (2) All forms and returns under this Act must be in such form as the
Superintendent may require.

 (3) Any person who fails to submit any form or return as required
under subsection (1), or who in any such form or return makes any
statement which is false in any material particular, shall be guilty of
an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding
$5,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months.

Power to compound offences

 5. The Superintendent may accept from any person who is
reasonably suspected of having committed an offence under this
Act or any of its orders or rules a sum of money not exceeding $500
by way of composition for the offence which may have been
committed.

Surcharges to be paid into Consolidated Fund

 6. All surcharges collected by the Superintendent must be paid into
the Consolidated Fund.

Exemption

 7. The Minister may exempt any parking place or part of any
parking place from all or any of the provisions of this Act.

Non-application

 8.—(1) The provisions of this Act do not apply to private parking
places used exclusively in connection with any private dwelling
house for the housing or parking of vehicles kept for private use only.

                                                                                                           [24/2018]

 (2) In this section, “dwelling house” means any building or
tenement, or any part thereof, which is used, constructed or
adapted for use for human habitation.

Rules

 9. The Minister may make rules for the purpose of carrying into
effect any of the provisions of this Act.
