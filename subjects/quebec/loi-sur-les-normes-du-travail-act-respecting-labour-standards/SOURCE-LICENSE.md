# Source license

The text of **Loi sur les normes du travail (Act respecting labour standards)** is published on the [LegisQuebec - Recueil des lois et des reglements du Quebec (Editeur officiel du Quebec / Publications du Quebec)](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/fr).

- **Terms**: Crown copyright, and the most restrictive of the four provinces. The Government of Quebec's own copyright page asserts exclusive intellectual-property rights over everything it produces, publishes or distributes and names statutes and regulations explicitly ('que ces documents soient des textes officiels ou administratifs, des lois et reglements'), then prohibits reproducing, downloading, storing, translating, adapting, publishing or publicly performing that content without prior authorisation. Adaptation is therefore expressly prohibited, not merely unaddressed - and so is plain reproduction. Authorisation is obtained through a request form that asks what use will be made of the work and who is to be invoiced, so a fee may attach; the stated turnaround is about 15 working days. Not an open licence.

## Language

French and English are both official. Section 133 of the Constitution Act, 1867 requires Quebec's Acts to be printed and published in both languages, and s. 7 of the Charter of the French language provides that the French and English versions of statutes and regulations are equally authoritative; LegisQuebec publishes both and the RLRQ chapter number is the same in each. French titles are given below with their official English equivalents. Neither text is subordinate to the other.
