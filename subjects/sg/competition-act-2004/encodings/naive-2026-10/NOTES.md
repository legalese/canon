**Method: naive.** Straight from the deposited text with the `writing-l4-rules`
skill and nothing else. No pipeline, no coverage table, no independent test

**Edition:** 2020 Revised Edition, informal consolidation. The deposit says it
incorporates amendments up to and including 1 December 2021; later amendments are

Act 13 of 2025 and Act 25 of 2021 also shown.

**Checks:** one case file, 54 assertions satisfied, 0 errors, 0 warnings.

## Why this Act, and why scoped

**4 of the 527 Singapore Acts** deposited here cite it. This row takes the rules a
business or its adviser meets: who Part 3 reaches (s 33(4), (5)), the three
prohibitions with their Third and Fourth Schedule exclusions (ss 34, 47, 54), the
ceiling on a financial penalty (s 69(3), (4)), whether an appeal suspends a decision
(s 71(3)), the investigation offences (ss 75, 77, 83) and private actions (s 86).
The Commission's constitution and finance, block exemptions, notification for
guidance or decision, commitments, investigation powers, the Appeal Board, the
content of s 69 directions, and the First and Second Schedules are not encoded.
Ministerial orders (Third Schedule paras 3, 4, 8; s 33(5)) are taken as given facts;
none was retrieved.

## What the Act turns out to say

### 1. Vertical agreements are excluded unless the Minister brings them back in

Third Schedule para 8: s 34 "does not apply to any vertical agreement, other than
such vertical agreement as the Minister may by order specify". Para 9 (net economic
benefit) excludes an agreement that improves production or distribution, or
promotes progress, unless it imposes restrictions that are not indispensable or
could eliminate competition in a substantial part. Neither para reaches s 47.
Asserted.

### 2. The Government is wholly outside Part 3; statutory bodies can be brought in

s 33(4) takes the Government, statutory bodies and those acting for them out of
Part 3. s 33(5) lets the Minister prescribe statutory bodies (and those acting for
them) back in; there is no such power for the Government. Asserted.

### 3. A dealer's temporary holding is not a merger, within a time limit

s 54(7)(d), (9), (10): a securities dealer holding temporarily, voting only to
arrange disposal within 12 months (or a longer period the Commission sets), does
not make a merger. Reading the purpose test as a limit on how long control has been
held is an inference. An approval under s 19 of the Significant Investments Review
Act 2024 does not take a merger out of s 54 (Fourth Schedule para 1(a), as amended
by S 18/2026). Asserted.

### 4. The penalty cap is 10% of Singapore turnover a year, for up to 3 years

s 69(4): no penalty may exceed "10% or such other percentage" of Singapore turnover
"for each year of infringement ... up to a maximum of 3 years", and s 69(3) allows a
penalty only for intentional or negligent infringement. Reading this as a per-year
multiple with constant turnover is an inference; whether an order prescribes
another percentage was not checked. Asserted.

### 5. Only a penalty appeal suspends

s 71(3): an appeal suspends the decision only if it is against the imposition or
amount of a financial penalty. Asserted.

### 6. A reasonable excuse does not excuse a failure to produce a document

s 75(2) gives the document defence only where the document was not in the person's
possession or control and compliance was not reasonably practicable; the reasonable
excuse defence (s 75(3)) is for information, answers and explanations. s 75(4)
removes the offence where the person imposing a requirement under s 61A, 63 or 64
did not act in accordance with that section; s 65 is not in that list. With no
penalty expressly provided, s 83 sets $10,000 or 12 months or both. Asserted.

### 7. Follow-on claims only, within 2 years, and not for cartel members

s 86: no action until a decision has established the infringement and the appeal
periods have run; none after 2 years from the end of those periods; and no party to
an infringing s 34 agreement has a right of action. Treating an action at exactly
24 months as in time is an inference on "after the end of". Asserted.
