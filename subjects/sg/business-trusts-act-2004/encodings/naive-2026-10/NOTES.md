## Why this Act, and why scoped

**20 of the 527 Singapore Acts** deposited here cite it. This row takes the
trustee-manager's duties and the unitholder's position. Registration, MAS directions,
officer indemnities, the trust deed's contents, the oppression and derivative actions,
meetings procedure, registers, accounts and the prescribed board composition are not
encoded.

## What the Act turns out to say

### 1. A director of the trustee-manager must put unitholders ahead of the director's own company

s 11(1)(b): a director must give priority to the unitholders "over the interests of the
trustee-manager", the company they direct. s 11(3) says this overrides the Companies Act
s 157 duty. s 11(4) bars any civil or criminal proceedings against the director for an
act s 11 required. A director who votes down the trustee-manager's own fee increase
cannot be sued by the trustee-manager. Asserted.

### 2. But unitholders cannot sue the director directly

s 11(6): "no action or proceedings whatsoever" may be brought by unitholders against a
director for breach of s 11, except through s 41 (oppression) or s 42 (derivative
action). The duty runs to the unitholders, but the remedy has to go through the court
procedures. Asserted.

### 3. A simple majority of votes cast removes the trustee-manager, whatever the deed says

s 20(1) applies "despite any provision in the trust deed", and s 20(2) voids a deed
clause that requires more or allows less. A special resolution (s 63: three-quarters of
votes cast, 21 days' notice) forces a winding up under s 45. Asserted.

### 4. Unitholders are never liable for the trust's debts

s 32: no liability beyond money the unitholder "expressly agreed to contribute", despite
the deed and despite winding up. A distribution needs a solvency statement signed by at
least 2 directors (s 33(1)). Once declared, the unitholder is a creditor for it (s 35).
A unitholder's own creditors cannot reach trust property (s 34). Asserted.

### 5. Smaller things worth recording

- **s 6:** the trustee-manager must be a company that is not an exempt private company,
  and must do nothing else.
- **s 12(19):** a director's "family" means spouse and children (including adopted and
  step-children). Parents and siblings are not included.
- **s 29:** a deed clause excusing negligence is void. A release by special resolution
  for specific past acts, or on the trustee-manager leaving office, is valid.
- **s 40A:** compulsory acquisition after 90% approval within 4 months, not counting
  units the bidder already holds.
- **s 40:** unitholder claims have a 6-year limit.
