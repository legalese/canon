# australian-capital-territory/

Australian Capital Territory legislation, one subject-id per body of law, following the subject-sidecar shape described in [`subjects/README.md`](../README.md).

The subjects here were laid out on 2026-09-22 for the legislation governing twelve everyday topics. Each was checked against the ACT Legislation Register (ACT Parliamentary Counsel's Office); the check is recorded in each subject's `registers/source-bundle/*.meta.json`. No statute text has been deposited and nothing is encoded yet.

**Licence category: open.** Statute text may be deposited and quoted in an encoding, subject to the attribution above.

## Topics with no legislation here

- **Termination/severance**: Commonwealth matter. Unfair dismissal, notice and redundancy pay are governed by the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth). The ACT does not legislate general termination rights. Payment of long service leave on termination sits under the Long Service Leave Act 1976, listed under Leave/holiday pay.
- **Pensions/social security**: Commonwealth matter: Social Security Act 1991 (Cth) and the superannuation guarantee legislation.
- **Government benefits**: Commonwealth matter: social security and family assistance legislation. ACT concessions are mostly administered under subordinate instruments or programs rather than a principal Act.
- **Personal tax/withholding**: Commonwealth matter: Income Tax Assessment Acts and Taxation Administration Act 1953 (Cth), which include PAYG withholding. The ACT has no personal income tax.
- **Passenger compensation**: Commonwealth matter: aviation passenger rights, the Civil Aviation (Carriers' Liability) Act 1959 (Cth), and the Australian Consumer Law.
- **Work permits/immigration**: Commonwealth matter: Migration Act 1958 (Cth).
