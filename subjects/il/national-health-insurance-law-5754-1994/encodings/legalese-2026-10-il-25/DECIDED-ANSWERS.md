# IL-25 independent decided answers: National Health Insurance Law 5754-1994, s 14

Author: fid-il-25, written from the Hebrew sources alone before opening the encoding directory.
Sources read: the Health Insurance Law s 14 and s 15 (lines 289-343 of the deposited wiki text), National Insurance Law (NIL) s 334(a), 341, 342, 345B, 348, 350, 350A, 351, 238, Schedule 10, Schedule 11, Schedule 16A, the definitions of average wage and basic amount (NIL s 1), the 1999 reduced-rates order, the 1985 rounding regulations.
Confidence: H = text is explicit, M = reasoned reading, L = text is silent or conflicting.
All amounts are NIS per month, computed exactly and shown to the agora (rounding is discussed at A9).

## Constants taken from the deposited text

- Rate above the reduced tier: 5.17% (s 14(b)(1), (b)(2), (c)(1), (c)(2)); the in-text note says 5% up to and including 2024 and 5.16% in January 2025.
- Rate on the reduced tier: 3.23% (s 14(f1), the paragraph numbered (ו1)), applying to contributions for January 2025 onward; the note says 3.1% up to 2024 under regulation 1 of the 1995 reduced-rates regulations (not deposited).
- Reduced collection threshold (NIL s 334(a), "מדרגת גבייה מופחתת"): 7,522 for 2025 and 7,703 for 2026, both stated in the NIL note; this is a figure in the sources.
- Minimum amount (s 14(a), "סכום המינימום"): 123 in 2026 (note in s 14(a)); no 2025 figure is deposited.
- Pensioner fixed amounts (s 14(e)(2)): 237, and 340 with a spouse supplement or a pensioned spouse, for 2026 (notes); no 2025 figure is deposited.
- Maximum income (NIL s 348(a) with Schedule 11 items 1 and 2): 5 times the basic amount per month; the basic amount in para (3) of its definition is 10,139 for 2025 and 10,382 for 2026 (note in NIL s 1), so the monthly cap is 50,695 for 2025 and 51,910 for 2026.
- Average wage 2026: 13,566 (note); not needed for the health contribution except as cross-check.

## Ambiguities (decided reading and the alternative)

- A1. Does the income cap apply to the health contribution? S 14 states no cap. S 15(a)-(b) applies the NIL "with the necessary changes as if they were national insurance contributions". I take the cap as applying (M). Alternative: uncapped; 60,000 wage in 2026 would give 2,952.56 instead of 2,534.31.
- A2. Does the 3.23% tier apply to a non-worker under s 14(c)(2)? The paragraph (ו1) names "the employer liable for an employee, an employee liable for himself, and a self-employed person liable under those subsections"; a person who is neither employee nor self-employed is not named. I take the literal reading: the non-worker pays a flat 5.17% with the minimum 123 (M). Alternative: reduced tier applies to non-workers too; income 10,000 gives 367.56 instead of 517.00.
- A3. Does the reduced tier apply to the persons paying under s 14(d) (benefits) and s 14(e1) (early pension, retirement grant, adjustment pension)? Subsection (ו1) overrides "(b) to (d) and (e1)" but names only the three payer classes. I take the tier as applying, because (d) says "at the rates at which an employer pays for his employee" and (e1) says "at the rates payable from the income of an employee under (b)", and (b) as overridden by (ו1) is the tiered rate (M for (d), L for (e1)). Alternative: flat 5.17%.
- A4. S 14(e)(4) says a person liable under subsection (e) is not liable under (b)-(d) and (ו1). I read it literally: a pensioner paying a fixed amount owes nothing on wages (L). Alternative: fixed amount plus contribution on earnings.
- A5. A person who is both an employee and self-employed: nothing in s 14 combines the two incomes. I take each capacity separately, each with its own reduced tier (L). Alternative: a single tier on combined income (NIL s 342(f) is the analogue for NIL).
- A6. February 2025 to December 2025 rate: the text states 5.17% and notes only January 2025 as 5.16%. I read 5.17% from February 2025 (M).
- A7. The temporary provision s 14(g2) (in the sources the paragraph numbered (ז2)) says income exempt under NIL s 350A(a) is also counted for subsections (b) and (c) from 1.1.2026 to 31.12.2035. I read "income" by the month of the income, not the month of payment (M). Alternative reading by collection date differs only around the boundaries of the period.
- A8. Whether the "first two months after service" exemption (NIL s 351(11)(2c), cited by s 14(g)) also needs income not above the minimum income of Schedule 11 item 3: the chapeau of s 351(11) says so; I apply it (M).
- A9. Rounding: the 1985 rounding regulations (reg 6(b)) round the total an employer deducts and quarterly advances to whole shekels. Whether the health contribution is rounded is not stated in s 14; I give exact figures and treat a shekel-rounded result as a legitimate alternative, not a defect.
