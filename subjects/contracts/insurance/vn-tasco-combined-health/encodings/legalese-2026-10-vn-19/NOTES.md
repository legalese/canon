## 1. What is encoded and what is not

**The whole document is in scope and every provision has a row in section 2.**
Encoded: Article 1 (who may be insured), the operative definitions of Article 2, Article 3 (double insurance), Article 4 (termination and the three refunds), Article 5 (continuity on renewal), Article 6, Article 7 (scope), the Article 8 benefit table for both programmes, Article 9, Article 10 (the waiting-period table and the supplementary clause 10.2), all eighteen exclusions of Article 11, the deadlines of Article 12 (as date functions and as regulative rules), the document list of Article 13, the operative items of Articles 14 and 15, Article 16 (time limits) and Article 17 (forum and law).
The three layers are joined in `the outcome of` (in `tasco-vn19-ch2-cover.l4`): is the claim covered (person, contract, scope, territory, definitions and dates; then exclusions; then waiting periods), and if so how much is payable.
Both lists are encoded as data, one L4 row per source row, with the retrieval date (6 October 2026) and the source's own statement that the excluded list "được cập nhật thường xuyên" (is updated regularly). [in:tasco-excluded-facilities]

**What the document leaves to the contract or certificate is an input with no default**: the start and end dates, the term in months, the sum insured of each scope, the premium, the number of persons, whether it is a group contract, any waiting period the group contract agrees, and whether the Article 10.2 clause is stated on it.
**Findings of fact are inputs**: whether an illness is one of the Article 2.11 special diseases (the source says this is determined from the medical records, src:93), whether a cause is an accident in the sense of 2.10, each circumstance an exclusion turns on, the treatment costs as assessed under 2.24.

**Two tables the rules rely on are not in the file.** The rules cite the "Bảng tỷ lệ trả tiền bảo hiểm thương tật" (injury table) as "ban hành kèm theo Quy tắc bảo hiểm này" (issued with these rules; src:200, 208, 228-229) and, in the Article 8 table, a surgery table (src:459-462). The 19-page PDF ends with the rules' last article, the company name (src:824) and a back cover (src:830-843); neither table is in it, and no other source in `source/raw/` contains them. So the table percentage for an injury or a surgery is an input (`percentage in the injury table`, `percentage in the surgery table`), and where it is not supplied benefits II.2, II.3 and III.3 are declined by name (finding X2). Nothing here improvises them.

**No decision number, date of issue or approval appears anywhere in the rules.** The cover (src:1-6) shows the title, a hotline and a website; the last page of the rules (src:824) shows only "CÔNG TY TNHH BẢO HIỂM TASCO"; a search of the text for a decision ("quyết định") finds only Article 16.1's "quyết định bồi thường" (claims decision, src:807). The upload timestamp in the URL (3 February 2026) is a hosting fact, not the rules' date (finding X25). The two lists carry no date either.

**The vintage** is the three documents as published at their URLs on 6 October 2026. A later amendment, or a version of the lists updated after that day, is not known to this encoding.

**Not encoded:** the Law on Insurance Business 08/2022/QH15 (an aid, read only to record where it fills or overrides the rules: forks tagged LAW in section 3); the "cases provided by law" of Article 4.3; the injury and surgery tables (not in the sources).

## 4. Findings

The hostile reading, as a policyholder's lawyer and as the insurer's. A finding is a defect in the instrument as written; a fork (section 3) is an ambiguity this encoding resolved.
Evidence is an `#ASSERT` or `#TRACE` in the tests (file and the assertion's subject), or the words "reading only" where the L4 cannot show it.
The three most likely to matter to a policyholder are marked ★.

**★ X2. Two of the tables the benefits are paid by are not in the document.**
Source: src:200, 208, 228-229 (the injury table "ban hành kèm theo Quy tắc bảo hiểm này"), src:405-420 (II.2, II.3), src:451-464 (III.3, the surgery table).
Scenario: a partial permanent disability, a temporary injury (standard programme), or any surgery. The amount is "the percentage of the sum insured in the table", and the published 19 pages contain no table. A policyholder reading the rules cannot learn what any of these benefits is worth; the insurer's table, published elsewhere or not at all, decides.
Evidence: `tasco-vn19-tests-cover.l4`, `#ASSERT REFUSED` on II.2 with no percentage and on III.3 with no percentage; with a percentage supplied the arithmetic is tested (II.2 at 30%: 6,000,000; III.3 at 10%: 2,000,000).

**★ X18. The notice period can run past the one-year bar.**
Source: src:649-651 (notice within 120 days of discharge, the end of treatment, or death), src:653-655 (documents within one year of the insured event, after which "mọi yêu cầu giải quyết quyền lợi không có giá trị", every claim has no validity).
Scenario: an illness begins on 10 January 2026 and the insured dies on 20 November 2026. Notice is due by 20 March 2027; the claim is barred after 10 January 2027. A beneficiary who uses the notice period the rules give loses the claim. This happens whenever death, discharge or the end of treatment comes more than 245 days after the event.
Evidence: `tasco-vn19-tests-claims.l4`: `the notice period of 12.1 ends after the bar of 12.2` is TRUE for that scenario and at day 246, FALSE at day 245; `Article 12.2, the claim has no validity` for documents sent on 1 February 2027.

**★ X22. Several "special diseases" that the rules cover from the second year are excluded outright.**
Source: src:84-92 (2.11 lists "lao các loại", "sốt rét", "lọc máu", "tuỷ xương", "nhược tủy", "các bệnh liên quan đến hệ thống tạo máu"), src:630-632 (11.17: special diseases covered from the second year), src:600 (11.9 excludes "Các bệnh suy tủy, bạch cầu, chạy thận nhân tạo; sốt rét, lao").

## 7. The quotation check

`tools/vnsrc.py` is this row's copy, extended as the lead allowed for a row with several source files (enc-vn-10 did the same). Exactly how it differs from the copy the lead deposited (sha256 `0fd1d84b236d16d10b8961358e54f21a4a9f03921cc5a01d7cd9a27c4c238fb6`):

- `quote --qualified RAW N [M]` prints `-- src:ID:N | …`, ID being RAW's basename less `.txt`. Plain `quote` is unchanged.
- `check` reads every `*.txt` beside RAW as a source with an ID. Three new forms, each reached only through a marker on the line: `-- src:ID:N[-M] | text` is checked as a slice of that source's lines; a line carrying the marker `in:ID` in square brackets has its Vietnamese runs checked against RAW and that source; a line carrying the marker `cell:ID:N-M` in square brackets has each run checked as consecutive pieces, each verbatim within one of lines N to M of that source, in reading order (for table cells the `-layout` rendering wraps and interleaves). A line with none of these is checked exactly as before.
- The summary line adds counts in brackets and still ends `N problems`.
- Tested on planted lines: a misquoted qualified line, an unknown ID, an unmarked list phrase, a word order swapped inside a wrapped cell, and an invented phrase all fail; known limit: a wrapped-cell word may be found in a neighbouring cell of the same lines.
