## Consequence for this subject

Reproduction is permitted on the conditions above, but the terms do not expressly grant a right to ADAPT. An L4 encoding that quotes the statute is arguably an adaptation. **Confirm before depositing text or quoting it in an encoding** -- a human decision recorded here.

## Language

English is not the only authoritative language. Legislation Act, 2006 (S.O. 2006, c. 21, Sched. F), s. 65, checked on e-Laws: 'The English and French versions of Acts and regulations that are enacted or made in both languages are equally authoritative.' Ontario public Acts are enacted in both languages, including the R.S.O. 1990 revision and the Acts listed here, so the French text (Lois de l'Ontario / L.O.) has equal force. Encodings should treat English as one of two equally authoritative texts. e-Laws publishes both at /laws/loi/<id>.
