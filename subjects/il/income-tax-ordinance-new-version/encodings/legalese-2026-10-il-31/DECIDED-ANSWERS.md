## 0. Reading of the law (the rules every case below rests on)

### 0.1 Current s 66(c) text (consolidation lines 2455-2478), the law from tax year 2024

"Year of birth" is the tax year in which the child was born, "year of majority" is the tax year in which the child turns 18 (s 40(b)(3) definitions, consolidation line 1648-1649).
"The year in which the child turned N" means the year of birth plus N.
Let d = tax year minus birth year.

Mother, separate computation, s 66(c)(4)(a), against her personal-effort income:

| d | points |
|---|---|
| 0 | 2.5 |
| 1 and 2 | 4.5 |
| 3 | 3.5 |
| 4 and 5 | 2.5 |
| 6 to 17 | 2 |
| 18 | 0.5 |
| 19 and over, or d < 0 | 0 |

Father, s 66(c)(5), against his personal-effort income:

| d | points |
|---|---|
| 0 | 2.5 |
| 1 and 2 | 4.5 |
| 3 | 3.5 |
| 4 and 5 | 2.5 |
| 6 to 17 | 1 |
| 18 and over, or d < 0 | 0 |

Hebrew for the mother: "2½ נקודות זיכוי בעד כל אחד מילדיה בשנת לידתו, ו־½ נקודת זיכוי בעד כל אחד מילדיה בשנת בגרותו, 4½ נקודות זיכוי בעד כל אחד מילדיה החל בשנת המס שלאחר שנת לידתו ועד לשנת המס שבה מלאו לו שנתיים, 3½ ... בשנת המס שבה מלאו לו שלוש שנים, 2½ ... בשנות המס שבהן מלאו לו ארבע שנים וחמש שנים, ושתי נקודות זיכוי ... החל בשנת המס שבה מלאו לו שש שנים ועד לשנת המס שקדמה לשנת בגרותו" (SH 3184 p. 630 s 1(2)(א), joined to SH 3048 p. 400 s 2(2)(א)(1) for the last limb).
Hebrew for the father: (5)(א) 2½ in the year of birth; (ב) 4½ from the year after birth to the year the child turned two, 3½ in the year the child turned three, 2½ in the years the child turned four and five (SH 3184 p. 630 s 1(2)(ב)); (ג) one point from the year the child turned six to the year before the year of majority (SH 3048 p. 400 s 2(2)(ב)(3)).
The father has no majority-year point: (5) lists none, and (ג) stops "עד לשנת המס שקדמה לשנת בגרותו".
Mother's choice, (4)(א1): the mother may choose whether one of the points she is entitled to in the year of birth is brought into account in the year of birth or in the year after.
The father has no such choice (the paragraph says "אמו של ילד").

### 0.2 What the Acts do, and from when

SH 2972 (Law 5782-2022, 18 May 2022, ITO amendment 260), s 1: for income produced in the period 28 Tevet 5782 (1 January 2022) to 7 Tevet 5783 (31 December 2022) it inserts one more credit point per child who "בשנת 2022 טרם מלאו לו שלוש עשרה שנים והוא אינו פעוט כהגדרתו בסעיף 40(ב)(3)": s 40(c)(1) (single-parent parent), s 40(c)(2) (single-parent father), s 66(c)(4)(ד) (mother), s 66(c)(5א) (father), and in (6) the cross-reference.
Commencement is s 4: 28 Tevet 5782 (1 January 2022).
The consolidation marks (4)(ד) and (5א) "(פקעה)": the point lapsed after 2022.
SH 3048 (Law 5783-2023, 7 June 2023, amendment 267) s 2: removes the toddler (פעוט) restriction from s 40(b) and s 66(c), deletes the definition "פעוט", adds one point per child from the year of age six to the year before majority (s 40(b)(1א)(ג), s 66(c)(5)(ג)) and 2½ points per child from the year after birth to age five and two points from age six for the mother (s 66(c)(4)(א)).
Commencement 20 Tevet 5784 (1 January 2024), "ובלבד שעד אותו מועד התקבל חוק התקציב לשנת 2024" (SH 3048 p. 401 s 3(א)); s 2 applies to income produced from that day (s 3(ד)); the work-grant sections 1(1),(3)(א) commence 1 January 2025.
SH 3184 (Law 5784-2024, 20 March 2024, amendment 271) s 1 replaces the birth-year 1½ by 2½ and sets the 4½ / 3½ / 2½ ladder for the years of age 1-5 for ss 40(b) and 66(c)(4),(5); s 3: "תחילתם של סעיפים 40 ו־66 לפקודה, כנוסחם בסעיף 1 לחוק זה, ביום כ' בטבת התשפ"ד (1 בינואר 2024)" (SH 3184 p. 634).
SH 3342 (Law 5785-2024, 26 December 2024, amendment 276) s 1(2)(ג) adds s 121B(a1); s 3(א) commencement 1 Tevet 5785 (1 January 2025); s 3(ב) "סעיף 121ב(א1) לפקודה, כנוסחו בחוק זה, יחול על הכנסה שהופקה או התקבלה ביום התחילה ואילך" (SH 3342 p. 152); s 1(1) adds s 120B(ה): no indexation on 1 January 2025 to 2027, amounts as on 1 January 2024 after rounding; 1 January 2028 indexation from the 1 January 2024 unrounded amounts using the 2027 index.

### 0.3 What the deposited material cannot answer

The text of s 66(c)(4),(5) and s 40(b) as in force before SH 3048 and SH 3184 (tax years 2021, 2022, 2023) is not deposited: SH 2972 and SH 3048 only say what they change.
So the credit points per child by age for 2021, 2022 and 2023 are a refusal: "needs the pre-2024 text of s 66(c)(4),(5) and s 40(b), and the definition of פעוט in s 40(b)(3), none of which is deposited".
What the sources do decide for 2022 is the increment of one point (section 0.2), and for 2021 and 2023 that there is no such increment.

### 0.4 s 121B(a1) (consolidation line 4457, SH 3342 p. 150 s 1(2)(ג))

"נוסף על האמור בסעיף קטן (א), יחיד אשר הכנסתו החייבת ממקורות הוניים בשנת המס עלתה על הסכום הקבוע באותו סעיף קטן, יהיה חייב במס נוסף על חלק הכנסתו החייבת ממקורות הוניים העולה על הסכום האמור, בשיעור של 2%".
Rule: additional tax (a1) = 2% x max(0, capital-source taxable income − T), T = the amount fixed in (a) for the year.
T for 2025 = 721,560 (consolidation note "בשנים 2024–2027, 721,560 ש״ח", confirmed by s 120B(ה)(1) which freezes 2025-2027 at the 1 January 2024 amount; the number itself is printed only in the consolidation note and the ITA booklet cited in SOURCES.json, not in an Act).
(a1) is in addition to (a) (3% on total taxable income above T), which the caller supplies.
(a1) applies to income produced or received from 1 January 2025: not to tax years 2024 and earlier.
The threshold is tested on capital income alone, not on total income, and the 2% base is the capital income above T, counted from T, not the part of the capital income that sits above T in the stack.
"הכנסה חייבת ממקור הוני" (s 121B(ה), SH 3342 p. 151) is taxable income except (1) income under s 2(1) or (2) (business, employment) and (2) personal-effort income that is not (1).
Rental of residential property, interest, dividends and capital gains are capital-source income unless excluded; pensions are personal-effort income and are excluded.
Under s 121B(ב) the s 91(ד) advance-payment rules do not apply to the income taxed under (a) or (a1).
Rounding: shekels and agorot, no rounding of the result is stated in the sources; I give exact arithmetic to the agora.

## 2. Flags (genuine ambiguities, with the reading taken)

1. Age year of the SH 2972 point (B06, B07): "turned 13 in 2022" excluded (reading above); the alternative adds the 2009 cohort.
2. Toddler boundary (B12 to B15): the definition is not deposited; I refuse for cohorts 2015 to 2022 in 2022 except that I decide 2014 and older are not toddlers, with L confidence for 2014.
3. Mother's birth-year deferral (A30): one point moves; alternative gives 2.5 and adds one in 2026 (rejected).
4. Capital income allocation when deductions and credits reduce it (not stated): the caller supplies the capital part; the encoding should not guess an allocation.
5. T = 721,560 is a consolidation note, not statute text (the Act freezes the 2024 level; the 2024 level is printed only in the note).
6. Consolidation typo noted: the definition "הכנסה חייבת" prints "המדד שפורסם ביום המדד שפורסם ביום ז׳ בשבט התשפ״ז"; the Act (SH 3342 p. 151) has the single words "המדד שפורסם ביום ז׳ בשבט התשפ״ז".
